## Ground rules for every option
- **She never fights at a campaign stop.**
  - She is CR 9: 5,000 XP, or 5,900 in her lair.
  - Once a day, *Truth or Die* casts *Disintegrate* at DC 15, averaging 75 force damage. That is one save to kill a level 5–8 character.
  - FP already handles the Mad Mage and the Abbot this way ("Don't fight him").
- **Barovia stays closed.**
  - In CoS the fog turns everyone back (Ch 2, p. 23). In RHW Strahd controls the border, and a Mist talisman can't get past a closed one (pp. 7, 40).
  - No option offers a way home before Strahd falls. His offer, "Walk her to my gate, and the mists will open", stays the only one (Prep IV, Scene 2).
- **No existing NPC is replaced or moved aside.** That includes our homebrew NPCs (Miranda, Viktor Ivanovich, Ethradir's mother, the brides' histories).
  - Don't make her "grandmotherly stranger" one of our hags. That would rewrite Miranda's and the contract's storyline.
  - Don't place her at Anastrasya's Vallaki parties. Anastrasya is already our "great hostess" (Brides p. 2).
  - Don't cast anyone as her family.
- **No settled item is touched.** C-01…C-12 and D-1…D-7 stay as they are. Eva's message wording stays as written. FP's "Eva becomes their ally" (C-01/C-02) is a separate fix.
- **Tarokka check (still to do).** FP doesn't say which cards our reading drew.
  - In the 2016 reading the Charlatan points to Old Bonegrinder (CoS Ch 1, p. 14). We put no item there, so it probably wasn't drawn.
  - Eva's last card, where Strahd waits, is still unrevealed (FP, "Madam Eva's messages").

## Option A — The Charlatan card (lore only)
- **Where/when:**
  - **Wizard of Wines (6–7).** Eva's message after the Tome comes by one of her own delivery methods: "a Tarokka card that wasn't there before, tucked into someone's pack". Make that card the **Charlatan** (liars).
  - **Van Richten's Tower (7–8).** Shown the card, Ezmerelda names it as the Vistani card for Port-a-Lucine, a city where a dead duchess holds balls and kills the guests who bore her.
- **What she wants:** nothing in play. She stays off stage.
- **Party gets:** a sly comment on the Tome, which is Strahd's own version of his story. They also get a hint that other prisons lie beyond the Mists, and that a card can lead there once the borders open (RHW p. 37). There is no mechanical benefit.
- **Touches:** Eva's message, whose text is unchanged; only the delivery card is chosen. Ezmerelda gets one optional line; her wants and role are unchanged.
- **Edits:**
  - FP › 2. Locations › *Madam Eva's messages* (a note on the "The Tome of Strahd" row).
  - FP › *Van Richten's Tower* › *Who's there* (Ezmerelda's line).
  - Nothing in Prep IV, I&T, Brides, the Strahd sheet or NPC cards.
- **Difficulty:** unchanged.
- **Risks:**
  - The payoff is close to nothing inside this campaign.
  - "Liars" may make players suspect an ally (Rictavio, Ireena's log lie). That could be fun or a distraction.
  - Players who know the book may go back to the mill.

## Option B — The brooch and a second invitation
- **Where/when:** **Vallaki**, then the **day of the castle dinner (6)**.
  - Card 27 reaches Ireena as written: Lady Wachter's thanks, or stolen.
  - At the first dawn after she first wears it (usually the morning Strahd's carriage is due), a plain mask and an invitation to the next Grand Masquerade lie by her bed. This is Saidra's *Invitation* power (RHW p. 74).
- **What she wants:** the brooch's wearer at her ball, to admire or unmask. Stay-aways "rarely survive for long" (p. 71), but she can't reach Ireena while Strahd keeps the border shut.
- **Party gets:** dread, and an irony: the brooch that "hides Ireena at last" (Prep IV) gets her noticed by someone else. No mechanical benefit. After Strahd falls it's a sequel hook (a jewelled mask is a Dementlieu talisman, p. 70).
- **Touches:** card 27 keeps its owner, source and printed text. Lady Wachter, Anastrasya and the dinner are unchanged.
- **Edits:**
  - I&T › Tier 1 › *Vallaki — things handed over*, row 27 (a DM note).
  - FP › 1 › *Major events along the way* (one row).
  - Optional: FP › *Castle Ravenloft — Dinner with Strahd* › *The dinner* (one line if they ask Strahd about the mask).
- **Difficulty:** unchanged. No fight.
- **Risks:**
  - It needs glue: she must know Ireena's name or have seen her. Leave it unexplained (the Dark Powers, RHW p. 5) or add one line of brooch backstory.
  - Players may read it as a way home; say plainly that it's inert.
  - It lands on the dinner day and may pull focus.

## Option C — A second guest at Strahd's table
- **Where/when:** **Dinner at Castle Ravenloft (6).** After the meat course she drifts in, masked and in costume, with no red so nobody mistakes her for Ludmilla. She leaves before he asks Ireena to dance.
- **What she wants:** to be received as an equal by the First Darklord (RHW p. 46), and to catch an impostor.
  - She asks one guest "Who are you?" and cries "Impostor!" at a lie. Strahd stops her: "My guests, Duchess. I gave my word."
  - If she picks Cassian, she reveals nothing beyond Strahd's own "Viktor" on the road. The Viktor thread is untouched.
- **Party gets:** a glimpse of the wider Mists, and proof his word holds even against another Darklord.
- **Touches:** nothing is replaced. The offer, the chair, the dance, Rahadin and the brides' lines stay.
- **Edits:**
  - FP › *Castle Ravenloft — Dinner with Strahd*: *The castle* (the table is "set for one more"; add a place), *The dinner* (her beat), *The rules*, *Possible results* (one row).
  - Brides › p. 5 *Where they appear*, row "Dinner at the castle" (optional).
  - The Strahd sheet.
  - A new NPC card, plus a DM index line.
- **Difficulty:** no fight; attacking her is FP's "a demonstration, not a fight". Her 5,000 XP sits between Moderate (4,225) and High (6,000) at level 6. Keep her out of the after-dinner hunt.
- **Risks:**
  - The biggest stretch of the book: a domain exists to hold its Darklord (RHW p. 8).
  - It crowds the busiest social scene and shares Strahd's spotlight.
  - Seeing the Mists open for her may make handing Ireena over tempting.

## Option D — One night in Port-a-Lucine (Mists side trip)
- **Where/when:** a side trip after **Van Richten's Tower (8)**, slotted like *5b Mount Baratok*.
  - Luvash's Vistani give the "safe passage" their faction offers (Prep IV › *The factions*), and Ezmerelda draws the Charlatan in the Mists (RHW p. 37).

- **Party gets:** at Renown 6 or more, her compliment and noble contacts. Card 27 finds a use (armour disguised as a gown passes "Dressed to Kill"), and so does card 03. Whether card 26 beats her Lie Detector is a DM call. The book's 600 XP doesn't apply, because levels are milestones (FP › 4).
- **Touches:** no stop replaced. But Ireena leaves Barovia or waits behind ("travels with the party"), and Volenta's three nights, Bella's offer and Ethradir's Miranda dreams pause.
- **Edits:**

  - Brides › p. 5 *Depending on what the party does*.

## Recommendation — for the user to decide
**Option B.** It gives Saidra a real presence through her own signature power, with no new scene and no fight. It changes no existing line, owner or difficulty, and it builds on card 27, which the campaign already has. It is easy to undo, and it leaves A as optional colour and C/D as sequel material after Strahd. If you'd rather keep her entirely off stage, choose A.
