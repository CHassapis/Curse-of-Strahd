## Summary
Counts: **BOOK FACT 11 · CURRENT CAMPAIGN VERSION 8 · CONFLICT 4 · DECISION REQUIRED 1 · CONFIRMED FIX 1 · OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENT 9** (34 rows).

The findings that matter most:
1. **DECISION REQUIRED: the Gulthias staff (card 33) at Yester Hill.** In the book the staff sits at the winery (W16). If it is broken or burned, every blight that hears it within 300 ft dies (App C p. 221). Our card moves it to the druid leader at Yester Hill, so breaking it there could kill Wintersplinter and the needle blights in one stroke. That would undercut "Wintersplinter awake: 7,000, High" (C-11). Our card text leaves this property out.
2. **CONFIRMED FIX (C-01/C-02): Madam Eva "becomes their ally".** The Route Map flag is now traced to three places in the real documents: FP §1 Major events ("All four items"), FP Madam Eva's Messages (the Icon row) and NPC card 23. The book's Artifact card makes Rictavio/van Richten the ally (Ch 1, p. 15). Keep Eva's messages and "where Strahd waits" (C-03).
3. **CONFLICT: Emil.** Our Emil is caged in the den (a deliberate move from Castle Ravenloft K75a) and "would stop the pack taking children". In the book Emil wanted *every* child kept and turned into a werewolf (p. 201). Freed, he betrays the party unless they claim Zuleika's friendship (Ch 4, p. 81). If he wins the duel, he keeps the children unless Zuleika talks him round (p. 205).
4. **CONFLICT: Yester Hill without Strahd.** In the book the druids will not start the ritual until Strahd arrives, and he watches, defends them and retreats if outmatched (pp. 198, 200). FP has the ritual under way with no Strahd. PROVISIONAL, because the Strahd DM sheet may cover it.
5. **CONFLICT: the Tome's spot.** Ours is "cellar, among the casks". The book's Tarokka spot at the winery is W10's barrel of sand, "wood and sand" (Three of Coins, Ch 1 p. 13; W10 p. 177). This matters if Eva's reading used that card's text.

Assigned checks a–g are answered below. The book supports every Tarokka placement in scope (the Sunsword at V7, the Tome at the winery). Nothing is lowered.

## Assigned extra checks
**a. Van Richten's Tower.**
- *The trap.* The lightning trap fits our version: touching the door before the dance deals 4d10 in a 10-minute sheath. A wrong dance brings a young blue dragon, and the third trigger drops the tower (V2 p. 169). Khazan's spell drain makes the tower an antimagic zone (p. 167).
- *The safe way in.* Ezmerelda knows it (p. 172). Van Richten lived in V7 for months (p. 171), so the book implies he knows it too.
- *Ezmerelda's wagon* (V1 p. 168). It holds an arsenal, kits, a Tarokka deck, two scrolls, a map, the journal page and household goods. It has summoned horses ("Drovash"/"Arvesh"), and the trapdoor underneath is the only safe entry. The booby trap deals 10d10 and destroys everything inside.
- *Their relationship* (App D pp. 230–231, 238). Her family's role in Erasmus's death was followed by his mercy to her parents. She tracked him down, and they hunted together for two years until he refused to trust a Vistana and they parted. She treats him as a father. He doesn't know she is in Barovia and keeps loved ones far away because of his curse.
- *The reunion.* The book has no reunion scene; ours is homebrew on the book's premise (row 10). Yan's head and the journal page give it teeth (row 11).
- *Van Richten as the Tarokka ally (C-01).* The Artifact card names him (p. 15). Told of the reading, he drops his disguise. As the chosen ally he gains Inspire, and Strahd targets him. He retreats to his tower if nearly unmasked (p. 238), and after the tiger escapes he names "an old tower to the west" himself (Ch 5 p. 124).

Khazan, the tower's builder, was ordered to destroy the Sunsword (App C p. 223). Ezmerelda learned of the tower in Vallaki (App D p. 231). **For D-7 (not resolved):** in the book the tower clue comes from Rictavio's own mouth as he flees (p. 124), not from where the wagon was parked. Prep IV already lists "later the Sunsword's tower" as Rictavio's offer.
