## Summary
**Counts (26 findings):** BOOK FACT 11 · CURRENT CAMPAIGN VERSION 8 · CONFLICT 1 · DECISION REQUIRED 2 · CONFIRMED FIX 1 · OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENT 3.

The findings that matter most:
1. **D-2 Hag Eye.** In the book, the coven's **only** hag eye hangs on **Cyrus Belview** in the castle (K62), and the pack defers to the Monster Manual for its rules. Our card 02 is at the mill and uses the **2024 DMG** text, which gives **no effect when the eye is destroyed**. The **2014 MM** gives each coven member 3d10 psychic damage and 24 hours of blindness. Both editions allow **one eye per coven**, and a new one needs all three hags. We keep Cyrus in the castle, so the pendant around his neck needs a decision (see extra check a).
2. **D-5 Patrina.** The campaign already keeps **Kasimir's wish "to bring back his dead sister"** (Future Possible, Amber Temple; NPC card 40). In the book that sister *is* Patrina (crypt 21). Our **Ludmilla** backstory (sought the Amber Temple, was found and handed over by Rahadin, keeps Strahd's books) also overlaps with the book's Patrina. Eleven touch points are listed in check (b).
3. **Where Strahd waits (C-03).** 8 of the 14 location cards point into this range (K60, K67, K85, K86, K88). The campaign keeps Eva's promise, but the same sentence still makes her an **ally** (C-01/C-02 CONFIRMED FIX). Which card was drawn isn't in the uploaded documents (**PROVISIONAL**). If it is **Donjon**, the pre-final **skull raid on K67 would trigger the final fight** (the book's first-arrival rule).
4. **The brides' tomb matches the book's place (K86).** Coffins, looks, stat blocks and jewellery (980 gp against the book's 7,250 gp) are deliberate campaign changes. The book's approach to K86 (a ring of teleport traps swapping intruders with 15 wights, a DC 25 portcullis with its lever inside, a back door through crypt 32, the yellow flame in K78) is useful concrete content for **Ludmilla's "where his coffin is"** (OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENT).
5. **Emil** is deliberately moved to a cage in the Werewolf Den, so the book's K75a prisoner and the "cry for help" in K73 are vacant in our castle.

## Assigned extra checks

**a. D-2 Hag Eye (Cyrus Belview, the pendant, what the hags see, and conflict or coexistence with card 02).** Not resolved.
- **Book, Cyrus** (Ch 4, K62, p. 76): a mongrelfolk (CR 1/4) with Keen Hearing and Smell, devoted to Strahd, crazed; he never attacks first. Around his neck is a **twine loop with the iron key to the K60 chest** (a jewelled crown, 2,500 gp) **and a wooden pendant set with a varnished human eye: the hag eye**. Morgantha gave it to him *so that she could spy on Strahd*. **Cyrus doesn't know it is magical.** If the key is taken he wails; DC 10 Intimidation makes him explain the key, the chest and its contents. The book says nothing about what the hags have learned through the eye, or whether Strahd knows.
- **Book, the coven** (Ch 6, p. 125): Morgantha gave the coven's eye to Cyrus to watch Castle Ravenloft and the vampire, and the hags fear Strahd. **"If one or more hags die, the coven is broken."**
- **Rules: the pack defers to the Monster Manual** ("Hag Covens" sidebar). MM (2014) pp. 176–177: a coven hag can use an action to see what the eye sees if it is on the same plane; the eye has darkvision 60 ft, AC 10 and 1 hp. **If destroyed, each coven member takes 3d10 psychic damage and is blinded for 24 hours.** A coven can have **only one** eye, and making a new one needs **all three** members in a 1-hour rite (not while blinded).
- **Rules, 2024** (DMG p. 265, which is what our card 02 prints): uncommon, 3 charges (*Darkvision* on self or *See Invisibility*); a coven hag can watch through it as a Magic action while concentrating, and several can watch at once; one per coven; creation needs all three. **No effect is stated for its destruction.**
- **Campaign:** card 02 is on the mill's top shelf, in a jar, set in an iron pendant (Prep IV Scene 1). Offalia sees through it and used it to find the party at the inn (Scene 5). It is how the hags watch the party (Future Possible §3), and "destroy it and the hags lose track of them" (Items and Treasure). Morgantha is dead, so by Ch 6 the coven is already broken. Future Possible, Berez: if Miranda is lost, "she's the new third sister. The coven is whole again."
- **What the user needs to decide:**
  1. The book's single coven eye is on **Cyrus in the castle**, watching **Strahd**. Ours is at **the mill**, watching **the party**, and we keep Cyrus at the castle (Future Possible, Castle, Who's there). Both editions forbid a coven two eyes at once. So either card 02 *is* the coven's eye and Cyrus's pendant goes, becomes mundane, or is absent; or Cyrus keeps it and card 02 is something else; or the hags took it back from Cyrus at some point. The documents don't say which.
  2. What smashing does: the 2024 card rules state nothing beyond losing the view. The 2014 MM adds 3d10 psychic and 24 h of blindness to each coven member. No text says whether that still applies to members of a **broken** coven (Bella and Offalia after Morgantha's death). That is a ruling.
  3. By either edition, Bella and Offalia **cannot make a replacement** eye unless the coven becomes whole again (the Miranda outcome at Berez).
  4. If the book's pendant stays on Cyrus, the chest key hangs on the same loop, so taking one means taking both.

**b. D-5 Patrina Velikovna (crypt, banshee, treasure, spellbook, Kasimir; the brides' tomb).** Not resolved.
