Findings that matter most:
1. **C-01 and C-02 confirmed from the card text.**
   - The book's Van Richten ally card is the **Artifact (Joker 1)**. Rictavio drops his disguise **as soon as he is told about the reading** (Ch 1 p. 15).
   - Madam Eva is on **none** of the 14 ally cards. Ch 2 (area G, p. 37) says she **never gives aid**.
   - Three campaign texts still make Eva the ally (row 1).
2. **The Icon of Ravenloft is not a Tarokka item in the book.** Its fixed place is the chapel altar in Castle Ravenloft (Ch 4, K15, p. 57), and **no card** places anything in Argynvostholt's chapel. The move is **deliberate**: three campaign documents give the in-world reason ("the knights carried it out of the castle"). It is flagged only so the user can confirm it (row 9).
3. **Gulthias Staff.** The book gives it to the druid at the winery (W16). Breaking or burning it kills **every blight that hears it**, which is how the book ends the winery siege. Our card 33 sits at Yester Hill and **leaves this property out**. At Yester Hill it would kill the 10 needle blights **and Wintersplinter** (rows 10–11).
4. **The Tome's card only allows the winery's glassblower's workshop (W10, in a barrel of sand)**, not the cellar among the casks. It is a small same-site difference (row 5).
5. **Nobody in the campaign reacts to the Tome.** By the book, once Strahd learns the party has it he **drops every other goal** and targets whoever carries it. Our Tome is found early (level 6–7), and Future Possible has no reaction to it (row 12).

Needs the user: rows 5, 9, 10, 11, 14 (CONFLICT) and rows 17–18 (the existing D-3 and D-5, with new book facts added; not resolved).

## Missing encounters / NPC relationships worth knowing
- **Rahadin:**
  - Honorary son of King Barov, so he calls Strahd "brother".
  - **He killed the dusk elf women and cut off Kasimir's ears.** Rahadin and Kasimir are natural enemies, and Patrina, if restored, tries to set the party on Rahadin (Ch 13 p. 196).
  - He prays at the Amber Temple statue (X5), which fits our Ludmilla.
  - Epilogue: **Rahadin's Revenge** after Strahd dies (p. 207).
  - NPC Card 22 mentions none of these.
- **Escher:** Strahd's favoured male spawn consort, sulking in the lounge. He fears being walled up in the catacombs with Strahd's discarded consorts, and wears a 150 gp rose ring and a 750 gp ruby pendant (Ch 4, K49, p. 70). He is absent from every uploaded campaign document; that may be deliberate (three brides).
- **Izek and Ireena:** Ireena fled into the woods during the wolf attack and wandered for days before Kolyan found her. Izek was "born without a soul" unlike her, a hint that hers is Tatyana's (App D p. 232).
- **Ezmerelda and Van Richten:**
  - Her family kidnapped Erasmus, and Van Richten spared them. He never fully trusted her.
  - The journal's massacre sits in tension with that mercy.
  - He would protect her from a distance and may steer adventurers into watching her (App D pp. 230, 238).
  - Once she has faced Strahd, **Strahd wants her dead** and may invite the party expecting her to come (Ch 11 p. 172).
- **Kasimir and Patrina:** see row 18. Strahd has lost interest in her and would make her a spawn if she came back (Ch 13 p. 196).
- **The hags:**
  - App D has no hag entry. Its "witches" are **Barovian witches**, Lysaga's coven of allies in Castle Ravenloft (K56), who helped her find the Keepers.
  - Lysaga's other book allies are the Yester Hill druids, and she wages war on the wereravens (App D p. 228).
  - Our Bella–Lysaga pact is homebrew and fits that pattern.
- **Madam Eva:** her real name is Katarina. She was a castle maid at Sergei and Tatyana's wedding and made a pact with Mother Night. The Dark Powers would accept her as darklord (App D p. 233). This is useful colour for her messages, especially the chapel and Icon one.
- **Mother Night links:** Eva's pact, Lysaga's gifts and new-moon blood baths, and the werewolves' shrine (Z7; the Myrmidon card).
- **Van Richten and his other ties:**
  - He studies the Keepers of the Feather and hopes to use them.
  - He targets evil Vistani: the tiger is meant for the camp, and Yan's head is in V7. Our Arrigal (Strahd's spy, NPC Card 19) is his natural target.
  - Strahd's spies hunt Van Richten (Ch 1 p. 10).
- **Pidlwick II and the Dilisnyas:** the castle's murderous clockwork jester. Leo Dilisnya's bones lie at Wachterhaus (N4o, the Traitor card). There is no campaign card for Pidlwick II (castle; reader 4).
- **Strahd's Minions table** (App D p. 239) for any appearance away from the card-5 room, and the Heart of Sorrow rules (50 hp, reset at dawn, re-link only inside the castle). Whether our Strahd sheet uses them is **PROVISIONAL** (sheet not uploaded).
