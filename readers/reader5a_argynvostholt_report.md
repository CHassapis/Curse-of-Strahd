**b. Icon of Ravenloft.**
- The book does **not** place the Icon by Tarokka. The Tarokka covers only the Tome, the Holy Symbol and the Sunsword (App C, p. 221). The Icon is fixed on the Castle Ravenloft chapel altar (Ch 4, K15, p. 57), with the ward against evil creatures that touch it.
- The book names **no Argynvostholt location for the Icon**.
- Where a *Tarokka treasure* can sit in Argynvostholt: Q36, in Vladimir's hands until the beacon is lit (One of Swords), and Q53, on the west windowsill (Four of Stars). The Q13 chapel has no Tarokka entry.
- Our chapel placement (Q13) is deliberate and consistent across FP, I&T, Prep IV and Eva's messages (#5–#7).

**c. The skull and lighting the beacon.**
- Book facts: the skull is in **K67, Hall of Bones** (Castle Ravenloft, 250 lb, p. 78). It must be **sealed in the Q16 mausoleum**; the dragon's spirit then becomes the light in Q53.
- Effects: +1 AC and +1 on saving throws for Strahd's foes in Barovia; **Vladimir and all revenants rest** and leave corpses; revenant random encounters stop; the light is seen across the valley, and Strahd sees its glow (pp. 135, 142).
- Only a Tarokka-ally Godfrey stays (p. 139).
- Against ours:
  - "Light the beacon — Argynvost's skull is in the castle" matches the book.
  - "In the beacon at the top of the tower" differs from the book's mausoleum (#8, CONFLICT).
  - "The knights rest; one rides with them" is a homebrew variant of the Godfrey exception (#10, #11).
  - No document says how the skull is fetched before the castle (#9, DECISION REQUIRED).

- Ours ("Only if they fight Vladimir: 9,700 XP, Moderate — and the revenants come back") matches in spirit. It uses a heavier line-up (Vladimir, 3 revenants, 2 phantom warriors) and is correctly Moderate against FP's budget. **Not lowered (C-11)** (#14–#16).
