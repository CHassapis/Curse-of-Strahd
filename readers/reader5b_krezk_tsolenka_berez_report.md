## Summary
**Counts:** BOOK FACT 6 · CURRENT CAMPAIGN VERSION 17 · CONFLICT 5 · DECISION REQUIRED 0 · CONFIRMED FIX 1 · OPTIONAL IMPROVEMENT 11 (40 findings).

Findings that matter most:
1. **CONFIRMED FIX (C-01/C-02)** — FP still says Madam Eva "becomes their ally" once all four items are found (FP §1 Major events; FP Madam Eva's messages, Icon row). The ally is Van Richten. Keep her messages and her promise to say where Strahd waits (C-03).
2. **Holy Symbol (card 39)** — the campaign has the Abbot keep it and hand it over for the dress. The book places it by Tarokka card at one of S4, S9, S13 or S23, and the Abbot never holds it.
   - This is an intentional change.
   - For the "or find it" route, use the book spot for the card actually drawn. Monk = the niche behind the golden sun disk in the Abbot's own hall; Missionary = the garden scarecrow with 7 wights; Philanthropist = the nursery crib.
   - The card drawn isn't in any uploaded document.
3. **Blessed Pool** — the campaign reworks "Something Blue" on purpose: Tatyana appears, and Sergei is kept as an ending option. Using the book's version at first sight would end the campaign's options, because Sergei takes Ireena away for good and Strahd's lightning destroys the pool's blessing. The campaign already avoids this. The book's reaction is ready for the ending scene (optional).
4. **Berez fight numbers**:
   - Our fight uses 3 scarecrows; the book has 7, and all of them come when the goat-pen skulls howl.
   - With seven, the fight is 8,600 XP: still Moderate at 9–10 on FP's own table. Using them is an option and respects C-11.
   - The book's own lure (damaging the goat-pen fence) supports FP's plan to trick Lysaga away from the hut.
5. **Low-stakes CONFLICTs to check intent:**
   - Dmitri pays 250 gp, but the book's Krezk has no money economy.
   - The hut walks on "a pair of huge, clawed legs"; the book's hut walks on tree-stump roots, and its stat block attacks with roots.
   - Lysaga flies off "to tell Strahd everything"; the book says she has never faced him, for fear of rejection.
   - The mongrelfolk are "stitched" on our NPC card; in the book they were changed by magic, and many were born that way.
   - The roc is a "guardian" met on the way up; in the book it hunts on the return crossing.
