Findings that matter most:
1. **D-5 is live at this stop.**
   - The campaign brings Kasimir to the temple with his book goal, bringing back his dead sister (FP §2; NPC 40), but says nothing about what follows.
   - In the book he takes **Zhudun's** gift (X42 east) and then needs an escort to Patrina's crypt (K84). Restored, she is a lying NE archmage who wants to be Strahd's bride and wants Rahadin dead (p. 196).
   - Zhudun is also the natural book anchor for **Fedra's ashes**, and it sits **directly below Ludmilla's library** (X30 → spiral stair → X42).
   - The campaign's fallback visitor, **Rahadin**, is the man who cut off Kasimir's ears (App D p. 237).
   - All facts are under check a; D-5 is not resolved here.
2. **The main fight is one flameskull short of the book (CONFLICT, #3).** In the book, the X17 flameskulls join the arcanaloth: 11,700 XP. We have 10,600. Under FP's own budget table both totals get the same labels (High at 10, Moderate at 11), so either choice is safe. Nothing is lowered.
3. **The library scene and the room below it** (#5, #7, #8).
   - Book X30 has gray stone walls, candelabras and no sarcophagus. The amber and the sarcophagi are one floor down in X42.
   - X42 also holds **6 of Strahd's vampire spawn (10,800 XP)**, which FP doesn't mention.
   - The library's books are blank without a command word or *true seeing*, which is a strong book detail for Ludmilla's scene.
4. **The four dark-gift threads (ashes, barlgura, Viktor, Ireena) contradict nothing in the book.** They are homebrew built on the temple's own logic. Watch-points:
   - The book's Vampyr and Tenebrous offer only to **evil** humanoids.
   - Nobody receives a gift **unwillingly**.
   - A non-evil acceptor makes a **DC 12 Charisma save** or turns evil.
   - FP's Mount Baratok option, "a vestige's gift", has **no book vestige behind it** (D-3).
5. **Maps:** the published "player versions" of both temple maps still show the secret rooms (including the phylactery room with its pedestal drawn in), the teleport **T** and the X40 treasure pile numbers. They need masking for the A4 set.

## Assigned extra checks

**a. D-5: Patrina and Kasimir (facts only; D-5 not resolved)**
- **Kasimir's goals** (App D pp. 232–233; Ch 5 N9a p. 121):
  - He wants to restore Patrina to flesh and end her torment, even at the cost of his own life.
  - He tells the party the temple *might* hold the key to breaking Strahd's pact. He doesn't know whether that's true; he says it to get their help.
  - He has spied on the temple for years and needs adventurers to survive it.
  - If a Tarokka treasure is with him, he gives it up only for a promise to go to the temple.
  - Stats: neutral mage; darkvision; Fey Ancestry; **ring of warmth**; spellbook with nine extra spells.
  - The book contradicts itself on whether he believes Patrina has repented (#27).
- **His dark gift:** **Zhudun, the Corpse Star** (X42 east, p. 196).
  - Touch the remains of a dead creature for *resurrection* however long ago it died; one use; the taker looks like a corpse.
  - He knows on touching it that this is the gift he seeks.
  - General rules: he must accept willingly, and as a neutral creature he makes a **DC 12 Charisma save** or turns evil (p. 191).
- **The special event:** "Kasimir's Dark Gift" (p. 196). Having taken the gift, he asks the party to escort him to Castle Ravenloft's catacombs, **K84 crypt 21**.
