# Castle Ravenloft, part 1 (Ch 4 introduction, random encounters, K1–K47) — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 4, pp. 49–68. Supporting cites: Intro p. 6; Ch 1 pp. 9–17; Ch 2 pp. 37–38; App C p. 222; App D pp. 225, 236–239; App F p. 251; Epilogue p. 207. Layer: [BOOK]. Draft.
K48–K88 (upper spires, larders, dungeon, catacombs, Strahd's tomb, the brides) → `castle-ravenloft-part2.md`.

## At a glance
- Strahd's seat, built on an older fortress's ruin and named for his mother Ravenovia, who is entombed below. The architect Artimus has a crypt there (p. 49). Book level: average 9th (Intro, p. 6).
- This part covers: walls and courtyards (K1–K6); the main floor with the invitation dining hall, the chapel and the Heart of Sorrow tower (K7–K24); the Court of the Count (K25–K34); the Rooms of Weeping with the study and treasury (K35–K46); the first spire landing (K47).
- The fifth Tarokka card fixes where Strahd waits; five of its fourteen outcomes are in this range: K6, K15, K25, K37, K41 (Ch 1, p. 17).
- Invited guests are escorted by Rahadin (K8) to a feast hosted by an illusory Strahd (K10). When the illusion vanishes, the castle seals itself (p. 56).
- Main dangers: guest-trapping dragon wyrmlings (K7) and gargoyles (K8), the Heart of Sorrow (K20), Strahd's animated armour (K46), the trapped route to the treasury (K37–K40), and random encounters.
- Tone: a gothic funhouse. Many scares are harmless (a flying mannequin, puppet armour, statues with moving eyes, a bloody-bath ghost, a dancing dress) and sit beside lethal ones.

## Areas
### Walls of Ravenloft (Map 2, K1–K6)
- **K1 Front Courtyard (p. 52).** The storm worsens on arrival. Walls 90 ft; the keep's doors stand open and torch-lit (→ K7). A broken window 50 ft up leads to K25. [DM] Gate towers: locked doors hold the drawbridge and portcullis gear. Each latch needs Strahd's word or *dispel magic* (DC 14), and both must be released.
- **K2 Center Court Gates (p. 54).** Rusted portcullises north and south of the keep. [DM] DC 15 Str lifts one; it drops unless propped. Only Strahd and Cyrus Belview know the command word.
- **K3 Servants' Courtyard (p. 54).** Door to K23 is stuck (DC 10 Str). **K4 Carriage House:** a sleek black carriage with glass windows and brass lanterns [PLAYER].
- **K5 Chapel Garden / K6 Overlook (p. 54).** A garden behind boarded stained glass; squealing gates open onto a gargoyle-walled overlook above Barovia village, 1,000 ft down [PLAYER]. [DM] Passive Perception 15+ spots the grimy windows of the K88 tomb 100 ft below (a 110-ft climb). A fall is 1,000 ft. Tarokka: Strahd gazes out from the balcony. The Epilogue's Sergei and Ireena scene happens here (p. 207).

### Main Floor (Map 3, K7–K24)
- **K7 Entry (p. 54).** The doors swing open to organ music; four dragon statues sit overhead [PLAYER]. [DM] They are **4 red dragon wyrmlings** that let guests in but not out. They attack anyone except Strahd entering from K8, turn back into weapon-proof stone once intruders leave, and never leave the room.
- **K8 Great Entry (p. 55).** A columned hall with eight stone gargoyles on the dome. Stairs lead to K19, bronze doors to K14, a hall to K9. **By invitation:** Rahadin comes down, says his master expects them, leads them to K10, shuts them in and withdraws to K72 via K21. He fights only if attacked. [DM] Once all the characters have left, the **8 gargoyles** attack anyone who returns; their wings snuff the torches.
- **K9 Guests' Hall (p. 56).** An ordinary, well-oiled suit of plate armour. The spiral stair (K21) goes down to K61 (the elevator trap) and up to K30; doors lead to K10.
- **K10 Dining Hall (p. 56).** [PLAYER] Chandeliers and a feast with a place and a goblet of amber wine for each guest; a caped figure plays a great organ between floor-to-ceiling mirrors. [DM] The figure is an **illusion of Strahd**: a gracious host who says they may explore and chats about family and history, but reveals nothing useful about the castle. It lasts at most 3 rounds, never leaves the bench, and vanishes laughing when time is up or it is attacked. **Then the castle seals**: wind kills every flame, doors slam in the distance, the portcullis drops, the drawbridge rises, this room's doors slam (unlocked), and the torches in K7–K9 are out. The food and wine are good. **Secret:** DC 20 Perception spots scratches; an organ pedal slides it out to reveal a door to K11, which can't be found before the organ moves.
- **K11 South Archers' Post (p. 57).** Arrow slits; 17 mirrors Strahd had taken down from around the castle. The organ side of the secret door can't be moved from here.
- **K12 Turret Posts / K13 Access Hall / K22 North Archers' Post (pp. 57, 59).** Domed octagons with arrow slits; a web-choked corridor.
- **K14 Hall of Faith (p. 57).** Knight statues whose eyes seem to follow (harmless); a bronze sun symbol over the doors to K15.
- **K15 Chapel (p. 57).** [PLAYER] A 90-ft dome with bats; boarded stained glass; benches in disarray; a balcony 50 ft up with two slumped figures in tall chairs (K28); a lit altar with a silver statuette; a cloaked corpse over the altar with a black mace below. [DM] The corpse is **Gustav Herrenghast**, an LE cleric who died going for the Icon. The statuette is the **Icon of Ravenloft**: an evil creature touching it while it's on the altar takes 88 (16d10) radiant, DC 17 Con for half. Tarokka: the treasure lies behind the altar; Strahd is among the bats or a dark shape at one end.
- **K16 / K17 Chapel Access (p. 58).** Harmless knight statues; they lead to K29 and K18.
- **K18 High Tower Staircase (p. 58).** Climbs 300 ft from K84 to K59 around shaft **K18a** (390 ft, webbed; the catacomb bats use it nightly). [DM] A new masonry wall 10 ft below the K17 landing has a chink that gas or a misty vampire can pass (DC 10 to spot); a crawl-hole takes 1 hour, demolition 2. A ½-inch crack near the foot looks into the wine cellar (K63).
- **K19 Grand Landing (p. 58).** Twin stairs up to K25; two alcoves of stained armour with dragon-headed maces [PLAYER]. [DM] Pressure plates (DC 15 to find, 40+ lb) make the armour flail: DC 14 Dex or 7 (2d6) bludgeoning; resets after 1 minute. Disarm with DC 15 Dex, or smash the armour (AC 18, 5 HP). A prank.
- **K20 Heart of Sorrow (p. 59).** A hollow tower with a railless spiral stair: landings at 50 ft (a secret door leads to a ladder down to K34), +40 ft (arches to K45/K46), +100 ft (under the heart), ending at K60. **K20a** goes down to K71. [DM] The tower is alive; see Events.
- **K21 South Tower Stair (p. 59).** K73, K61, K9, K30, K35, K47. Rahadin's route.
- **K23 Servants' Entrance (p. 59).** [PLAYER] A dusty desk with an ancient register and fresh ink; every page asks visitors to sign for their next of kin's sake, and all the names are illegible. Two chain-mailed skeletons with halberds flank the stair down to K62: harmless wired props by Cyrus. **K24 Servants' Quarters (p. 61):** wreckage; a stair up to K34.

### Court of the Count (Map 4, K25–K34)
- **K25 Audience Hall (p. 61).** A dark, cobwebbed hall behind the broken window; a throne on a marble dais faces south [PLAYER]. [DM] A secret door leads to K13 (DC 16). Tarokka: the treasure lies behind the throne; Strahd sits on it.
- **K26 Guards' Post (p. 61).** "Floating" guard skeletons on pegs (Cyrus's props). [DM] A secret door behind the north one leads to K33.
- **K27 King's Hall (p. 61).** The moaning is the wind. [DM] DC 20 spots the pulleys. **Flight of the Vampire:** at mid-hall a Strahd mannequin swoops down the rope with a magical chuckle; run it on initiative 5, gliding end to end at 10 ft; AC 15, 10 HP. A secret door leads to K31.
- **K28 King's Balcony (p. 62).** [DM] **2 Strahd zombies** on thrones facing the chapel attack when disturbed or approached. **K29 Creaky Landing:** sturdy but loud; climbers can't surprise K28.
- **K30 King's Accountant (p. 62).** Scrolls, four locked chests, and a chained clerk with a tasselled rope [PLAYER]. [DM] **Lief Lipsiege** pulls it if threatened: a gong, then after 1d6 rounds (d4) 1d6 shadows / 1d4 vampire spawn / 1d4 wights / a wraith and 1d4+1 specters. The key is hidden in a hollow book (10 minutes + DC 15 Investigation).
- **K31 Trapworks / K31a Shaft / K31b Shaft Access (pp. 63–64).** A lever raises (up) or resets (down) the K61 elevator; 1 minute disables the machinery. The 170-ft shaft runs from K61 (+90) past K31 (+40) and K31b (+40) to a trapdoor into K47; unclimbable without magic or a kit. Secret doors lead to K27 (from K31) and K39 (from K31b), obvious from inside. Diagram on p. 63.
- **K32 Maid in Hell (p. 64).** Eight canopied beds; a pale maid in a ruby necklace, dusting [PLAYER]. [DM] **Helga Ruvak**, a vampire spawn (see NPCs).
- **K33 King's Apartment Stair (p. 64).** [DM] Hidden between two secret doors; leads to K32 and up 40 ft to K45.
- **K34 Servants' Upper Floor (p. 64).** A coffin-shaped fey-painted wardrobe; opened, it releases a yellowed white dress that dances to the storm until touched (harmless). [DM] A secret door behind the west mirror opens on a closet with a 20-ft ladder to the K20 stair.

### Rooms of Weeping (Map 5, K35–K46)
- **K35 Guardian Vermin (p. 64).** A shining engraved steel door (a king on horseback, mountains, shooting stars, wolves) with dark figures in the alcoves [PLAYER]. [DM] **4 swarms of rats** under Strahd's control.
- **K36 Dining Hall of the Count (p. 65).** A four-century-old wedding cake gone green, a lone bride figurine on top, a lute, and a cobwebbed harp [PLAYER]. [DM] The groom figurine is on the floor (DC 10). If it leaves the room, on return the cake has burst and the window is smashed: either Strahd's theatre, or Strahd's hate made flesh (an **invisible stalker**) hunting its carrier. Playing the harp (DC 15 Performance) summons **Pidlwick's ghost** once, who promises a reward in his crypt (K84 crypt 9: a *deck of illusions*). The lute is a **Doss lute**.
- **K37 Study (p. 66).** A blazing hearth, fine books, armchairs; over the mantel, a portrait exactly like Ireena [PLAYER]. [DM] It is **Tatyana**, painted over 400 years ago; to Strahd the likeness proves Ireena has her soul. Lifting the poker opens a door in the fireplace's back wall (→ K38). While the fire burns, entering costs 1d10 fire and sets you alight (1d10 per turn until doused). The library of 1,000+ tomes is worth 80,000 gp but hard to move. The K78 red flame teleports here. Tarokka: the treasure is on the mantel; Strahd sits by the fire.
- **K38 False Treasury (p. 66).** [DM] A hidden room: a chest amid coins, two sconces (one empty), and a dead adventurer holding the missing torch. The unlocked chest releases gas: DC 18 Con or paralysed 4 hours. If all fail, the K56 witches drag them to K50 unharmed. The west door opens with the poker-linked catch; the east door (→ K39) only with the torch in its sconce, and it re-locks if the torch is removed. *Knock* bypasses both.
- **K39 Hall of Webs (p. 67).** A clear path through giant-spider webs to bronze doors (→ K40). [DM] The K38 door is one-way and locks unless propped; a web-hidden secret door leads to K31b (DC 15).
- **K40 Belfry (p. 67).** [DM] Pulling or climbing the bell rope drops **5 giant spiders**. A web-hidden secret door leads to K41.
- **K41 Treasury (p. 67).** [DM] An octagonal vault under a starry pitch dome. Inside stands an adamantine tower (20 ft × 30 ft) that is a **Daern's instant fortress**: only Strahd knows its word and only he opens its door or roof hatch; the walls are 3 in thick. Otherwise only a shrunken or gaseous creature fits through the 4-inch slits. Tarokka: the treasure lies on the ground-floor coins; Strahd is perched on top.
- **K42 King's Bedchamber (p. 68).** Candles, red drapes, a bed with a carved "Z", and a young woman in a nightgown on it [PLAYER]; the window hinges open to K46. [DM] **Gertruda** (see NPCs). A secret door by the bed leads through a hall to a K45 alcove; she doesn't know it's there.
- **K43 Bath Chamber (p. 68).** An iron tub of blood [PLAYER]. [DM] This is the illusory spirit of **Varushka**, a maid who killed herself rather than become a spawn. Disturbed, a harmless blood-soaked apparition scuttles into K44 and vanishes.
- **K44 Closet (p. 68).** 28 black capes and 16 sets of fine clothes.
- **K45 Hall of Heroes (p. 68).** Ten hero statues whose faces turn to terror when lightning flashes [PLAYER]. [DM] They hold the spirits of Strahd's ancestors: each answers one question briefly and vaguely, 20% chance wrong. Stairs lead to K33 and an arch to K20.
- **K46 Parapets (p. 68).** A walkway round the keep, linked to the outer walls; locked but breakable windows. [DM] After 5+ minutes here or on the walls, **Strahd's animated armor** (it patrols day and night) arrives. It can't be salvaged.

### Spires (Map 6)
- **K47 Portrait of Strahd (p. 68).** A draughty landing with an ornate rug, an ironbound door, a trapdoor, and a portrait of a pale man with following eyes [PLAYER]. [DM] The rug is a **rug of smothering** (it attacks non-undead). The trapdoor opens on the K31a shaft, or on the elevator roof if raised. The portrait (Strahd in life) is a bolted **guardian portrait** that attacks if it or the rug is harmed or moved.

## NPCs
- **Strahd** (App D; CR 15, 144 HP, AC 16). Here he appears as the K10 illusion, as a random encounter, or at his card location. He wants Ireena as his consort (he believes she has Tatyana's soul) and tests newcomers (Ch 1, p. 10). He and his minions never attack Ireena. Tactics (App D, p. 239): he strikes from advantage, withdraws when out-damaged, and charms low-Wisdom characters.
- **Rahadin** (App D, pp. 236–237; CR 10, 135 HP, AC 18). A dusk elf chamberlain for nearly 500 years, loyal only to Strahd. He escorts guests (K8) and has an office at K72. Anyone within 10 ft hears the screams of those he has killed. [DM] He resented Patrina and later punished the dusk elves for her death, cutting off Kasimir's ears. *Rahadin's Revenge*: he attacks the moment Strahd dies (Epilogue, p. 207).
- **Lief Lipsiege** (K30; CE commoner). A chained accountant, sore that Strahd hides treasure from him. Treated kindly, he reveals the card-reading location of the *Holy Symbol of Ravenkind* and draws a route that ignores the dangers. He has forgotten where the chest key is.
- **Helga Ruvak** (K32; vampire spawn). Really the bootmaker's daughter, but she chose Strahd. She begs rescue, joins if asked, strikes when she can isolate a victim or when Strahd orders.
- **Gertruda** (K42; NG commoner). Mad Mary's daughter (Ch 3 E3, p. 44). Charmed, naive, fairy-tale-minded, and not yet bitten; Strahd wants to bite her in front of the party.
- **Gustav Herrenghast** (K15, dead cleric); **Varushka** (K43, a spirit); **Pidlwick's ghost** (K36, Duchess Dorfniya's fool); **the ancestors** (K45); **Cyrus Belview** (K62, part 2: knows the K2 word, made the skeleton props).
- **Random:** Ezmerelda d'Avenir (App D, CR 8); a Barovian witch from K56 hunting her lost cat; Vistani thugs posing as escapees (they serve Strahd); a treacherous Vistana in a cocoon.

## Events and triggers
- **Invited arrival:** the black carriage from Ch 2 area I (p. 37), organ music at K7, Rahadin at K8, the illusion at K10, then the castle seals. From then on the K7 wyrmlings block the way out and the K8 gargoyles hit anyone coming back through.
- **Heart of Sorrow (K20, p. 59; App D, p. 239).** The first step on the stair reveals a 10-ft crystal heart glowing near the top; roll initiative (the heart's count is fixed). On its turn the tower shakes: anyone on the stair or walls makes DC 10 Dex or falls to the base (crawling or prone succeeds). The heart has AC 15 and 50 HP, and needs 10-ft reach to melee from the stair. Destroyed, it rains blood and the tower goes dark (1,500 XP). **Damage to Strahd goes to the heart first**; only the excess reaches him. The heart heals fully at dawn if it has at least 1 HP; *dispel magic* doesn't work on it. Strahd can drop or restore the link as a bonus action (restore only in the castle); an antimagic field suppresses it. The 10 halberds defend it, and 4 vampire spawn arrive 3 rounds after it is damaged.
- **Lair actions** (App D, p. 239; initiative 20): phase through walls and floors until the next count 20; open or close and magically lock doors and windows (DC 20 Str); summon a castle ghost (specter stats) for one attack; steal a creature's shadow (DC 17 Cha) to make a shadow under his command.
- **Strahd's location:** on the party's first arrival at the foretold place he is there, unless forced into his coffin (Ch 1, p. 17). His minions table applies only elsewhere (App D, p. 239).
- **Triggers:** K19 plates; K27 mannequin; K30 gong; K31 lever / K61 elevator; K36 figurine and harp; K37 fire and poker; K38 gas and torch; K40 bell; K46 after 5 minutes; K47 rug and portrait.
- **Endings** (Epilogue, p. 207): if Strahd prevails, the party is sealed in the catacombs and Ireena is turned and entombed. If Strahd dies, he mists to his coffin, Rahadin's Revenge follows, and the Sergei and Ireena farewell plays at K6.

## Player-facing [PLAYER]
- **Strahd's Invitation** (App F, p. 251), paraphrased: he brought them here, only he can release them, and he invites them to dine in civilised surroundings with safe passage. With it, no threatening random encounters on the way (Ch 5, p. 124; Ch 8, p. 156).
- Visible: the storm, the open lit doors, the organ music, the statues, a feast set for each of them, the host at the organ, then slamming doors and a rising drawbridge. Also the chapel's altar and statuette, the register asking for next of kin, the flying "vampire", the dancing dress, the rotten wedding cake, a portrait that is exactly Ireena, Strahd's portrait with watching eyes, the bloody bath, and the glowing heart.
