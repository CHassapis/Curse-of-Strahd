# Castle Ravenloft, part 1 (Ch 4 introduction, random encounters, K1–K47) — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 4, pp. 49–68. Supporting cites: Intro p. 6; Ch 1 pp. 9–17; Ch 2 pp. 37–38; App C p. 222; App D pp. 225, 236–239; App F p. 251; Epilogue p. 207. Layer: [BOOK]. Draft.
K48–K88 (spires above K47, larders, dungeon, catacombs, Strahd's tomb, the brides) → `castle-ravenloft-part2.md`.

## At a glance
- Strahd's seat, raised on an older fortress by his family's loyal artisans, wizards and workers; named for his mother Ravenovia, who lies below; the architect Artimus was rewarded with a crypt (p. 49). Book level: average 9th (Intro, p. 6).
- This part covers: outer walls and courtyards (K1–K6), main floor with the invitation dining hall, chapel and Heart of Sorrow tower (K7–K24), Court of the Count (K25–K34), Rooms of Weeping with the study and treasury (K35–K46), first spire landing (K47).
- The final showdown is fixed by the fifth Tarokka card; five of its fourteen outcomes fall in this range: K6, K15, K25, K37, K41 (Ch 1, p. 17).
- An invited party is met by Rahadin (K8) and shut into the dining hall (K10) with an illusory Strahd; when it vanishes the castle seals itself (p. 56).
- Main dangers: guest-trapping dragon wyrmlings (K7) and gargoyles (K8); the Heart of Sorrow (K20), which soaks Strahd's damage; Strahd's animated armour on the parapets (K46); the trapped route to the treasury (K37–K40); random encounters.
- Tone: gothic funhouse. Many scares are harmless (flying mannequin, puppet armour, statues with moving eyes, bloody-bath ghost, dancing dress) and sit beside lethal ones.

## Areas

### Walls of Ravenloft (Map 2, K1–K6)
- **K1 Front Courtyard (p. 52).** The weather worsens on entry: rain becomes a torrent within the hour, with lightning and thunder. Walls are 90 ft high, and the keep's main doors stand open and torch-lit (→ K7). [PLAYER] A shattered round window 50 ft up (→ K25) shows no light. **Gate towers**: ironbound locked doors, with drawbridge and portcullis machinery inside. [DM] Each tower's latch opens only with a word Strahd knows or *dispel magic* (DC 14), and both latches must be opened before anything moves.
- **K2 Center Court Gates (p. 54).** Two rusted portcullises block the walls linking the keep to the outer walls (north and south). [DM] Unlocked: DC 15 Str lifts one, and it drops again unless propped. Only Strahd and Cyrus Belview (K62) know the command word.
- **K3 Servants' Courtyard (p. 54).** Carriage house (K4). The swollen door to K23 needs DC 10 Str.
- **K4 Carriage House (p. 54).** [PLAYER] A sleek black carriage with glass windows and brass lanterns.
- **K5 Chapel Garden (p. 54).** A struggling garden behind the keep, under boarded stained glass. Rusty iron gates squeal open to K6.
- **K6 Overlook (p. 54).** A flagstone avenue between empty outbuildings leads to a gargoyle-lined wall. [PLAYER] Barovia village lies 1,000 ft below the fog. [DM] Passive Perception 15+ notices a stone structure 100 ft down with three grimy windows: the K88 tomb. Reaching it means a 110-ft descent plus 20 ft back under the platform (magic or a climber's kit). A fall is 1,000 ft. Tarokka: Strahd is looking out over the balcony. The Epilogue's Sergei-and-Ireena farewell happens here (p. 207).

### Main Floor (Map 3, K7–K24)
- **K7 Entry (p. 54).** [PLAYER] The inner doors swing open to organ music. Four dragon statues glare from the vaulted ceiling. [DM] They are four **red dragon wyrmlings**, told to let guests in but not out. They attack anyone except Strahd who enters from the K8 side. Characters coming in from K1 are not attacked. When intruders leave they return to their perches as stone, which weapons can't harm. They never leave the room.
- **K8 Great Entry (p. 55).** Columned, cobwebbed hall with eight stone gargoyles on the dome's rim. The stairs north go to K19, the bronze doors east to K14, and the lit hall south to K9. **By invitation:** Rahadin comes down the stairs, says his master expects them, leads them to K10, shuts them in and withdraws to K72 by way of K21. He fights only if attacked. [DM] **Development:** once every character has left K8, the **8 gargoyles** attack anyone who comes back, or anyone who attacks them first. Their wingbeats put out the torches.
- **K9 Guests' Hall (p. 56).** Torch-lit vault holding a well-oiled suit of ordinary plate armour. The spiral stair (K21) goes down to K61 (the elevator trap) and up to K30. Organ music comes from the double doors to K10.
- **K10 Dining Hall (p. 56).** [PLAYER] Three crystal chandeliers and marble walls. A long table under white satin holds a lavish feast, with a place set for each character and a crystal goblet of fragrant amber wine. A massive organ stands between floor-to-ceiling mirrors on the west wall, and a caped figure plays it. [DM] The figure is an **illusion of Strahd**. It plays the gracious host, tells the guests they may explore freely, and may talk about family or castle history. It gives no useful information on the castle's inhabitants, treasure or dangers, beyond saying that guests are rare. It lasts at most 3 rounds, never leaves the bench, and vanishes with a mocking laugh when time runs out or it is attacked. **Then the castle seals**: a wind snuffs every flame, doors slam one after another into the distance, the portcullis drops, the drawbridge rises, and this room's doors slam shut (they don't lock). The torches in K7–K9 are now out. The food and wine are genuinely good. **Secret:** DC 20 Perception spots scratch marks. One pedal slides the organ out about 2 ft, uncovering a secret door to K11, which can't be found until the organ moves.
- **K11 South Archers' Post (p. 57).** Arrow slits (2½ ft × 4 in). Seventeen framed mirrors of all sizes that Strahd had taken down from around the castle. [DM] The secret door to the organ's back can't be used while the organ is in place, and the organ can't be moved from this side.
- **K12 Turret Posts (p. 57).** Domed octagonal rooms with faded frescoes and arrow slits.
- **K13 Turret Post Access Hall (p. 57).** A web-choked east–west corridor.
- **K14 Hall of Faith (p. 57).** Life-size knight statues whose eyes seem to follow viewers (an optical illusion, harmless). A beaten-bronze sun symbol hangs over the doors to K15.
- **K15 Chapel (p. 57).** [PLAYER] A 90-ft dome with bats and boarded stained-glass windows. Dusty benches lie in disarray. A west balcony 50 ft up holds two slumped figures in tall chairs (K28). A shaft of light falls on a silver statuette on the altar, which is carved with angels and vines. A cloaked body lies across the altar with a black mace at its feet. [DM] The body is **Gustav Herrenghast**, an LE cleric who died trying to take the Icon. The statuette is the **Icon of Ravenloft**: any evil creature touching it while it sits on the altar takes 88 (16d10) radiant damage, halved on a DC 17 Con save. Once it is off the altar anyone can handle it. Tarokka: a treasure lies behind the altar. Strahd is among the bats, or a dark shape at one end of the hall.
- **K16 / K17 North and South Chapel Access (p. 58).** Harmless helmed-knight statues. K16 leads to the K29 stair; K17 to the K18 landing.
- **K18 High Tower Staircase (p. 58).** Spirals 300 ft from K84 up to K59 around shaft K18a. [DM] A new masonry wall blocks the stair 10 ft below the K17 landing. A chink in it lets gas or a gaseous vampire through (DC 10 Perception to spot). Crawling through takes 1 hour of work, demolishing it 2 hours. Lower down, 50 ft above the foot, a ½-inch crack opens into the wine cellar (K63). It is noticed automatically, and widening it would take days.
- **K18a High Tower Shaft (p. 58).** A 10-ft-wide shaft rising 390 ft from K84 to K59, webbed and windy. Climbing needs magic or a climber's kit. The catacomb bats fly up it each night to feed and come back the same way.
- **K19 Grand Landing (p. 58).** [PLAYER] A knight fresco. Two stairs climb to K25. Two alcoves hold stained armour gripping dragon-headed maces under scratched-out inscriptions. [DM] Hidden pressure plates (DC 15 Perception; trigger at 40+ lb) make the armour flail: DC 14 Dex or 7 (2d6) bludgeoning. They reset after 1 minute. Disarm with thieves' tools (DC 15 Dex) or destroy the armour (AC 18, 5 HP, immune to psychic and poison). It is a prank, not a killer.
- **K20 Heart of Sorrow (p. 59).** [PLAYER] A mosaic-floored hollow tower. A railless spiral stair climbs the wall, and a central stair (K20a) leads down to K71. The spiral's first landing is at 50 ft, with an arch and a secret door to a ladder down to K34. The next is 40 ft higher (arches to K45 and K46), then 100 ft more to a landing under the heart; the stair ends at K60. [DM] The tower is alive; see Events.
- **K20a Tower Hall Stair (p. 59).** Connects K20 and K71.
- **K21 South Tower Stair (p. 59).** A torch-lit spiral: K73, K61, K9, K30, K35, K47. Rahadin's route to K72.
- **K22 North Archers' Post (p. 59).** Arrow slits.
- **K23 Servants' Entrance (p. 59).** [PLAYER] A dusty room with a heavy table and a desk holding an ancient register with fresh ink. Every page asks visitors to sign for their own convenience and their next of kin's. It is over half full of illegible names. Two chain-mailed skeletons with halberds flank the stair down to K62. [DM] The skeletons are wired props made by Cyrus Belview and are harmless. The courtyard door is stuck (DC 10 Str). A broken door leads north to K24.
- **K24 Servants' Quarters (p. 61).** Broken furniture. A railless stair leads up to K34.

### Court of the Count (Map 4, K25–K34)
- **K25 Audience Hall (p. 61).** A vast dark hall behind the broken great window, cobweb-hung. A wooden throne on a marble dais faces south. Stairs lead down to K19, and doors east to K26 and K30. [DM] A secret door in the south wall leads to K13 (DC 16 Perception). Tarokka: a treasure lies on the dais behind the throne. Strahd sits on the throne.
- **K26 Guards' Post (p. 61).** A corridor between double doors. At each end a "floating" guard skeleton hangs on pegs: a wired prop by Cyrus, harmless. [DM] A secret door behind the north skeleton leads to K33.
- **K27 King's Hall (p. 61).** A vaulted hall where a moan rises and falls (only the wind). [DM] DC 20 Perception spots the pulleys and rope. **Flight of the Vampire:** at the hall's midpoint a Strahd mannequin bursts from a compartment and glides overhead to a magical chuckle. Run it as combat on initiative 5: it flies 10 ft up to the far end, returns on its next turn, and resets after 1 minute. It has AC 15, 10 HP and immunity to poison and psychic damage. Hitting it from the floor needs 10-ft reach. A narrow secret door on the south side leads to K31.
- **K28 King's Balcony (p. 62).** A railed balcony 50 ft above K15 with two dusty thrones facing away from the doors. [DM] Two **Strahd zombies** sit on them and attack when disturbed or approached. A stair leads down to K29.
- **K29 Creaky Landing (p. 62).** A loud but sturdy wooden stair from K16 to K28. Nobody climbing it can surprise K28's zombies.
- **K30 King's Accountant (p. 62).** [PLAYER] Scrolls and ledgers everywhere around four locked, iron-bound chests. A clerk on a tall stool writes on an endless scroll, and a tasselled rope hangs nearby. [DM] **Lief Lipsiege** (see NPCs) pulls the rope (an action) the moment he feels threatened, sounding a gong. 1d6 rounds later a d4 roll brings 1d6 shadows, 1d4 vampire spawn, 1d4 wights, or a wraith with 1d4+1 specters. The doors lead west to K25 and east to K21.
- **K31 Trapworks (p. 63).** A room of greased gears and a wall lever. Lever up raises the K61 elevator trap; lever down lowers and resets it. One minute's work disables the machinery until it is repaired. The secret door north to K27 is obvious from this side. A shaft diagram is on p. 63.
- **K31a Elevator Shaft (p. 63).** 170 ft tall: K61 at the bottom, K31 at +90 ft, K31b at +40 ft, and a stone trapdoor into K47 at +40 ft. The elevator car runs in the west half and a counterweight in the east. Slick walls and chains make it unclimbable without magic or a climber's kit.
- **K31b Shaft Access (p. 64).** A 10-ft ledge 130 ft above the shaft floor. The door north to K39 is obvious from this side.
- **K32 Maid in Hell (p. 64).** [PLAYER] A lamp-lit, oak-panelled room with eight canopied beds. A pale maid dusts and hums, wearing a gold necklace with a ruby pendant. [DM] **Helga Ruvak**, a vampire spawn (see NPCs).
- **K33 King's Apartment Stair (p. 64).** [DM] A swept hall hidden between two secret doors. A door leads to K32 (light leaks through it), and a stair climbs 40 ft to K45.
- **K34 Servants' Upper Floor (p. 64).** A coffin-shaped wardrobe painted with fey stands between cracked mirrors. Opening it releases a yellowed white dress that dances to the storm until someone touches it (harmless). [DM] A secret door behind the west mirror opens on a dusty closet with a 20-ft ladder up to a secret door in the K20 stair.

### Rooms of Weeping (Map 5, K35–K46)
- **K35 Guardian Vermin (p. 64).** [PLAYER] A gleaming engraved steel door showing an armoured king on horseback with mountains, shooting stars, people and wolves. Two dark man-shapes stand in the alcoves beside it. [DM] They are **4 swarms of rats** stacked into human shapes, under Strahd's control. They attack anyone passing.
- **K36 Dining Hall of the Count (p. 65).** [PLAYER] A dusty long table. A four-century-old wedding cake, gone green and draped in webs, has a lone lady figurine on top. Also a webbed iron chandelier, a lute on a stand and a cobwebbed harp. [DM] The groom figurine lies on the floor (DC 10 Perception). If it is carried out, on the party's return the cake has burst and the window is smashed outward. The DM chooses the explanation: Strahd's theatre, or Strahd's hate made flesh (an **invisible stalker**) hunting whoever carries the groom. The harp is 6½ ft tall and about 300 lb. Playing it well (DC 15 Performance) summons **Pidlwick's ghost** once. He promises a treasure in his crypt (→ K84 crypt 9: a *deck of illusions*, in part 2). If Pidlwick II is present, the ghost accuses the effigy of pushing him down the stairs. If attacked, the ghost fights back. The lute is a **Doss lute** (magic).
- **K37 Study (p. 66).** [PLAYER] A blazing hearth, well-kept books, a thick rug, a mirror-polished table, divans and two leather armchairs. Over the mantel hangs a portrait that is an exact likeness of Ireena. [DM] The portrait is **Tatyana**, commissioned over 400 years ago; Strahd takes the likeness as proof that she and Ireena share one soul. Lifting the poker opens a secret door in the back wall of the fireplace (→ K38). While the fire burns, anyone entering the fireplace takes 5 (1d10) fire damage and catches fire, taking 1d10 fire each turn until doused (cumulative). The library holds over 1,000 unique tomes worth 80,000 gp but is hard to transport (a d12 table gives subjects). Arrivals teleporting from K78 (red flame) appear in front of the portrait. Tarokka: a treasure lies on the mantelpiece. Strahd sits in an armchair staring into the fire.
- **K38 False Treasury (p. 66).** [DM] A smoke-filled room hidden between two secret doors. A fine clawed chest sits among scattered coins. Two sconces hang on the wall, one empty, and a dead adventurer in plate holds the matching torch. The chest (40 lb, unlocked) releases **sleeping gas**: DC 18 Con or paralysed for 4 hours. If every character fails, the K56 witches drag them to K50 unharmed, but they don't come if even one character resisted. The west door opens from inside with the catch linked to the poker. The east door opens only when the skeleton's torch goes in the empty sconce, and removing it closes and locks the door again. *Knock* bypasses both. A search reveals each door but not how to open it.
- **K39 Hall of Webs (p. 67).** A clear path runs through giant-spider webs (DMG web hazard) to bronze doors (→ K40). [DM] The west secret door (from K38) can't be opened from this side except by magic, and it locks behind anyone who doesn't prop it. Another secret door, hidden behind webs in the south wall, leads to K31b (DC 15 Perception once the webs are cleared).
- **K40 Belfry (p. 67).** Webs, and a rope hanging from a bell 50 ft up. [DM] Pulling or climbing the rope rings it, and **5 giant spiders** drop to attack; otherwise they attack only if attacked. A secret door behind the webs (west end of the north wall) leads to K41.
- **K41 Treasury (p. 67).** [DM] A dust-free octagonal vault under a black dome studded with glowing crystal "stars" set in dry pitch (dim light). Inside stands a 20-ft square, 30-ft adamantine tower with arrow slits: a **Daern's instant fortress**. Only Strahd knows its command word, and it can't change shape until it is emptied. Only he can open its two entrances, a sealed door on the north side and a roof trapdoor. The walls are 3 in thick. The only other way in is through the 4-in × 2-ft slits, for a creature that is shrunk or in gaseous form. Treasure is listed below. Tarokka: a treasure lies on the ground-floor coins. Strahd is perched on top of the tower.
- **K42 King's Bedchamber (p. 68).** [PLAYER] A sweet-smelling, candle-lit room with red drapes over a great west window (two panes hinge open onto K46). A silk-canopied bed has a carved "Z" headboard, and a young woman lies on it in a nightgown with one slipper fallen. [DM] **Gertruda** (see NPCs). A secret door beside the bed opens on a dusty hall to a secret door in a K45 alcove; Gertruda doesn't know it exists.
- **K43 Bath Chamber (p. 68).** [PLAYER] An ornate clawed iron tub full of blood between red satin curtains. [DM] The blood is not real: it is the spirit of **Varushka**, a maid who killed herself so that Strahd couldn't make her a spawn. Disturbing it releases a harmless blood-soaked apparition that scuttles across the ceiling into K44 and vanishes. It can't be harmed and doesn't attack.
- **K44 Closet (p. 68).** 28 black capes and 16 sets of fine clothes.
- **K45 Hall of Heroes (p. 68).** [PLAYER] The ceiling has fallen. Ten hero statues stand in alcoves, and their faces twist into terror whenever lightning flashes. [DM] They hold the spirits of Strahd's ancestors, grieving the end of the bloodline. Each answers one question put to it directly, briefly and vaguely, with a 20% chance of being wrong. The stairs lead down 40 ft to K33, and an arch east to a K20 landing.
- **K46 Parapets (p. 68).** A 10-ft walkway around the upper keep, with battlements running out to the outer walls. The windows into the keep are locked but break easily. [DM] Anyone who lingers 5+ minutes on the parapets or walls meets **Strahd's animated armor**, which patrols day and night and is usually heard before it is seen. It can't be salvaged.

### Spires (Map 6, K47)
- **K47 Portrait of Strahd (p. 68).** [PLAYER] A dark, draughty landing with an ornate square rug, an ironbound door west with a wooden trapdoor in front of it, and a portrait of a pale, handsome man whose eyes follow viewers. [DM] The rug is a **rug of smothering**; it attacks any creature except undead that crosses or disturbs it. The trapdoor (4 ft, iron ring) opens on the 170-ft K31a shaft, or on the roof hatch of the elevator car if it has been raised. The portrait shows Strahd before he became a vampire. It is a **guardian portrait**, bolted in place, which can't be removed without destroying it. It attacks if the rug or the portrait is attacked or moved.

## NPCs
- **Strahd von Zarovich** (App D, CR 15, 144 HP, AC 16). In this range he appears as the K10 illusion, as a random encounter (a surprise appearance) and at his Tarokka location. He wants Ireena as his consort (he believes she carries Tatyana's soul) and tests newcomers as possible successors or playthings (Ch 1, p. 10). Tactics (App D, p. 239): he strikes at the best moment and position, withdraws when out-damaged, and charms low-Wisdom characters. He and his minions never attack Ireena (Ch 1, p. 10).
- **Rahadin** (App D pp. 236–237, CR 10, 135 HP, AC 18). A dusk elf chamberlain who has served Strahd's family for nearly 500 years and cares only for Strahd. He greets invited guests at K8 and withdraws to K72. As a random encounter he summons the party to a random place. Anyone within 10 ft of him hears the screams of those he has killed. [DM] Patrina link: he mistrusted her, told her she was no longer wanted once Tatyana appeared, and later, on Strahd's orders, killed the dusk elf women and cut off Kasimir's ears (App D p. 237). *Rahadin's Revenge*: if Strahd dies while Rahadin lives, Rahadin appears moments later and attacks, beyond reason (Epilogue, p. 207).
- **Lief Lipsiege** (K30; CE male human commoner). An accountant chained to his desk for longer than he remembers. He keeps Strahd's books and resents not being told where all the treasure is. Treated kindly, he reveals where the *Holy Symbol of Ravenkind* is (per the card reading) and sketches an accurate but danger-blind route. He has forgotten where he hid the chest key.
- **Helga Ruvak** (K32; vampire spawn). Claims to be the village bootmaker's daughter, kidnapped, and begs to be saved. She really is that daughter, but she chose Strahd. She joins if asked and attacks only when she can do so without taking on the whole party, or when Strahd commands. She plays the damsel to the end.
- **Gertruda** (K42; NG female human commoner). Mad Mary's daughter (Ch 3, area E3, p. 44), kept indoors all her life, who ran off to the castle. She is charmed by Strahd and blind to danger, with a fairy-tale outlook, and makes the simplest choice every time. Strahd hasn't bitten her yet but means to, ideally while the party watches helpless.
- **Gustav Herrenghast** (K15, corpse). An LE cleric killed trying to take the Icon.
- **Varushka** (K43). A maid's harmless tormented spirit.
- **Pidlwick's ghost** (K36). The late fool of Duchess Dorfniya. Appears once and fights only if attacked.
- **Strahd's ancestors** (K45). Statue spirits, one question each.
- **Cyrus Belview** (lives in K62; part 2). Knows the K2 command word and built the wired skeleton props in K23 and K26.
- **Random-encounter NPCs:** Ezmerelda d'Avenir (App D, CR 8; see Encounters); a Barovian witch from K56 searching for her lost cat familiar; Vistani thugs who pose as escaped prisoners but serve Strahd; a Vistana bandit found in a cocoon, who helps until Strahd or other Vistani appear.

## Treasure
- **Random:** a Vistani thug's pouch of 2d8 gems (50 gp each); each wight carries a longsword with the Barovian crest and 2d20 ep stamped with Strahd's profile. Unseen servant (d6): tarnished silver platter (25 gp; a scone grants inspiration, later a crawling claw); silver goblet (50 gp; poisoned wine the first time, DC 15 Con, 44 (8d10) poison, half on save; later a *potion of healing*); gold candelabrum (150 gp); silk handkerchief (1 gp; later bloodied); crystal dinner bell (25 gp; the servant rings it and 1d4 vampire spawn arrive in 1d4+1 rounds); **Strahd's spellbook** holding all his prepared spells (on repeats, a 25 gp storybook).
- **K15:** **Icon of Ravenloft** (legendary artifact, App C p. 222; given to Strahd by the archpriest Ciril Romulich to consecrate the castle and chapel; creatures within 30 ft are protected by *protection from evil and good* against fiends and undead). Gustav's fur-lined black cloak with gold thread (250 gp), chain mail, **mace of terror** (magic).
- **K30:** iron key hidden in a hollowed book (10 minutes + DC 15 Investigation). Chests: 10,000 cp, 10,000 cp, 1,000 gp, and 500 pp over a **manual of bodily health** (magic). Worth about 6,200 gp plus the manual.
- **K32:** Helga's gold and ruby necklace, a gift from Strahd, nearly 500 years old (750 gp).
- **K36:** **Doss lute** (magic instrument of the bards). The groom figurine (a plot trigger, no value).
- **K37:** Strahd's library, over 1,000 unique tomes (80,000 gp, hard to move).
- **K38:** 50 gp, 100 sp and 2,000 cp around the gas-trapped chest (about 80 gp).
- **K41 (inside the fortress):** the ground floor holds 50,000 cp, 10,000 sp, 10,000 gp, 1,000 pp, 15 gems (100 gp each) and a **+2 shield** bearing the silver dragon of the Order of the Silver Dragon (it whispers warnings: +2 initiative while the bearer isn't incapacitated; taken from Argynvostholt, Ch 7 p. 139). The upper floor holds 10 pieces of jewellery (250 gp each) in a red velvet sack, an **alchemy jug**, a **helm of brilliance**, a **rod of the pact keeper, +1** and a coffer of four **potions of greater healing**. The **Daern's instant fortress** itself is magic, but only Strahd knows its word. Coins, gems and jewellery come to about 25,500 gp.
- **Mundane:** plate armour (K9); 17 mirrors (K11); capes and fine clothes (K44). The book gives no values.

## Events and triggers
- **Arrival:** the weather turns to storm at K1. With an invitation, the black carriage from Ch 2 area I (p. 37) delivers the party, the doors open to organ music (K7), Rahadin escorts them (K8), and they reach the K10 dinner with the illusion. When it vanishes, the castle seals: portcullis down, drawbridge up, torches out. From then on the K7 wyrmlings stop anyone leaving through the front, and the K8 gargoyles attack anyone who returns there.
- **Heart of Sorrow (K20, p. 59; App D p. 239).** When someone first steps on the spiral stair, a red glow reveals the whole tower and a 10-ft crystal heart pulsing near the top. Everyone rolls initiative; the heart's count never changes, though characters who return may reroll. On the heart's turn the tower shakes: anyone on the stairs or clinging to the walls makes a DC 10 Dex save or falls to the base (crawling or prone characters succeed automatically). The heart has AC 15 and 50 HP, and melee from the stairs needs 10-ft reach. At 0 HP it shatters into a rain of blood, the tower stops shaking and goes dark (1,500 XP). **All damage Strahd takes goes to the heart**, and only the excess reaches him once it breaks. It regains all its HP at dawn if it has at least 1 left. *Dispel magic* has no effect on it. Strahd can break the link as a bonus action and restore it as a bonus action, but only inside the castle. An antimagic field around Strahd or the heart suppresses the absorption. Ten animated halberds attack anyone who threatens the heart. Strahd senses any damage to it and sends 4 vampire spawn, which arrive in 3 rounds.
- **Strahd's lair actions** (App D, p. 239; in the castle, on initiative 20, one per round): pass through walls, floors and ceilings until the next count 20; open or close any doors and windows he can see, with closed ones magically locked (DC 20 Str to force); call the angry spirit of someone who died in the castle (specter stats) to make one attack and vanish; tear a creature's shadow loose (DC 17 Cha), creating a shadow that serves him, which only *greater restoration* or *remove curse* cures, and only after the shadow is destroyed.
- **Strahd's location:** the first time the party reaches the foretold place, Strahd is there unless he has been forced into his coffin (Ch 1, p. 17). His minions table applies only when he appears somewhere other than his tomb or card location (App D, p. 239).
- **Traps and triggers:** the K19 armour plates; the K27 flying mannequin; the K30 gong; the K31 lever and the K61 elevator; the K36 groom figurine and harp; the K37 fire and poker; the K38 gas and torch lock; the K40 bell; the K46 armour after 5 minutes; the K47 rug and portrait.
- **Strahd prevails** (Epilogue, p. 207): he seals the characters in the catacombs and turns Ireena into a vampire spawn in her crypt (part 2). **Strahd dies:** he mists to his coffin, Rahadin's Revenge follows, and the Sergei-and-Ireena scene plays at K6.

## Player-facing [PLAYER]
- **Strahd's Invitation** (App F, p. 251, a handout), in paraphrase: Strahd claims he brought them into his land and that only he can release them. He invites them to dine at the castle in civilised surroundings and promises safe passage. Characters heading to the castle meet no threatening random encounters on the way (Ch 5, p. 124; Ch 8, p. 156).
- Visible on arrival: the storm, the open torch-lit doors, the organ music, the dragon and gargoyle statues, the feast with a place set for each of them, the host at the organ, then the slamming doors and the drawbridge rising.
