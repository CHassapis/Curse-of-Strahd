# Argynvostholt — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 7, pp. 129–142 (with Ch 1 pp. 12–16, Ch 2 p. 31, Ch 4 K15/K41/K67, App C, D, F). Layer: [BOOK]. Draft.
Stats marked (5etools) were checked in the 5etools GitHub data mirror (github.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-src).

## At a glance
- The ruined mansion of **Argynvost**, a silver dragon who lived as the human **Lord Argynvost** and founded the **Order of the Silver Dragon**. He built it to guard the Amber Temple (p. 129).
- Strahd's army destroyed the order and killed the dragon here. The bones were carried off to Castle Ravenloft; **the skull hangs over the east doors of K67, Hall of Bones** (p. 129; Ch 4, K67, p. 78).
- **Vladimir Horngaard** came back as a revenant, and his hatred pulled other knights back too. Madam Eva once turned their march on Ravenloft back by telling them Strahd was already dead and imprisoned. Since then they kill Strahd's agents but **refuse to let Strahd be destroyed**, because they want him to suffer for ever (p. 129).
- **Core quest:** seal the skull in the mausoleum (Q16) → the beacon (Q53) lights → all revenants rest, and Strahd's foes gain +1 AC and +1 on saving throws in Barovia (pp. 129, 135, 142).
- Book level **7** (Intro, p. 6). Lighting the beacon is the book's example of a milestone worth a level (Intro, p. 6).
- Tarokka: treasure cards **One of Swords → Q36** and **Four of Stars → Q53**; ally card **Ghost A → Sir Godfrey, Q37** (Ch 1, pp. 12–13, 15–16).
- Location: a spur **west of Vallaki**, up a branch of the Old Svalich Road, above the Luna River valley (p. 130).

## Areas
**(unlit)** / **(lit)** = before / after the beacon is lit.

**Grounds**
- **Q1 Dragon Statue** (p. 130): a 10-ft silver dragon statue on a granite block. [DM] It holds a dead cold-breath trap (evocation aura).
- **Q2 Main Entrance** (p. 130): [DM] the first creature on the steps makes Q1 breathe a harmless 60-ft cone of cold (once per dawn). The doors are unlocked.
- **Q5 Stable** (p. 132): burned, nothing of value. **Q8 Iron Gate** (p. 133): chained; DC 20 Dex (thieves' tools) or DC 15 Str to smash.
- **Q15 Cemetery** (p. 134): iron fence, fog. [PLAYER] **(unlit)** On the first crossing, a man with thistledown hair watches from a tower window, then draws the curtain. [DM] He is a lure at the Q42 window. Five graves were dug open **from inside** (DC 10 Perception): the revenants' bodies. A turret stair leads to a door into Q13, barred on the inside.
- **Q16 Dragon's Mausoleum** (pp. 134–135): a marble door engraved *Argynvost* (DC 15 Str to open), four empty alcoves and a Draconic epitaph. [DM] **Sealing the skull here lights the beacon.**

**Ground floor (Map 7.1)**
- **Q3 Foyer** (p. 132): a tapestry and busts of Argynvost's human guises. [PLAYER] On the first pass, a harmless winged shadow and a hiss.
- **Q4 Ballroom** (p. 132): **9 giant spiders** attack anyone who comes close.
- **Q6 Den** (p. 132): a sarcophagus once used as a wine cabinet. [DM] **Secret door** (east wall) to Q11. **Living Fire (unlit):** a dragon made of fire rises in the hearth (AC 15, 1 hp, acts on initiative 10). It never attacks: it pleads for his fallen knights to be shown their lost light, then dies out. Destroy it and it explodes: DC 12 Dex, 4d10 fire, and the room catches fire.
- **Q7 Parlor, Q9 Servants' Quarters, Q10 Kitchen** (p. 133): nothing of value. In the kitchen, a bat bursts out of a rattling pot.
- **Q11 Wine Storage** (p. 133): empty Wizard of Wines barrels; **Savid** is hiding here. [DM] **Secret door** (west wall) to Q6.
- **Q12 Dining Hall** (p. 133): dragon furniture, a chandelier under *continual flame*, inert knight statues, glass doors into the chapel.
- **Q13 Chapel of Morning** (p. 134): a sunrise altar with a balcony above (Q24, 20 ft). [PLAYER] DC 10 Int (Religion) identifies the Morninglord. Looking up, you see the hollow beacon tower (Q50 at 60 ft, Q51 at 80 ft). [DM] **(unlit) 3 revenants** kneel at the altar and **attack on sight**. **(lit)** Three armoured corpses. **No Tarokka entry here.**
- **Q14 Chapel Staircases** (p. 134): spiral stairs to Q24 and a small room above.

**Second floor (Map 7.1)**
- **Q17 West Staircases** (p. 135): up to the third floor. **Q18 Balconies** (p. 135): **nonmagical** weapons and shields on the walls; the busts' eyes seem to follow you (illusion).
- **Q19 Ruined Bedchambers** (p. 135): walking on the creaking floor brings Q4's spiders up.
- **Q20 South Alcove** (p. 135): [DM] under a cloth, Strahd's illusion shows a **random PC's severed head**. It is really a bust of Argynvost; breaking or re-covering it ends the illusion.
- **Q21 North Alcove** (p. 135): the curtain closes itself again. **Q22 Bathroom** (p. 135): a Balinok Mountains mural. **Q23 Storage** (p. 135): [DM] the floor fails over 100 lb (20-ft fall to Q12).
- **Q24 Chapel Balcony** (p. 136): a carved throne and a chandelier with silver-dragon candle holders.
- **Q25 Trapped Hallway** (p. 136): the Q27/Q28 doors are locked (DC 20 Str). [DM] **Trap squares (T):** a *wall of stone* seals the hall for 10 minutes and the phantom warriors charge out. It resets afterwards. *Detect magic* shows evocation auras.
- **Q26 NE Guest Room** (p. 136): [DM] a smoke **dragonet** (smoke mephit, fights only in self-defence). Left alone, it flies to Vladimir's throne (Q36) by way of Q17, Q30 and Q33, which makes it a guide.
- **Q27 / Q28 Knights' Quarters** (p. 136): **4 / 3 phantom warriors**, who fight until destroyed. **Q28 treasure:** a coffer with **4 potions of invulnerability** (found by searching).
- **Q29 NW Guest Room** (p. 136): a portrait of Lord Argynvost, the thistledown-haired man from Q15.

[PLAYER] A **shield-shaped patch** above the fireplace. [DM] The magic shield is now in Castle Ravenloft's treasury, K41.

**Roof and beacon (Map 7.2)**

- **Q49 Beacon Tower Door** (p. 141): barred from inside. [DM] The Q52 archers shoot anyone forcing it, from cover.

- **Q53 Beacon** (p. 142): ravens and tall windows. [PLAYER] Views of Vallaki, Old Bonegrinder, the southern marsh, and the Abbey (Krezk). **Fortunes (Four of Stars):** a Tarokka treasure on the **west windowsill**.

## NPCs
- **Argynvost (spirit):** never a combatant. He reaches out through Q3, Q6, Q15, Q26, Q40 and Q44 to get his skull brought home and his knights redeemed.
- **Vladimir Horngaard** (Q36; App D, pp. 241–242): LE revenant, the order's field commander. Strahd killed his beloved, **Sir Godfrey**, in front of him.
  - *Wants:* Strahd tormented for ever. He kills Strahd's agents and **anyone who might ease Strahd's torment**. He believes Barovia is hell and accepts his own imprisonment as the price.
  - *Attitude:* tells visitors "go away", then warns them: destroy his body and he will return and hunt them; he will destroy anyone who tries to end Strahd's torment.
  - *Fight:* only in self-defence or if pressed for help destroying Strahd. When he is **first damaged, 6 phantom warriors** appear.
  - *Godfrey:* hate has buried the memory of his love for Godfrey. Seeing Godfrey stand against him stirs recognition, but **only the beacon frees him** (p. 138).
  - *Stats (5etools):* AC 17, 192 hp, *+2 greatsword* with a silver dragon hilt and black-opal pommel: 2 attacks at +9, 4d6+6, +4d6 against Strahd. CR 7 (2,900 XP). Regeneration 10 hp a turn (blocked by fire or radiant damage). Rejuvenation after 24 h. Immune to turning. His tracker and glare work against **Strahd only**.
  - *Ideal / bond / flaw:* vengeance is all he has; loyalty to the order; he will never allow Strahd's destruction.
  - *Fate:* rests if the beacon is lit, or if Strahd is ever defeated, even for a time (the mists fade; p. 242).
- **Sir Godfrey Gwilym** (Q37, p. 139): LE revenant, CR 6 (2,300 XP).
  - *Spells:* casts as a 16th-level paladin, save DC 14: *command, divine favor, thunderous smite, branding smite, magic weapon, blinding smite, dispel magic, staggering smite*.
  - He knows the order's history and will tell it, but his **oaths to Vladimir stop him helping**.
  - *As Tarokka ally (Ghost A; Ch 1, pp. 15–16):* a DC 15 Cha (Persuasion) check that the order's honour can be restored brings him in. **(unlit)** His defection starts a fight with the other revenants. **(lit)** He alone remains, now **LG**, to help against Strahd at the castle.
- **Revenant knights:** 3 in Q13, 4 in Q37. Tattered chain (AC as leather); longsword ×2, 2d10+4.
- **Savid** (Q11, p. 133): N dusk elf **scout** at 4 hp, from the camp outside Vallaki (Ch 5, N9). Needle blights drove him here while he was searching for **Arabelle**. He shares the dragon's and the order's lore.
- **Kolya** (CN **bandit**), **Ezmerelda**, **Arrigal** (NE **assassin**) and two CE **bandits**: special events (p. 142).

## Events and triggers
- **Revenants of Barovia** (p. 130): a destroyed body is replaced; the spirit takes a new corpse (24 h, Rejuvenation). Roll d20 for where it rises:

  | d20 | Where it rises |
  |---|---|
  | 1–4 | Barovia village cemetery |
  | 5–11 | Vallaki churchyard |
  | 12 | Q15 |
  | 13 | Krezk |
  | 14 | Berez |
  | 15–16 | A grave in the Svalich Woods |
  | 17–20 | The corpse of a dead PC or NPC |

  Their vengeance can't be completed, so they keep returning.
- **Lighting the beacon** (pp. 135, 142): the light stays even if the tower falls.
  - **+1 AC and +1 on saving throws** for Strahd's opponents while in Barovia.
  - **All revenants rest.** Afterwards, a revenant random encounter means no encounter.
  - **Visibility:** seen from Vallaki, Krezk, Berez, Old Bonegrinder, Van Richten's Tower and the werewolf den. Strahd sees the glow in the west.
  - Once lit, Q6, Q13, Q15, Q36 and Q37 all change. The book is silent on the phantom warriors.
- **Special Delivery** (p. 142): Kolya leaves a cart holding a **Henrik van der Voort coffin** with a PC's name on it. A **bat swarm** bursts out.
- **Arrigal's Hunt** (p. 142): Ezmerelda arrives on a stolen horse, with **Arrigal** hunting her. He won't fight the party and leaves if refused.

## Secrets [DM]
- Every haunting points to one goal: the **skull (K67, 250 lb)** into **Q16**. "A place of ill omen" means the Larders of Ill Omen.
- **Secret doors:** Q6 ↔ Q11 and Q30 → Q36. **Traps:** Q25, Q51, Q23.
- The Q20 severed head is Strahd's illusion.
- Vladimir and Godfrey were lovers.
- The knights are now LE: hatred, not honour.

## Player-facing [PLAYER]
- **Rumours:** a haunted mansion west of Vallaki where a dragon died (Ch 5, Vallaki Lore, p. 96); the fallen knights are Strahd's undead enemies (Morgantha, Ch 3, Dream Pastries, p. 48); a wandering revenant urges the party to seek Vladimir (Ch 2, p. 31).
- **Visible:** the statue, the octagonal tower, the sunrise altar, portraits of the thistledown-haired noble, the Saints window, the empty shield patch.
- **Told (paraphrase):** the epitaph, Savid's lore, Godfrey's history, Vladimir's warning, the fire's plea, the dragon's request, the Q44 rhyme.
- **Handout:** the **Journal of Argynvost** (App F, p. 256). It is his last page before the final battle: he regrets his bones won't rest in the mausoleum and hopes his spirit becomes a beacon.
