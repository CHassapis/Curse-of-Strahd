# Castle Ravenloft, part 2 (K48–K88): published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 4, pp. 70–94. Layer: [BOOK]. Draft (reader 4b).
Part 1 (chapter intro, random encounters, K1–K47) is `castle-ravenloft-part1.md`.

## At a glance
- Three zones. **Spires** (K48–K60a, pp. 70–74): Escher's lounge, the guest room, the Barovian witches' rooms, the tower roofs and bridge, the high tower peak (Pidlwick II), and the north tower peak (a locked chest with a crown). **Larders of Ill Omen** (K61–K72, pp. 74–79): the elevator trap, Cyrus Belview, the wine cellar and kitchen, the Hall of Bones with **Argynvost's skull**, the guard barracks and **Rahadin's office**. **Dungeon and Catacombs** (K73–K88, pp. 79–94): flooded cells reached by teleport pits (coins, Emil), the torture chamber, the brazier teleport room (two iron golems), 40 crypts, **Sergei's tomb**, **Strahd's tomb** (his coffin and the three brides), and the tomb of Barov and Ravenovia.
- Why it matters. K86 is Strahd's resting place, where he must be finished (Epilogue, p. 207). 8 of the 14 "Strahd's location" cards and 11 of the 40 treasure cards point into this range (Ch 1, pp. 12–17). The range also holds Patrina Velikovna (Kasimir's sister), the coven's hag eye on Cyrus, Ireena's prepared crypt, and Saint Markovia's thighbone.
- Level. The book gives no level. Threats run from CR 1/4 up to two CR 16 iron golems (only if provoked) and fifteen wights. It is built for the endgame.
- The chapter **ends at K88 (p. 94)**. There is no closing section, special-events section or conclusion after it. The aftermath of Strahd's fall is in the Epilogue (pp. 207–208).
- Book inconsistency: the high tower shaft is 450 ft to K84 in K59 (p. 73) but 390 ft tall in K18a (p. 58).

## Areas

### Spires of Ravenloft (maps 6–10)
- **K48 Offstair** (p. 70). A dusty spiral stair from K47, past K54, up to K57.
- **K49 Lounge** (p. 70). Escher, a vampire spawn and a neglected favourite of Strahd, lounges on a couch. [DM] If attacked, he leaps out of a window onto the keep roof (K53) and leads his pursuers straight to Strahd, wherever Strahd is. The windows are unlocked. A window left open has a 50% chance of drawing a vampire spawn that is crawling on the tower's outside. The bookcase holds joke titles of no value. Treasure: a platinum ring with roses and thorns (150 gp) and a gold-and-ruby pendant (750 gp). [PLAYER] Faded couches, three hanging lanterns, a handsome young man in worn finery. He is witty and melancholy.
- **K50 Guest Room** (p. 70). A canopied bed and divans. It is safe by day. [DM] A short rest here at night is interrupted by 1d4 Barovian witches from K56 casting *sleep*; a wounded witch retreats.
- **K51 Closet** (p. 70). Mildew, iron hooks, one black cloak. [DM] A secret ceiling trapdoor leads to K55: search plus DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) to find it. It opens only by pulling the cloak's hook (the cloak is the witches' reminder), or with thieves' tools or *knock*.
- **K52 Smokestack** (p. 71). A chimney 60 ft down to the K37 fireplace. A creature that starts its turn inside takes 1d6 fire.
- **K53 Rooftop** (p. 71). Loose tiles and gargoyle statues; the courtyard is 130 ft below. DC 15 Dexterity (Acrobatics) to cross (automatic if crawling). Failing by 5 or more slides the character off, falling 40 ft to the parapet (K46).
- **K54 Familiar Room** (p. 71). Shredded couches and three cats, the witches' familiars. If they see the party, the witches are warned.
- **K55 Element Room** (p. 72). Labelled jars of witch ingredients (no potions). The windows are locked, with the same 50% spawn chance if left open. A drag trail in the dust runs from the NE corner to the east door. [DM] Because of the trail, the secret floor trapdoor in the NE corner is found without a check. It opens only if someone knocks on it three times; no check reveals this, and the witches or divination can. It leads down to K51.
- **K56 Cauldron** (p. 72). A pungent smell and cackling (unless the witches are warned). Seven Barovian witches (minus any lost at K50). If warned, they wait invisible in the corners. They use spells at range and *alter self* claws. The cauldron heats any liquid for 3 hours when touched while saying the command word ("Gorah!"), once per dawn. Captured witches trade the command word and the K55 trick for their lives. Treasure: each witch has a potion of healing, each with a 30% chance of being a potion of poison. A crumbling evil spellbook lies behind the cauldron (a non-evil holder takes 1d10 psychic; see Treasure).
- **K57 Tower Roof** (p. 72). A 60-ft battlemented roof and a railless bridge north. The courtyard is 190 ft below, the keep roof 80 ft. (It is a Rahadin random-encounter destination, Ch 4 p. 51.)
- **K58 Bridge** (p. 73). Links K20 and K57. A creature that takes damage on it makes a DC 10 Dexterity save or falls 60 ft onto the keep roof.
- **K59 High Tower Peak** (p. 73). A walkway rings the 15-ft mouth of shaft K18a, which drops to the catacombs. There are arrow slits and a broken roof open to the sky (bats and Beucephalus fly out this way). **Pidlwick II** hides in the rafters (passive Perception against its Stealth). See NPCs.
- **K60 North Tower Peak** (p. 74). Manacles, a bed with leather restraints, a locked iron chest with an emblem, and a ladder to a rotten trapdoor. Treasure: the chest (key on Cyrus, K62) holds a **bejewelled gold crown (2,500 gp)**. Teleport arrival point for the orange flame from K78. Tarokka: treasure in the chest (Transmuter); Strahd standing by the chest (Marionette).
- **K60a North Tower Rooftop** (p. 74). The storm clouds form Strahd's face and pour out bats. Characters who stay on the roof face 10 swarms of bats in 3 rounds. If they go back down, the bats fly via K59 and K18a to roost in K84. The courtyard is 260 ft below.

### Larders of Ill Omen (map 11)
- **K61 Elevator Trap** (pp. 74–76). A 30-ft hall. [DM] The middle 10-ft square (marked T on the map) is a lift. It triggers at 400 lb or when the lever in K31 is raised. Searching with DC 15 Perception reveals seams. DC 15 Investigation shows it can't be disarmed from here (the machinery is disabled at K31, which takes 1 minute). When it triggers, portcullises drop and the compartment rises the 170-ft shaft (K31a) in 1 round. Sleep gas: DC 15 Constitution save or fall unconscious. A 10-ft granite counterweight drops into the eastern half of the shaft and pulverises anything in the space it lands on. Occupants roll initiative and get one turn. A secret hatch in the compartment ceiling needs DC 10 Perception and opens downward. Anyone on top at the stop makes a DC 15 Dexterity save against 8d10 bludgeoning (half on a success). The lift stops under the K47 trapdoor and stays up until the K31 lever is lowered. The noise carries through the whole castle, and Strahd reaches trapped or sleeping victims via K47.
- **K62 Servants' Hall** (p. 76). Knee-high fog, a lantern, and **Cyrus Belview** shuffling toward the party (see NPCs). He never attacks first. A twine loop around his neck carries the **iron key to the K60 chest** and a **wooden pendant with a varnished human eye: a hag eye** that Morgantha gave him so she could spy on Strahd. He doesn't know it is magic, and the book refers the DM to the Monster Manual's "Hag Covens" sidebar. Exits: stairs up to K23; a portcullis to K63 (1-inch bars, DC 20 Strength to lift); steel-banded double doors west to K67.
- **K63 Wine Cellar** (pp. 77–78). Rats (Cyrus's pets, harmless) and a half-inch crack into K18. Twelve Wizard of Wines casks. North (3): empty. East (6): empty, except one holding **yellow mold** (DC 13 Perception on close inspection; spores if smashed). South (3): the middle one holds a **black pudding** that bursts out if the cask is broken. Tarokka: treasure hidden by Cyrus in an empty north cask (Merchant). Rahadin random-encounter destination.
- **K64 Guards' Stair** (p. 78). From K68, past K13, up to K46.
- **K65 Kitchen** (p. 78). A cauldron-pot over a fire pit. Looking in raises **3 zombies** (13 hp each). If Cyrus is present, he clubs them back into the pot.
- **K66 Butler's Quarters** (p. 78). Cyrus's lair: junk, a tapestry of the castle, heaps of salvaged adventurers' gear. Nothing of value. With Cyrus along, he strokes the party's gear, looking forward to adding it to his collection.
- **K67 Hall of Bones** (p. 78). Desecrated ground (DMG). Cyrus's ossuary art: bone furniture, a bone chandelier, ten bone chairs and a bone bowl. The floor is stained with the blood of guards Strahd slaughtered. **Argynvost's skull** (a silver dragon killed by Strahd's army before the castle was founded) is mounted **above the east double doors; it weighs 250 lb**. Tarokka: treasure on the bone table (Anarchist); Strahd seated at the table's end holding an old foe's skull (Donjon).
- **K68 Guards' Run** (p. 79). A cold corridor: north door to K67, stair K64 south, archway west to K69.
- **K69 Guards' Quarters** (p. 79). Harmless yellow lichen; alcoves with guard skeletons. At the hall's midpoint, **10 skeletons** attack.
- **K70 Kingsmen Hall** (p. 79). A wrecked room with weapons driven into the walls (DC 10 Strength to pull out, worthless); scene of Strahd's slaughter of his guards.
- **K71 Kingsmen Quarters** (p. 79). Alcoves; stair K20a up to K20. [DM] A loose flagstone in the SE alcove (DC 10 Perception) hides **150 ep** stamped with Strahd's profile.
- **K72 Chamberlain's Office** (p. 79). An orderly room with weapons bearing the Barovian crest. **Rahadin** waits here to kill the party, unless he has been beaten elsewhere. [DM] A **shadow demon** strikes the nearest character from behind in the round after the fight starts (noticed only if that character's passive Perception meets its Stealth). Both fight to the death. A secret door at the north end of the west wall opens on stair K79.

### Dungeon and Catacombs (map 12)
- **K73 Dungeon Hall** (pp. 79–80). Stairs from K21 descend into 3 ft of opaque water. Half-submerged iron doors lead north and south, and a weak cry comes from the south (Emil). [DM] Weight-sensitive trapdoors lie off a safe path (see the "Traps in Area K73" diagram): 5% chance per 10 lb. The 10-ft pit under each is a teleport trap that sends a Medium or smaller creature into a flooded cell (K74a, K74c, K74e, K74h or K75d). Onlookers see a burst of air and water. The trapdoor recharges after 24 hours.
- **K74 North Dungeon** (pp. 80–81). A rusty door (DC 10 Athletics, underwater) opens on 5 ft of water; 8 barred cells line the corridor, and one glows. Cell locks take thieves' tools, DC 20 and 1 minute (disadvantage from inside), or DC 25 Strength. Strahd checks the cells now and then in mist form.
  - K74a: **3,000 ep** underfoot (a character scoops 100 coins a minute). Teleport destination.
  - K74b: the door is ajar; **300 pp**.
  - K74c: a half-elf corpse in leather armour: a longsword, **5 gems (50 gp each)**, a **potion of heroism**. Teleport destination.
  - K74d, K74f: empty.
  - K74e: teleport destination. [DM] A one-way secret door 5 ft up the north wall (*knock* only, from this side) leads to chute K82.
  - K74g: a **gray ooze**, effectively invisible underwater.
  - K74h: a glowing **sentient +1 shortsword** (lawful good). Teleport destination.
- **K75 South Dungeon** (pp. 81–82). The same door. A gruff voice asks who is there.
  - K75a: **Emil Toranescu** (werewolf, 72 hp). See NPCs.
  - K75b: **2,100 ep**.
  - K75c, K75e, K75h: empty.
  - K75d: a dwarf skeleton in rusted plate with a usable nonmagical battleaxe. Teleport destination.
  - K75f: a dead human wizard, shackled, bled to death by Strahd (fang marks).
  - K75g: a stout bard hung upside down with his head underwater, drowned; a smashed lyre.
- **K76 Torture Chamber** (p. 82). A 50-ft square room under 3 ft of water, with torture devices still holding skeletons and a balcony to the north. Once anyone is more than 10 ft in, **6 Strahd zombies** rise. Rahadin random-encounter destination.
- **K77 Observation Balcony** (p. 82). Two thrones and a red curtain. A door behind the curtain.
- **K78 Brazier Room** (pp. 82–84). A heatless white flame with 7 coloured crystal spheres on the brazier's rim. An hourglass hangs overhead with a glowing verse in Common that hints at each colour's destination. Two **iron golems** stand as mounted knights. Throwing a stone in turns the flame that colour for 5 rounds, and touching it teleports the toucher: red to K37 (study), orange to K60, **yellow to K86 (Strahd's tomb)**, green to Vallaki N6f (the vampire nest in the coffin maker's shop), blue to the Amber Temple X42, indigo to the Abbey S17, violet to Tsolenka Pass T4. The flame then returns to white and the stone reappears. The hourglass has AC 12, 20 hp and thunder vulnerability; the brazier has AC 17, 25 hp and resistance to all damage. *Dispel magic* (DC 16) puts the flame out for 1 hour. [DM] Attacking the brazier, the hourglass or a golem slams and locks the doors (DC 25 Athletics; AC 15, 25 hp) and wakes the golems: two poison breaths in round 1, then a sword and a hoof attack each. They never leave the room.
- **K79 Western Stair** (p. 84). Dusty and cobwebbed; it rises to K72's secret door. [DM] A *glyph of warding* on the landing (DC 15 Investigation once the dust is brushed off) conjures a one-time *major image* of an enraged Strahd. Attacks pass through it, and it melts away on initiative count 0.
- **K80 Center Stair** (p. 84). Light fog; links K78 and K81.
- **K81 Tunnel** (p. 84). A newer tunnel cut into the Pillarstone: 6-ft ceiling, fog, 120 ft long, with a stone door east (crypt 1). [DM] A trapdoor at the midpoint is found only by an active search with DC 20 Perception. 100 lb or more opens it and dumps everyone into chute K82; it can be spiked shut.
- **K82 Marble Slide** (p. 85). A polished black chute through a one-way secret door into K74e. No damage, and it can't be climbed without magic.
- **K83 Spiral Stair / K83a Landing** (p. 85; the landing is on map 11). K78 up to K37. Treasure: a **tapestry of King Barov leading his knights (750 gp, 10 lb)**, worthless if damaged unless mended.
- **K84 Catacombs** (pp. 85–93). A 110 × 180 ft hall of arched walkways between 10-ft crypt-pillars; 20-ft ceiling, fog, bat guano. **Tens of thousands of bats** roost here by day and fly out at night via K18a. They attack only if provoked or ordered; harming bats in a 10-ft square forms 2d4 swarms there (once per square per dawn). Exits: the door by crypt 1 (to K81); barred archways north (K85), south (K86, warded) and east (K87); K18/K18a to the west. Crypt doors are stone slabs (an action and DC 15 Strength to move). Unless noted, each crypt holds a marble bier and a skeleton in rags.
  - [DM] **Teleport-trap ring around K86.** Three invisible 10-ft squares: between crypts 37 and 38, between 37 and the south wall, and between 38 and the south wall. *Detect magic* shows conjuration. *Dispel magic* (DC 16) suppresses a square for 1 minute, and an antimagic field also suppresses it. A creature entering one swaps places with a **wight from crypt 14**, which appears and attacks.
  - Crypts (name, then what matters):
    1. Epitaph only. Not a crypt: a door to tunnel K81.
    2. Artista DeSlop, court ceiling painter. An imp-and-flower mural; a box of brushes and dried paint.
    3. Lady Isolde Yunk. Heaps of junk antiques, not worth hauling.
    4. Prince Ariel du Plumette. Evil **ghost** with artificial wings. A possessed host climbs to K59 and leaps down the shaft.
    5. Artank Swilovich. Thousands of empty Wizard of Wines bottles. Tarokka treasure under the bottles, found automatically (Guild Member).
    6. **Saint Markovia**. [DM] A pressure plate in front fires poison darts (DMG sample trap; 4 uses). Inside, a smell of roses and one thighbone; a faint ghost urges its use against the vampire. **Saint Markovia's thighbone** (magic, App C).
    7. Endorovich. The door is down. Disturbing the bones wakes one of two **gargoyles** (77 hp each), and his spirit jumps to the second when the first falls. Backstory: the poisoned-glass tragedy linked to the Ivlis crossroads grave (Ch 2, area F). Tarokka: compartment under the remains (Swashbuckler).
    8. Duchess Dorfniya Dilisnya. A preserved quilt, no value.
    9. Pidlwick. A small skeleton; Pidlwick II won't enter. A **deck of illusions** is here only if the ghost of Pidlwick was summoned at K36.
    10. Sir Leonid Krushkin. A giant skeleton and a harmless maul; **3 jewelled necklaces (750 gp each)**.
    11. Tasha Petrovna. A sun mural: creatures hurt by sunlight have disadvantage on checks, attacks and saves inside. A **sun holy symbol (25 gp)**. A good-aligned character who lifts it hears a message pointing to a rose-carved grave at the Abbey of Saint Markovia, where it unlocks a **ring of regeneration** (Ch 8, S7, p. 148).
    12. King Troisky. A three-faced helm (nonmagical). [DM] Lifting it without replacing 10 lb releases poison gas: DC 14 Constitution, 4d10 (DC 12 Perception spots the vents).
    13. King Katsky. A **musket, a powder horn and 20 silvered bullets** (DMG firearms), and a **dragon-wing glider** (80-lb limit; AC 12, 1 hp).
    14. Stahbal Indi-Bhak. A 40-ft shaft (DC 10 Athletics without a kit) to a vault of 15 coffins holding **15 wights**, less any teleported out; coffin lids need DC 15 Strength. Each wight slain raises 2d6 skeletons from the bone-strewn floor (up to 100).
    15. Khazan (archmage turned lich). **Black-opal eyes (2 × 1,000 gp) and 8 amber teeth (100 gp each)**. Speaking his name in the crypt summons a **staff of power**. The first to grab it makes a DC 17 Constitution save (8d10 lightning, half on a success); if no one grabs it within 1 round it vanishes for good.
    16. Elsa Fallona von Twitterberg. Her nine consorts' bones in painted alcoves; nothing of value.
    17. Admiral Spinwitovich. A funeral barge and clay coins painted gold (worthless).
    18. **"Ireena Kolyana: Wife"**. Freshly engraved, the door set aside, empty and swept. Strahd's place for Ireena once she is spawn.
    19. Artimus, the castle's builder. Nothing.
    20. **Sasha Ivliskova, "Wife"**. An old wife of Strahd, a **vampire spawn** in a web shroud. She hopes her husband has come, then attacks.
    21. **Patrina Velikovna, "Bride"**. A **banshee** that wails on opening. See NPCs, Treasure and Secrets.
    22. Sir Erik Vonderbucks. A gilded corpse; **500 gp** of gold if peeled.
    23. [DM] A random character's name appears on the door; inside, a corpse that looks like that character melts at a touch. Later visits find it blank and empty. (Strahd seals defeated characters here, Epilogue p. 207.)
    24. Ivan Ivliskovich, dog-racing champion. A peeling winter-forest mural; nothing.
    25. Stefan Gregorovich. A polished skull answers 5 questions a day, as *speak with dead*, while in the crypt. **Its answers about Strahd and the castle are false.**
    26. Intree Sik-Valoo. The same, but **its answers are true**.
    27. No door. **3 giant wolf spiders** leap at anyone passing in front.
    28. Bascal Ofenheiss, chef. [DM] Ringing the bell inside fills the crypt with fire: DC 17 Dexterity, 4d10, and those who fail burn for 1d10 a turn. An **electrum spork (250 gp)** under his hat.
    29. Baron Eisglaze Drüf. **Brown mold** (reaches 5 ft beyond the doorway). Under it, a **luck blade with one wish**: a wish to escape Barovia fails, and a wish for Strahd's destruction teleports him to the sword.
    30. Prefect Ciril Romulich. Stone ravens (harmless). A **jewelled gold Morninglord holy symbol (750 gp)**: an evil creature touching it destroys it, dealing 2d10 radiant to everything within 5 ft.
    31. An epitaph about wealth; a gold-coin mural. [DM] The floor is a spring-loaded **30-ft spiked pit** (100 lb). A dead adventurer at the bottom: rope, dagger, **25 pp, scroll of magic circle**. Tarokka: treasure by the skeleton (Mercenary).
    32. Unnamed; a carved warning over two alcoves. [DM] The **east alcove teleports to the east alcove of K86**; the west alcove receives arrivals from K86's west alcove.
    33. Sir Klutz Tripalotsky. Pulling the sword from the armour summons a **phantom warrior** who fights for the puller for 7 days (he knows nothing of Strahd).
    34. King Dostron the Hellborn. A lead sarcophagus clad in **gold (500 gp, 10 lb, pried off)**, a stuffed owlbear, and an invisible **imp** bound to the remains. The imp lies (a "pit fiend" inside) but won't fight.
    35. Sir Jarnwald the Trickster. [DM] An illusory floor over a smooth 20-ft pit with **6 ghouls** under permanent *silence* (dispel DC 14 each). A **signet ring (25 gp)**.
    36. Name clawed away. Nothing.
    37. Gralmore Nimblenobs. [DM] The marble end of his staff in the slab's recess lifts the slab for 1 minute, revealing **3 scrolls: cone of cold, fireball, lightning bolt**. The brass end deals 4d10 lightning to the holder. Tarokka: in the compartment (Evoker).
    38. General Grislek ("Mad Dog"). **3 hell hounds**, then his **wraith** commanding them in Infernal. A shattered **silvered spear** (*mending* restores it). Tarokka: compartment under the bones (Berserker).
    39. Beucephalus. An oversized door (DC 20 Strength). Strahd's **nightmare** (104 hp). Kill it and Strahd hunts the killers mercilessly. It leaves via K18a and K59.
    40. Tatsaul Eris. Torches flare on first entry; the bones are plaster fakes.
- **K85 Sergei's Tomb** (p. 93). A portcullis (DC 25 Strength), with a lever inside on the south wall. White marble and a sense of calm. An inlaid coffin on a slab carved with Sergei's name, and three alcoves with pristine statues (a young man and two angels). The coffin opens at a lawful good creature's touch, otherwise DC 15 Strength. Sergei's body is preserved as if asleep and **wears +2 plate armour**. Tarokka: treasure in the coffin (Paladin); Strahd lying across the coffin weeping (Broken One, Innocent).
- **K86 Strahd's Tomb** (pp. 93–94). A heavy portcullis (DC 25 Strength), with a lever inside on the north wall. Black marble steps and freshly turned earth. A shining black waxed coffin with brass fittings, and three alcoves. [DM] **The three brides lie buried in the earth by the east wall** and rise against anyone who approaches Strahd's coffin. The west alcove teleports to crypt 32 (west); the east alcove receives arrivals from crypt 32 (east); the centre alcove is mundane. The yellow flame (K78) lands at the foot of the stairs. Treasure: the brides' jewellery. Tarokka: treasure in the centre alcove (Warrior); Strahd in his coffin, ready for whoever lifts the lid (Darklord, Horseman).
- **K87 Guardians** (p. 94). Two 30-ft bronze spear-bearing statues and a soft blue curtain of light. Going east to west is free. Going west to east, only lawful good creatures pass; everyone else is teleported back to the top of the stairs. A Small creature can squeeze behind a statue.
- **K88 Tomb of King Barov and Queen Ravenovia** (p. 94). Grimy, opaque stained-glass windows that can be smashed (the K6 overlook is 110 ft above; a fall out of them is about 900 ft). A gold mosaic ceiling, not worth prying out. North coffin: a wax effigy of Barov, with his bones in a compartment beneath. South coffin: Ravenovia's skeleton under a white shroud (the preservation failed). Tarokka: treasure on Ravenovia's coffin (Shepherd); Strahd in a frenzy of rage and despair (Ghost, Raven).

## NPCs
- **Escher** (K49; vampire spawn; App D). A dashing out-of-favour consort, witty and melancholy. He dreads being locked in K84 with the other castoffs. If attacked, he flees to Strahd and leads pursuers there. Carries 900 gp of jewellery. Fate: if Strahd dies, he leaves the realm to seek a way to become a vampire lord (Epilogue, p. 208).
- **Barovian witches** ×7 (+1d4 at K50; App D). Sworn to Strahd for power. They trade secrets for their lives. The cats are their familiars.
- **Pidlwick II** (K59; App D, CR 1/4). A clockwork effigy of the fool Pidlwick. Mute; understands Common; can't read. Kindness makes it a silent guide who knows the castle. Meanness leads it to push someone down a staircase (DC 10 Dexterity, 1d6 per 10 ft). It murdered the real Pidlwick (App D) and won't enter crypt 9.
- **Cyrus Belview** (K62; mongrelfolk, CR 1/4; Keen Hearing and Smell). Strahd's crazed, devoted servant: lizard scales, panther ears, a duck foot. He giggles and tells bad jokes. He wants to take guests to "their room in the tower" (K49) through the elevator trap (advantage on the sleep save), then return to the kitchen. He carries the K60 chest key and the **hag eye** (unaware it is magic). If the key is taken, he wails that the master will be displeased; DC 10 Intimidation gets the key's purpose, the chest and its contents out of him. He and Strahd alone know the command word for the K2 center court gate portcullis (Ch 4, K2, p. 54). He built the K67 ossuary and wired up the decorative skeletons in K23 and K26 (pp. 59, 61).
- **Rahadin** (K72; App D, CR 10). The chamberlain. He waits here to kill intruders and fights to the death alongside a shadow demon. App D (pp. 236–237): he doubted Patrina, told her to leave when Tatyana arrived, slew the dusk elf women after the stoning, and cut off Kasimir's ears.
- **Emil Toranescu** (K75a; werewolf, 72 hp). He claims to be a Vallakian chased here by dire wolves. In truth Strahd jailed him for splitting Kiril's pack (Ch 15). He betrays his rescuers to win Strahd's favour, unless they say they are allies of his wife Zuleika; then he just slips away to her. His presence changes the Werewolf Den (Ch 15, pp. 203–205).
- **Sasha Ivliskova** (crypt 20; vampire spawn). An old wife, discarded.
- **Patrina Velikovna** (crypt 21; banshee). A dusk elf and black-arts scholar, nearly Strahd's match, who sought a dark marriage with him. Her own people (led by her brother Kasimir) stoned her to death before he could finish draining her; Strahd claimed the body. Once woken, she roams up to 5 miles from the crypt. At 0 hp she re-forms in the crypt 24 hours later, until she is formally wed to Strahd; *hallow* on the crypt keeps her out while it lasts. Restored by **Kasimir's dark gift** (Zhudun, Ch 13 X42, p. 196), she returns as an **NE dusk elf archmage with no spells prepared**. She asks sweetly for her spellbook, claiming she will help destroy Strahd (a lie), studies the party, and pursues her own ends: becoming Strahd's vampire bride, and getting the party to kill Rahadin for mutilating her brother. Strahd has lost interest; given the chance, he makes her spawn and re-entombs her. Epilogue (p. 208): if she lives after Strahd falls, she loots the castle and temple to become Barovia's new master. App D (Kasimir, pp. 232–233): she speaks to him in dreams, claims she has repented, and told him the Amber Temple holds the secret of restoring the dead. His bond is to bring her back.
- **The three brides** (K86; vampire spawn, CR 5 each): **Ludmilla Vilisevic, Anastrasya Karelova, Volenta Popofsky**. The book gives them names, gowns and jewellery only; no personalities or roles. Freed if Strahd dies (Epilogue, p. 208).
- **Beucephalus** (crypt 39; nightmare, 104 hp). Strahd's steed.
- **Sir Klutz** (crypt 33; phantom warrior, App D, CR 3). An ally for 7 days.
- **The imp** (crypt 34). A bound liar; not hostile.
- **Speaking skulls** (crypts 25 and 26). One liar, one truth-teller; 5 questions a day each.

## Events and triggers
- **Cyrus's escort** (K62 to K61 to K47 to K49): he springs the elevator deliberately; the noise alerts the castle; Strahd can come to sleepers via K47 ("the master will be along shortly").
- **Night rest in K50** brings 1d4 witches. **Loitering on K60a** brings 10 bat swarms in 3 rounds.
- **Attacking Escher** makes him flee straight to Strahd, dragging pursuers into the showdown.
- **K73 pits** scatter the party into separate flooded cells. Each pit recharges after 24 hours.
- **K78**: coloured flames teleport for 5 rounds each; any attack on the fixtures wakes the golems.
- **K84**: harming bats forms swarms. Entering the ring of teleport traps swaps characters with crypt 14's wights. Crypt 32's east alcove is the back door into K86.
- **Patrina**, once woken, roams and re-forms daily.
- **Killing Beucephalus** makes Strahd hunt the killers.
- **Emil** betrays the party at a good moment unless Zuleika is invoked.
- Strahd checks the dungeon cells now and then in mist form (K74).
- **Rahadin random encounter** (Ch 4, p. 51) sends the party to K57, K63 or K76 (among others). Strahd is there only if the card says so.
- **Showdown rule** (Ch 1, p. 17): the first time the party reaches the location on card 5, Strahd is there, unless he has been forced back into his coffin.
- **Epilogue** (pp. 207–208): Strahd must be destroyed in his coffin (K86). If he wins, he seals the party in crypt 23 and makes Ireena spawn, sealed in crypt 18. Rahadin's revenge follows Strahd's death. His spawn (Escher, the brides) go free. A living Patrina reaches for rule.

## Secrets [DM]
- The **hag eye** on Cyrus is the Bonegrinder coven's spy on Strahd (K62, p. 76; Ch 6, p. 125). Cyrus doesn't know; the book never says whether Strahd does. The CoS text gives no rules of its own for it. Outside rules: MM (2014) Hags, pp. 176–177: one eye per coven; a new eye needs all three hags; if it is destroyed, each coven member takes 3d10 psychic and is blinded for 24 h. DMG (2024) p. 265 Hag Eye: an uncommon item with 3 charges (*darkvision* or *see invisibility*) and a coven sensor; no effect on destruction stated.
- **Patrina is not repentant.** Her dream-messages to Kasimir are manipulation; restored, she lies about helping.
- **Crypt 23** is an illusion keyed to a character; **crypt 40** holds fake bones; **crypt 17's** gold is clay; **crypt 3's** antiques are junk.
- **Crypts 25 and 26**: the well-kept skulls look alike, but one lies and one tells the truth.
- **Luck blade** (crypt 29): wishing to leave Barovia fails, and wishing for Strahd's destruction summons him to the sword.
- **Secret and one-way passages**: K51 ceiling trapdoor (cloak hook); K55 floor trapdoor (knock 3 times); K72 to K79 secret door; K74e one-way door to K82; K81 hidden trapdoor into K82; the K61 compartment's ceiling hatch; crypt 32 and K86 alcoves; the K63 crack to K18; crypt 14's shaft.
- **Portcullis levers** for K85 and K86 are *inside* the tombs.
- **K87**: only lawful good creatures pass east; a Small creature can bypass it behind a statue.
- **Ireena's crypt (18)** is already prepared and freshly engraved as "Wife".
