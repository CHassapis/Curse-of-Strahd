# Van Richten's Tower, Wizard of Wines, Yester Hill, Werewolf Den — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 11 pp. 167–172, Ch 12 pp. 173–180, Ch 14 pp. 197–200, Ch 15 pp. 201–205 (plus Ch 1 Tarokka and App C–D). Layer: [BOOK]. Draft (reader 6a).

## At a glance
- **Van Richten's Tower (area V, Ch 11)**: the dead lich Khazan's tower on an island in Lake Baratok, reached by a 100-yard causeway. Van Richten used it as a base, then moved to Vallaki. Ezmerelda now lives there while she searches for him. Inside is a magic-dead zone, the door is a dance puzzle guarded by lightning, and her wagon is booby-trapped. Book level 6 (Intro, Areas by Level, p. 6).
- **The Wizard of Wines (area W, Ch 12)**: the Martikov wereravens' winery, which supplies Barovia with wine. Druids and blights have taken it, and all three magic gems that keep the vines alive are gone. Level 5.
- **Yester Hill (area Y, Ch 14)**: druids who worship Strahd, with berserkers, at the edge of the mists. Here stand the Gulthias tree (the source of every blight) and a 50-ft effigy of Strahd holding a stolen gem, ready to become the tree blight Wintersplinter. Level 6.
- **Werewolf Den (area Z, Ch 15)**: Kiril Stoyanovich's pack, the Children of Mother Night, west of Lake Baratok. It holds caged children, a split in the pack, and Zuleika's grief. Level 7.
- Milestones: the party gains a level for obtaining the Tome or the Sunsword, for stopping the Yester Hill ritual, and for allying with Ezmerelda (Intro, Character Levels, p. 6).

## Areas

### Ch 11 — Van Richten's Tower (V)
- **Approach, p. 167** — [PLAYER] misty lake, causeway, 80-ft four-level tower, gash with failing scaffolds, mossy griffon statues (decorative), a muddy barrel-top wagon by the door.
- **Khazan's Spell Drain, p. 167** — [DM] antimagic field on the tower + 5 ft; Khazan's trap (V2), golems (V4) and armor (V7) still work; unlocking magic fails.
- **V1 Ezmerelda's Magic Wagon, p. 168** — [PLAYER] purple, gold trim, lanterns, padlock, "keep out" sign. [DM] *Identify* gives command words "Drovash"/"Arvesh" (summon/dismiss two quasi-real draft horses, speed 30). Trapdoor underneath (DC 13 Perception from below) is **the only safe way in**. **Booby trap:** the door handle sets off 101 flasks of alchemist's fire — within 30 ft DC 12 Con, 55 (10d10) fire, half on success, disadvantage inside/within 5 ft; wagon and contents destroyed. From inside: spotted automatically, DC 10 Dex to disarm, failure triggers it.
- **V2 Tower Door, p. 169** — [PLAYER] handle-less iron door, eight stick figures on connected lines, "Khazan" on the lintel; **show the door-symbol handout**. [DM] Touching it untrapped: lightning sheath, DC 15 Dex (disadvantage in metal armor), 22 (4d10), and 22 to anyone entering/starting there, 10 min. **Third trigger collapses the tower** (24d10 inside; DC 15 Dex or 8d10 within 20 ft; V7 armor lost; chest dug out in 1d8 + 2 h; 10%/h berserk clay golem). **Opening:** an action within 5 ft copying the eight arm poses in line order (either end, no repeats) opens it for 10 min; wrong order summons a **young blue dragon** (gone at 0 hp or when the door opens). Vestibule (p. 170): opens safely from inside.
- **V3 Scaffolding, p. 170** — [DM] 200 lb max; 20-ft fall + 2d6 piercing; DC 13 Dex or 4d6 below.
- **V4 First Floor, p. 170** — [DM] four **clay golems** run the chain lift (5 ft/round, lift orders only); fight only if attacked; one destroyed stops the lift.
- **V5 / V6, pp. 170–171** — [DM] rotten floors around the shaft hold 150 lb (20-ft fall) / 50 lb (40-ft fall).
- **V7 Fourth Floor, p. 171** — [PLAYER] bed, desk, stove, armor, chest. [DM] Van Richten memorised then **burned his notes and journals** here. **Animated armor** obeys whoever says "Khazan" within 10 ft. Chest: embalmed head of **Yan**, a Vistana thief; via *speak with dead* (away from the drain) he says Rictavio killed him for trying to steal the wagon and monkey, and begs the party to warn the Vistani. **Fortunes of Ravenloft:** Tarokka treasure in a hidden wall compartment behind the armor, which pulls it out on command.

### Ch 12 — The Wizard of Wines (W)
- **Background, p. 173** — three gems keep the vines alive; Krezkov then Martikov land; free wine for Barovia; Champagne du le Stomp no longer made. **Gem 1**: stolen ten years ago, fate unknown; Davian blames middle son **Urwin** (on watch), who denies it — estranged since. **Gem 2**: taken three weeks ago by Lysaga's scarecrows; animates her hut (Ch 10, U3). **Gem 3**: stolen five days ago by druids, at Yester Hill. Two days ago the druids drove the family out and poisoned the vats.
- **Approaching, p. 174** — [PLAYER] "Vineyard" sign, a cloaked man beckoning from a grove (nine Martikovs). [DM] **30 needle blights** close from 120 ft; barricade or fight. Fighting outside draws round 3: druid + 24 twig (W9); round 4: druid + 5 needle (W14); round 5: druid + 2 vine (W20). The W16 druid holds a **Gulthias staff**.
- **W1–W8, p. 176** — stable (2 horses); dock wagon with 3 barrels of Grapemash, forced south door; barrel workshop; 13 empty barrels; grape-crush veranda (doors chained/barred inside, DC 20 Str); well; outhouse; storage with bar-beam.
- **W9 Fermentation Vats, p. 176** — [DM] four **raven swarms** (never hostile; each kills a twig blight per turn once fighting starts); **druid** poisoning the vats; **24 twig blights** in the split eastern cask. Three western vats poisoned (20 barrels; *potion of poison*); antitoxin cures but spoils, *purify food and drink* cures.
- **W10 Glassblower's Workshop, p. 177** — barrel of sand. **Fortunes of Ravenloft:** Tarokka treasure in the sand.
- **W11 / W12, p. 177** — spiral stair and barrel ramp, both linking all three levels.
- **W13–W14, p. 178** — back stair; icy cellar split by a brick wall. [DM] **druid** + **5 needle blights** (opens with *thunderwave*, 1d20 + 10 bottles break); 3 barrels Grapemash, 40 bottles Red Dragon Crush. **Secret door** to W15.
- **W15 Brown Mold, p. 178** — [DM] ten patches; safe at a distance.
- **W16 Loading Winch, p. 178** — [DM] **druid** with the staff; flees onto the W2 wagon unless cornered. **Secret door** to W17.
- **W17 Master Bedroom, pp. 178–179** — Stefania and Dag's room now. Manifests: BV, BW, K, VISTANI; oldest entries "S" (Strahd). Chest key in a bedpost knob. **Secret door** to W16.
- **W18–W19, p. 179** — kitchen; bunk rooms; Blinsky's "Beucephalus" nightmare toy.
- **W20 Printing Press, p. 179** — [DM] **druid** + **2 vine blights**, to the death; cabinet holds the padlock key.

### Ch 14 — Yester Hill (Y)
- **Y1 Trail / Y2 Cairns, p. 197** — [PLAYER] dead grass, black cairns in two rings (ancient berserker tribe), lightning, fog to the west. One cairn holds Kavan's spear.
- **Y3 Druids' Circle, pp. 197–198** — [PLAYER] 250-ft boulder ring round a 50-ft fanged effigy. [DM] Climbing boulders: 10% for 44 (8d10) lightning; trails safe. **Statue**: Strahd's likeness, bound by Gulthias roots; **gem** in its heart; AC 10, 50 hp, immune poison/psychic; oil + fire burns 2d6/round; at 0 hp the gem rolls out; if the gem's place is divined, a climber digs it out on d20 13+. **Hidden graves**: six **druids** + six **berserkers** (passive Perception 16 spots the graves); rise if the statue is approached or harmed. They won't start the ritual without Strahd. Replenish 1d4 − 1 of each at dusk.
- **Y4 Gulthias Tree, p. 198** — [PLAYER] bleeding tree, axe in the trunk, skeleton. [DM] **3 vine, 6 needle, 12 twig blights** guard it. AC 15, 250 hp, immune bludgeoning/piercing/psychic; at 0 hp only seems dead (regrows); DC 15 Nature: uproot it; *hallow* kills it in 3d10 days. Dead tree = no new blights in Barovia (1,500 XP). **Fortunes of Ravenloft:** Tarokka treasure under the skeleton.
- **Y5 Wall of Fog, p. 200** — [PLAYER] a white fortress and city in the fog, a church bell. [DM] Dark Powers mirage of Strahd's homeland. Legend of the **Whispering Wall**: a god's last breath holding memories and futures; a prepared seeker can make a vision quest in it.

### Ch 15 — Werewolf Den (Z)
- **Background, p. 201** — Mother Night worshippers serving Strahd out of fear. Schism: Kiril made kidnapped children fight to the death and turned the winner; **Emil wanted every child kept and turned** to grow the pack. Strahd's dire wolves took Emil to the castle. Most of the pack, Kiril included, is out hunting on the first visit. Z8 ledge is 100 ft above the mouth, out of sight.
- **Z1 Cave Mouth, p. 203** — flute music. [DM] With Emil present, the den won't attack.
- **Z2 Guard Post, p. 203** — **Aziana, Davanka** (werewolves) raise the alarm.
- **Z3 Wolf Den, p. 203** — old **Skennis** (36 hp) + **9 wolves**.
- **Z4 Spring, p. 203** — pool, adult clothes in crates.
- **Z5 Deep Caves, p. 203** — crunching bones (Stealth disadvantage). **Z5a** **Bianca** (Kiril's mate). **Z5b** **Wensencia** + **Kellen** (10, werewolf child from Liam's Hold; zombie doll resembling a PC; cured by *remove curse* or *greater restoration*).
- **Z6 Kiril's Cave, p. 204** — skin curtain hides a stair tunnel to a **secret door** onto Z8.
- **Z7 Shrine of Mother Night, p. 204** — **eight caged children** (1 hp, age 1d6 + 6), statue, treasure hoard, two corpse offerings; **Zuleika** praying for Emil, set to guard the children. **Fortunes of Ravenloft (p. 205):** Tarokka treasure in the (cursed) hoard.
- **Z8 Ring of Stone, p. 205** — the children's fighting ring; **secret door** to the Z6 stair.

## NPCs
- **Rudolph van Richten / "Rictavio"** (App D p. 238; Rictavio, CR 5) — old human hunter disguised as a half-elf showman (*hat of disguise*, *ring of mind shielding*). Waits for the right moment to strike Strahd; studies the Keepers of the Feather; hunts Strahd's Vistani spies. Doesn't know Ezmerelda is in Barovia; if he learns, protects her quietly and steers adventurers to watch her. Works alone (a Vistani curse dooms his friends). **Retreats to his tower if nearly unmasked** (p. 238); names "an old tower to the west" after the tiger escapes (Ch 5, p. 124). Killed Yan. **Artifact** ally card: told of the reading, he drops the disguise and joins (Ch 1, p. 15).
- **Ezmerelda d'Avenir** (App D pp. 230–231; CR 8) — Vistana hunter, prosthetic right leg (werewolf bite). Her family sold van Richten's son Erasmus to a vampire; he spared her parents; she ran away at 15, found him at 17 (sword at her throat), hunted with him two years; he never fully trusted a Vistana; they parted, at her suggestion, still friends. Bond: he is like a father. Wants to find him, earn his respect and kill Strahd. Has a Tarokka deck and can read it (Ch 1 p. 11). **Knows the door trick** (p. 172). **Mists** ally card (pp. 15–16).
- **Davian Martikov** (pp. 173–174; wereraven, 31 hp, CR 2) — old, suspicious; reveals the gems only after the winery is freed, then sends the party to Berez and Yester Hill; knows nothing of gem 1; estranged from Urwin; late wife Angelika. **Raven** ally card: hands the winery to Adrian and Elvir, reconciles with Urwin first (pp. 15–16).
- **Martikovs** (p. 174) — Adrian (eldest), Elvir (youngest), Stefania, her husband Dag Tomescu (all Keepers); children Claudiu (teen), Martin, Viggo (7 hp), baby Yolanda (1 hp). Urwin runs the Blue Water Inn (Ch 5 N2, p. 98).
- **Druids** — worship Strahd as lord of land and weather; Lysaga has convinced them she bore Strahd (App D p. 228); wilderness druids flee to Yester Hill (Ch 2, p. 30).
- **Wintersplinter** (p. 200; tree blight, CR 7) — the gem sits in its heart until it dies.
- **Kavan** (p. 200) — dead chieftain's spirit offering his spear, preferably to a barbarian, druid or ranger.
- **Kiril Stoyanovich** (werewolf, 90 hp) — brutal leader, loyal to Strahd from fear; no talks while he lives; mate Bianca.
- **Emil Toranescu** (Ch 4, K75a, p. 81; werewolf, 72 hp) — **caged in Castle Ravenloft**; poses as a Vallakian; freed, betrays the party to regain Strahd's favour **unless** they claim Zuleika's friendship.
- **Zuleika Toranescu** (werewolf, 58 hp) — frees the children if Emil returns alive; if he is dead, may free them if the party helps kill Kiril. **Beast** ally card (pp. 15–16).
- **Others** — Bianca, Wensencia, Kellen, Skennis, Aziana, Davanka; Franz Groza (vicious, treacherous) leads if Kiril, Emil and Zuleika all die.
- **Khazan** — archwizard of Ravenloft's building, lich, Strahd's adviser, self-destroyed; K84 crypt 15. Ordered to destroy the Sunsword; his apprentice stole the hilt (App C p. 223).

## Events and triggers
- **Pack Attack (p. 171)** — see Encounters; beating it cancels *Leader of the Pack*.
- **Ezmerelda's Retreat (p. 172)** — back from a failed attack on Strahd on a stolen Vistani horse, 30 hp; annoyed if her wagon is gone; leaves if the tower is gone too unless the party is her best hope. Strahd then hunts her through druids, werewolves, spies and spawn; if she works with the party he sends *Strahd's Invitation* (App F p. 251).
- **Wine Delivery (p. 180)** — Adrian and Elvir drive six barrels to the Blue Water Inn, the Vistani or Krezk; escort, random check per mile, two raven swarms.
- **Wintersplinter Attacks (p. 180)** — leave and return before dealing with it: winery ruined, tracks south to the blight, Martikovs flee to the Blue Water Inn, Vallaki's morale collapses; three days later Lysaga's seven scarecrows hold the vineyard.
- **Druids' Ritual (p. 200)** — Strahd arrives (Beucephalus or bat); druids chant 10 straight rounds; no ritual if the tree is at 0 hp. Wintersplinter marches north on the vineyard; Strahd dismisses the druids and watches the fog-city.
- **Blood Spear of Kavan (p. 200)** — a whisper draws a character to the cairn.
- **Leader of the Pack (p. 205)** — hourly d20, 18+: Kiril, 6 werewolves, 9 wolves return; three circle round via Z8. **With Emil present, he duels Kiril**; winning, he lets the party go but keeps the children unless Zuleika intervenes. Kiril and Emil dead: Zuleika leads, breaks with Strahd; she dead too: Franz Groza.
- **Die Kinder (p. 205)** — Kiril alive hunts the children; a wereraven suggests Krezk and reports to Davian; *calm emotions* or DC 15 Charisma quiets the children; Krezk or the Blue Water Inn shelters them.

## Secrets [DM]
- Rictavio is van Richten. Yan's head shows he killed a Vistana, and the journal page tells of his Vistani curse and the tribe he let zombies eat (App F p. 254). His notes are burned; what he knows is in his head.
- Tower: the dance solution; a dragon if it's done wrong; the third lightning trigger collapses the tower; the wagon is safe only via the trapdoor; the armor answers to "Khazan".
- Winery: secret doors W14 ↔ W15 and W16 ↔ W17; the bedpost key and the lid compartment; twig blights in the cask; poisoned vats; the staff's death-scream.
- Hill: the gem is in the effigy; the druids wait for Strahd; the tree must be uprooted or hallowed; the city is a mirage.
- Den: the Z6 → Z8 tunnel and secret door; Emil is alive in the castle; the hoard is cursed.
- The book never says where the first gem went.

## Player-facing [PLAYER]
- Rictavio may mention "an old tower to the west" (Ch 5 p. 124). At the tower they can see the lake, causeway, wagon and sign, the door symbol and the word "Khazan".
- The winery supplies the taverns and Krezk. Urwin offers free board for bringing back the late wine shipment (Ch 5 N2, p. 98), and Krezk's burgomaster wants wine (Ch 8 S2, p. 145). Once he trusts them, Davian describes the three glowing, pinecone-sized gems.
- Yester Hill: lightning, cairns, the fanged effigy, the bleeding tree with an axe in it, and the white city in the fog. Muriel (Ch 10, U6, p. 165) or any wereraven can guide them. An NPC spellcaster may tell the Whispering Wall legend.
- The den: captured werewolves reveal it (pp. 172, 201). Inside are caged children, Zuleika's prayer, and a doll that looks like a party member.

## Cross-links
- **Tarokka treasure cards (Ch 1, Treasure Locations, pp. 12–14)**: Master of Stars (Wizard) → V7 ("the wizard's name and servant" = "Khazan" and the armor); Three of Coins (Trader) → W10 ("wood and sand"); Five of Glyphs (Druid) → Y4; Five of Swords (Myrmidon) → Z7. Four of Coins (Merchant, an empty cask of the finest wine) → Castle Ravenloft's cellar K63, not the winery. The book's reading places only the Tome, the Holy Symbol and the Sunsword (App C p. 221).
- **Ally cards (Ch 1, Strahd's Enemy, pp. 15–16)**: Artifact = van Richten; Mists = Ezmerelda; Raven = Davian; Beast = Zuleika; Broken One A = the Mad Mage (Ch 2, M, p. 39), who is not referenced in Ch 11, 12, 14 or 15. The chosen ally gains **Inspire** (while in Strahd's sight, gives one PC inspiration), and Strahd tries to kill them quickly.
- **Sunsword (App C p. 223)**: Sergei's sentient CG weapon, now a hilt that forms a sun blade (+2, radiant damage, +1d8 against undead, sunlight). Its purpose is Strahd's destruction, out of revenge for its lost crystal blade. Khazan, the tower's builder, was the one ordered to destroy it.
- **Tome of Strahd (App C p. 221)**: once Strahd knows the party has it, getting it back overrides his other goals, and he targets whoever holds it. Handout: App F p. 252.
- **Khazan's crypt** (Ch 4, K84 crypt 15, p. 89): speaking his name there summons a *staff of power*. It is not the Mad Mage's lost staff.
- **Vallaki (Ch 5)**: Urwin and the Keepers' attic (N2 and N2q, p. 98); Rictavio's wagon and tiger (N5; pp. 123–124); the Vistani camp (N9 and N9i); Blinsky (N7), whose toys turn up at W19 and Z5b.
- **Berez (Ch 10, U3)**: a winery gem powers Lysaga's hut, and she is allied with the druids (App D p. 228).
- **Krezk (Ch 8)**: wine opens the gates (S2, p. 145); villagers never leave the walls for fear of wolves and werewolves (Krezk Lore, p. 146); Krezk takes in rescued children (Ch 15, p. 205).
- **Countryside (Ch 2)**: werewolf random encounters point to the den above Lake Baratok (p. 32).
- **Castle Ravenloft (Ch 4)**: Emil in K75a (p. 81); Beucephalus; Khazan's crypt. **Amber Temple (Ch 13)**: where Khazan became a lich (reader 6b).
