# Appendix C Treasures — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), App C pp. 221–223. Placements come from Ch 1 pp. 11–14 and each chapter's Treasure and Fortunes notes. Item statistics are from the same 5etools data (`items.json`, source CoS). Layer: [BOOK]. Draft.

## At a glance
- App C describes seven special items. The **Tome of Strahd** is not magic. The six magic items are the **Blood Spear, Gulthias Staff, Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, Icon of Ravenloft, Saint Markovia's Thighbone** and the **Sunsword**.
- [DM] The card reading places only three of them: the **Tome** (card 1), the **Holy Symbol** (card 2) and the **Sunsword** (card 3) (App C p. 221; Ch 1 p. 12). Every other item has a fixed location that the characters discover in play. See `tarokka.md` for all 40 possible locations.
- Fixed locations: **Icon** on the chapel altar in Castle Ravenloft (Ch 4, K15, p. 57) · **Thighbone** in the catacombs (Ch 4, K84, crypt 6, p. 86) · **Blood Spear** in a cairn on Yester Hill (Ch 14, "Blood Spear of Kavan", p. 200) · **Gulthias Staff** carried by the druid in the winery (Ch 12, W16, p. 178).

## Items

### Tome of Strahd (p. 221) — not magic, unique; card-placed (card 1)
- Strahd's own old account of how he fell. It has a thick leather cover with steel hinges and clasps, and brittle parchment pages. Most of it is in a private shorthand only Strahd uses, and stains and age have ruined most of it. A few paragraphs can still be read: this is the handout "From the Tome of Strahd" (App F p. 252; see `handouts.md`).
- [DM] If Strahd sees, or hears from a minion, that the party has the Tome, **he drops every other goal until he gets it back**. In a fight, he goes first for **whoever is carrying it** (p. 221).

### Holy Symbol of Ravenkind (p. 222) — legendary; attunement by a good cleric or paladin; card-placed (card 2)
- A platinum amulet shaped like the sun, with a large crystal in the centre. It is sacred to Barovia's good faithful and older than any church in the land. Legend says a giant raven (or an angel in that form) gave it to the paladin **Lugdana**, who used it to destroy nests of vampires. After her death, Ravenloft's high priests wore it.
- 10 charges; regains 1d6 + 4 at dawn.
  - **Hold Vampires** (1 charge, action): vampires and vampire spawn within 30 ft must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom save or be paralysed for 1 minute (they repeat the save at the end of each of their turns).
  - **Turn Undead** (3 charges, used with Turn Undead or Turn the Unholy): undead make their saves at disadvantage.
  - **Sunlight** (5 charges, action): real sunlight, bright 30 ft and dim 30 ft more, for 10 minutes.

### Sunsword (p. 223) — legendary, sentient, attunement; card-placed (card 3)
- History [DM]: this was the blade of Strahd's brother **Sergei**: a platinum hilt and guard with a thin crystal blade. Strahd hired the wizard **Khazan** to destroy it. Khazan separated the hilt from the blade, but while he was destroying the blade his apprentice stole the hilt and ran. The apprentice was later found torn apart in the Svalich Woods, with no hilt. Khazan told Strahd the whole sword had been destroyed.
- Only the **hilt** survives. It knows it can never have its crystal blade back. It now works as a *sun blade*:
  - As a bonus action, it makes a blade of pure radiance appear or disappear. The blade is a finesse longsword; proficiency with shortswords or longswords counts.
  - +2 to attack and damage; deals radiant damage; +1d8 radiant against undead.
  - Sheds **sunlight**, bright 15 ft and dim 15 ft more. As an action, the wielder can widen or narrow it by 5 ft (10–30 ft).
- Sentience: chaotic good; Int 11, Wis 17, Cha 16; hearing and sight 60 ft. It communicates only by passing **emotions** to whoever carries it.
- Personality [DM]: its purpose is to **destroy Strahd**, out of revenge for its lost blade rather than to free Barovia. It secretly fears being destroyed.
- Link: in the Tome excerpt, Strahd says he fears and hates the sword Sergei brought as much as the sun (App F p. 252). At Van Richten's Tower, the wall compartment behind the armour opens with the word **"Khazan"** (Ch 11, V7, p. 171).

### Icon of Ravenloft (p. 222) — legendary; attunement by a good creature; **fixed location, not card-placed**
- A 12-inch statuette of the purest silver showing a cleric kneeling in prayer; it weighs 10 lb. It was a gift to Strahd from the archpriest **Ciril Romulich**, a friend of the family, to consecrate the castle and its chapel.
- **Anyone** within 30 ft is protected against fiends and undead as if by *protection from evil and good* (no attunement needed).
- Attuned: *augury* once per dawn (no components) · used as a holy symbol with Turn Undead or Turn the Unholy, the save DC rises by 2 · once per dawn, as an action, heals one creature within 30 ft for 3d8 + 3 (not undead, constructs or fiends).
- Where: on the altar in the chapel of Castle Ravenloft (Ch 4, K15, p. 57), beside the dead cleric **Gustav Herrenghast**. [DM] An evil creature that touches it while it rests on the altar makes a DC 17 Constitution save, taking 88 (16d10) radiant damage on a failure or half on a success. Once off the altar it is safe for anyone to handle. Gustav's corpse also has a *mace of terror*, a 250 gp cloak and chain mail.

### Saint Markovia's Thighbone (p. 222) — rare; attunement; fixed
- Works as a ***mace of disruption***: +2d6 radiant against fiends and undead. A target left with 25 hp or fewer must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom save or be destroyed (on a success it is frightened until the end of the wielder's next turn). It sheds light, bright 20 ft and dim 20 ft more.
- [DM] If it hits a vampire or vampire spawn even once in a battle, it **crumbles to dust** when that battle ends.
- History: Markovia became a priest of the Morninglord at 18. She challenged Strahd only after he became a vampire, and destroyed every spawn he sent to her abbey. She then attacked Castle Ravenloft, and the fight ranged from the catacombs to the battlements. She never came back, Strahd limped for a long time afterward, and she is said to be trapped in a crypt below the castle.
- Where: Ch 4, K84, **crypt 6**, p. 86. A pressure plate in front of the crypt fires poison darts. The crypt smells of roses, and a faint ghost urges the finders to use her against the vampire.

### Blood Spear (p. 221) — uncommon; attunement; fixed
- The spear of **Kavan**, a mountain chieftain who lived long before Strahd. He had vampire-like habits: he slept by day, drank his prey's blood and lived underground.
- When a melee hit with it drops a target to 0 hp, the wielder gains **2d6 temporary hp**. Anyone can wield it, but only the person **Kavan chooses** gets +2 to attack and damage. (It is a spear: thrown, versatile, 1d6 piercing.)
- Where: Yester Hill (Ch 14). In the special event "Blood Spear of Kavan" (p. 200), Kavan's spirit calls one character, preferably a barbarian, druid or ranger, to a cairn (Y2). The spear lies among his bones under the rocks.

### Gulthias Staff (p. 221) — rare; attunement; fixed
- Spongy black wood from a Gulthias tree. Beasts are visibly uneasy within 30 ft of it. 10 charges; regains 1d6 + 4 at **dusk**. It is a magic quarterstaff.
- **Vampiric Strike:** on a hit, spend 1 charge to regain hp equal to the damage. Blood oozes from the wood, and the wielder must succeed on a DC 12 Wisdom save or gain a short-term madness.
- **Blight Bane:** while attuned, blights and other evil plants don't treat the wielder as hostile unless harmed.
- [DM] **If the staff is broken or burned**, it gives a terrible scream that can be heard 300 ft away, and **every blight that hears it withers and dies**.
- Where: the druid at the Wizard of Wines, **W16** loading winch (Ch 12, p. 178). The siege section of the chapter says that destroying the staff kills all the blights within 300 ft; it is the book's way to end the winery siege (Ch 12, p. 176).

## Other named magic items elsewhere in the book (not in App C; pointers for the chapter readers)
- Shield of the Silver Dragon: a +2 shield with the Order's silver dragon that gives +2 initiative. It is in the Castle Ravenloft **treasury**, inside Daern's instant fortress (Ch 4, K41, pp. 67–68).
- A sentient lawful good +1 shortsword, glowing under water (Ch 4, p. 81). *Mace of terror* on Gustav (K15, p. 57).
- Jakarion's *staff of frost*, which gives its first holder a power-hungry flaw (Ch 13, X17, p. 187). A green copper ewer that turns poison into wine (Ch 13, X22, p. 187). A golden statuette of Saint Markovia, worth 250 gp, +1 to saves for a good creature (Ch 8, p. 152). The Yester Hill battleaxe, extra damage against plants (Ch 14, Y4, p. 198).
- NPC gear listed in App D stat blocks (see `npcs.md`):
  - Rictavio: *hat of disguise*, *ring of mind shielding*, sword cane, *scroll of raise dead*.
  - Ezmerelda: +1 studded leather, +1 rapier, +1 handaxe, silvered shortsword, two potions of greater healing, 6 holy water, 3 stakes.
  - Kasimir: *ring of warmth*, spellbook.
  - Vladimir: +2 greatsword with a silver-dragon hilt and a black opal pommel.

## Secrets [DM]
- The Tome's surviving text hints at the Sunsword, at sunlight, at the stake, and at his lair below the castle.
- The Sunsword wants revenge and fears its own destruction.
- The Gulthias Staff's scream kills blights, including tree blights such as Wintersplinter (tree blight, App D p. 230).
- The Icon hurts evil creatures only while it stands on the K15 altar.
