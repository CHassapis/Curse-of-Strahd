# The Tarokka deck and the card reading — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 1 "Fortunes of Ravenloft" pp. 11–18; App E pp. 243–250; each area's "Fortunes of Ravenloft" note (pages below). Layer: [BOOK]. Draft.

This file was built from the raw data in `book/_src/adventure-cos.json`. All card clues are **paraphrased**, not quoted.

## At a glance
- [DM] Before play, the DM draws five cards. They set where three treasures are (**Tome of Strahd, Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, Sunsword**), who the **ally** ("Strahd's enemy") is, and **where in Castle Ravenloft Strahd waits** for the final fight (Ch 1 p. 11).
- [PLAYER] Madam Eva (Ch 2, area G, pp. 36–37) does the same reading for the party. Ezmerelda d'Avenir can do it too if she has her deck, which is hidden in her wagon (Ch 11, V1, p. 168; App D p. 231). If the reading is done at the table, its results **replace** the DM's earlier draw (Ch 1 p. 11).
- [DM] Only these three treasures are placed by the cards. The Icon of Ravenloft, the Blood Spear, the Gulthias Staff and Saint Markovia's Thighbone have fixed places (App C p. 221; see `treasure.md`).
- [DM] Strahd is **always** at the card-5 location the first time the party gets there, unless he has been forced back into his coffin (Ch 1 p. 17; App D p. 239).

## The deck (App E p. 243)
- 54 cards: a **common deck** of 40 (four suits, 1–9 plus a "master" card) and a **high deck** of 14 crown cards. Every card also has its own name (for example, the 3 of glyphs is the Healer and the master of stars is the Wizard).
- Anyone can make a deck, but only someone of Vistani blood can give it the power of prophecy. When it is not in use it must be wrapped in silk and kept in a wooden box, or it stops working.
- If a high-deck card seems to contradict an earlier card, the Vistani take the high card as the one that counts.
- Suits: **Swords** stand for aggression, warriors and government. **Stars** stand for arcane power, knowledge and reason over faith. **Coins** stand for greed, trade and wealth used well or badly. **Glyphs** stand for faith, spirit, health and betrayal.
- High deck, with the meaning of each (paraphrased): Artifact (an object that must be won, guarded or destroyed) · Beast (rage; something bestial hidden in plain sight) · Broken One (defeat, a loss that leaves one incomplete) · Darklord (a single powerful evil person with far-reaching aims) · Donjon (imprisonment, or being a prisoner of one's own beliefs) · Ghost (the past returns; an old enemy, a buried secret) · Executioner (the coming death of someone convicted, rightly or not) · Horseman (death, ruin, the end of a bloodline) · Innocent (an important being in danger) · Marionette (a spy, puppet or underling) · Mists (the unavoidable unknown; a quest that tests the spirit) · Raven (a hidden source of information; luck; a secret potential for good) · Seer (insight; an outcome that turns on a clever mind) · Tempter (someone led astray, or someone who tempts others).
- Using ordinary playing cards (Ch 1 p. 11): Spades = Swords, Clubs = Stars, Diamonds = Coins, Hearts = Glyphs. The ace is the 1 and the 10 is the master. High deck: K♥ Ghost, Q♥ Innocent, J♥ Marionette, K♠ Darklord, Q♠ Mists, J♠ Executioner, K♦ Broken One, Q♦ Tempter, J♦ Beast, K♣ Donjon, Q♣ Raven, J♣ Seer, Joker 1 Artifact, Joker 2 Horseman.

## The reading procedure (Ch 1 pp. 11–12)
1. Separate the two decks and shuffle each one. Put the top three **common** cards face down in positions 1–3, then the top two **high** cards face down in positions 4–5.
2. Turn the cards over in order. Before each card, the reader says one short line about what it concerns (paraphrased): 1, history and knowing your enemy → **Tome**; 2, a holy force for protection → **Holy Symbol**; 3, a weapon of vengeance made of sunlight → **Sunsword**; 4, someone who will help you greatly → **ally**; 5, the creature of darkness and where to find him → **Strahd**.
3. For each card, read its own clue from the tables below (the book prints each clue as a boxed read-aloud; they are paraphrased here).
- [DM] The three common cards are always different cards, and so are the two high cards. **The card that names the ally can never also be Strahd's location card.**

- **Master: Warrior** — the devil's lair, the one place he must go back to → Strahd's tomb (Ch 4, K86, p. 93). In the centre alcove.

## Card 4: Strahd's enemy, the ally (Ch 1 pp. 15–16)
- [DM] The chosen NPC gains an extra action, **Inspire**: while within sight of Strahd, it gives inspiration to one player character it can see. Strahd senses this NPC is a threat and tries to **kill it as quickly as possible**. For this NPC, the card's text overrides anything the chapters say about them. Some cards have two options (A/B); the DM picks one.
- **Artifact (Joker 1)** — an entertaining man with a monkey, more than he seems → **Rictavio = Rudolph van Richten**, at the Blue Water Inn (Ch 5, N2, p. 100). He is usually reluctant, but **if the party tells him about the reading he drops the disguise** and introduces himself as Van Richten. Red herring: the toymaker Gadof Blinsky (N7) owns a monkey. He jokes about it and turns down any fight, but if told about the reading he admits he got the monkey from Rictavio.
- **Beast (J♦)** — a werewolf who secretly hates your enemy → Zuleika Toranescu (Ch 15, Z7). She joins if they promise to avenge her mate Emil by killing Kiril.
- **Broken One (K♦)** — A: a wizard with a broken mind but strong spells → **the Mad Mage** (Ch 2, M, p. 39). B: a man of faith close to madness, who has lost someone → Donavich (Ch 3, E5). He won't come until his son Doru is dead and buried.
- **Darklord (K♠)** — you face the evil alone → no ally.
- **Donjon (K♣)** — A: a troubled young man whose rich, mad home is his prison → Victor Vallakovich (Ch 5, N3t). He comes eagerly. B: a girl driven mad and locked in her dead father's house → Stella Wachter (Ch 5, N4n). She is no help until her madness is cured; then she joins gladly.
- **Seer (J♣)** — a dusk elf living among the Vistani, grieving and troubled by dark dreams → **Kasimir Velikov** (Ch 5, N9a). He goes to the castle only after they take him to the Amber Temple and help him find a way to bring back **Patrina**.
- **Ghost (K♥)** — A: a fallen paladin of a fallen order, lingering in a dead dragon's lair → Sir Godfrey Gwilym (Ch 7, Q37, p. 139). They need a DC 15 Persuasion check, convincing him the Order's honour can be restored. If the beacon is not yet lit, his choice sets the other revenants fighting. B: the clumsy knight whose crypt is deep in the castle → Sir Klutz, a phantom warrior (Ch 4, K84, crypt 33). As the ally, he lasts until he or Strahd drops to 0 hp, instead of seven days.
- **Executioner (J♠)** — the brother of the devil's bride, called "the lesser" → Ismark (Ch 3, E2). He won't go to the castle until Ireena is safe.
- **Horseman (Joker 2)** — A: a dead nobleman guarded by his widow; bring him back → Nikolai Wachter the elder (Ch 5, N4o). *Raise dead* or *resurrection* on his preserved body makes him an ally (LN noble). **Rictavio gives them a scroll of raise dead** if he learns they need one. B: Arrigal (Ch 5, N9c). He accepts his fate and joins, but **betrays them** once Strahd falls, thinking he is meant to be the next lord.
- **Innocent (Q♥)** — A: a kind, strong, simple young man in Barovia village → Parriwimple (Ch 3, E1). He needs a good-hearted reason, and his employer Bildrath won't let him go. B: the bride of evil → **Ireena** (Ch 3, E4). Once told, she insists on going to the castle, but only after Kolyan is buried. Ismark objects.
- **Marionette (J♥)** — A: a man made by a man, ageless, haunting the towers → Pidlwick II (Ch 4, K59). B: a two-headed musician in a place of hunger and sorrow → Clovin Belview (Ch 8, S17). He comes only if the Abbot is dead, and for wine. He is no use without his viol.
- **Mists (Q♠)** — a lone Vistana searching for her mentor, found at Saint Markovia's abbey near the mists → **Ezmerelda d'Avenir** (Ch 8, S19, and elsewhere).
- **Raven (Q♣)** — the old leader of the feathered ones among the vines, with one fight left in him → Davian Martikov (Ch 12). He leaves the winery to his sons Adrian and Elvir, but first insists on making peace with his son Urwin (Ch 5, N2).
- **Tempter (Q♦)** — A: a Vistana child whose fate hangs in the balance, at the lake → Arabelle (Ch 2, L). She joins gladly, but if she gets back to camp her father Luvash won't let her go. B: a wedding bell or a death knell at a mountain abbey, a woman more than the sum of her parts → Vasilka (Ch 8, S13).
- **Madam Eva is not on any card.** By the book she can never be the ally.

## Secrets [DM]
- Eva's reading is real, but her aims are her own: she is Strahd's half-sister and wants him freed from his curse, or given a successor (App D pp. 233–234).
- Strahd's location card and the ally card come from the same 14 cards, so they can never be the same card.
- Rictavio turns up in three cards: the Artifact ally, the Illusionist treasure (his wagon), and the Horseman A option (his raise dead scroll).

## Player-facing [PLAYER]
- Each card's clue as Eva says it (paraphrase it at the table, or read it from the book).
- Rumour-level: the Vistani read the future in cards. A deck only works in Vistani hands, and it is kept wrapped in silk in a wooden box.
