## Handouts

### 1. Kolyan Indirovich's Letter, version 1 (p. 251)
- **Who and when:** In the "Plea for Help" hook, a brightly dressed Vistana walks into a foggy tavern, drops a sealed letter on the party's table, buys the whole room a drink and leaves. He tells them to travel west through the Svalich Woods at first light (Ch 1 p. 18). The messenger is **Arrigal** (NE assassin). He claims to serve the burgomaster but really serves Strahd. The seal is **Strahd's crest**; the book says to show the players the crest too.
- **Reveals [PLAYER]:** an old-fashioned plea from "Kolyan Indirovich, Burgomaster". His beloved Ireena Kolyana suffers from an evil the village can't fight, and he promises all the wealth he has if heroes come quickly.
- **[DM]:** Strahd wrote it as bait. The real Kolyan is Ireena's adoptive father. Taking the bait brings the party to Ch 2, area A.

### 2. Strahd's Invitation (p. 251)
- **Reveals [PLAYER]:** a short, courteous note signed by Strahd. He admits he brought them to Barovia and that only he can let them go, invites them to dine at his castle, and promises them safe passage.
- **Triggers:**
  - (a) Ch 5, St Andral's Feast, "Development" (p. 124): if the party saves the church and protects Father Lucian, Strahd writes the letter at Wachterhaus. Lady Wachter has her spy **Ernst Larnak** or one of her sons deliver it. On the way to the castle there are **no threatening random encounters**.
  - (b) Ch 8, "Development" (p. 156): once Sergei and Ireena are united at the pool, Strahd blames the party and sends a servant with the invitation.
  - (c) Ch 11, "Development" (p. 172): if Strahd learns that Ezmerelda is with the party, a spy delivers an invitation, because he expects her to come along.
- **[DM]:** Ch 4 has extra text for arriving at the castle "by invitation" (see the Ch 4 notes).

### 3. From the Tome of Strahd (p. 252)
- **Who and when:** whoever finds the Tome (a card-1 treasure) and reads the few paragraphs still legible (App C p. 221).
- **Reveals [PLAYER]:** Strahd's own story, told with self-pity and anger:
  - He was a warrior of a just cause who grew old in endless wars, conquered the valley, and brought his family to Castle Ravenloft, including his handsome younger brother **Sergei**, whom he hated for his youth.
  - He loved **Tatyana**, but she chose Sergei and saw only death and old age in Strahd. He made a **pact with death, sealed with Sergei's blood**, killing him on the wedding day.
  - Tatyana ran from him and threw herself from the castle walls into the mist. The guards' arrows struck him, and he became undead.
  - He calls himself "vampyr". He fears the **sun** most of all. A **stake** holds him still but does not kill him. He hates and fears **the sword Sergei brought**.
  - He keeps hunting for Tatyana, who seems to slip away from him again and again.
  - He now lives far below the castle among the dead, and has sealed the stairway walls so no one disturbs him.
- **[DM]:** the text points at the Sunsword, sunlight, the stake, Tatyana's rebirths (Ireena), and his tomb in the catacombs (K84–K86). It also tells the party what Strahd did at the chapel.

### 4. Journal of Rudolph van Richten (p. 254)
- **Who and when:** a charred page among the things in Ezmerelda's wagon at Van Richten's Tower (Ch 11, V1, p. 168). Van Richten burned his other journals in V7 (p. 171).
- **Reveals [PLAYER]:**
  - He has hunted creatures of darkness for over thirty years. He calls himself a man who has lived too long and has lost many friends.
  - He now believes a **Vistani curse** is to blame: it falls on the people around him, not on him.
  - He finally tells the whole story of his son **Erasmus**. The Vistani brought him a dying member of their tribe; he could not save the young man, and in fear begged them to take anything of his instead. They secretly took his son.
  - Following them into the woods at night, he was saved by a **lich**. It warded him against undead and made a dead Vistana tell him where the tribe was.
  - When the tribe said the boy had been sold to the vampire **Baron Metus**, he let the zombies following him **devour the whole tribe**. Their dying leader cursed him that everyone he loves would die by monsters' claws.
  - He later found Erasmus a vampire and ended him at the boy's own plea.
- **[DM]:** this explains why he works alone (App D p. 238). It also sits badly next to App D p. 230, where Van Richten **spares** Ezmerelda's family after the kidnapping. Treat it as a secret he has kept from her.

### 5. Kolyan Indirovich's Letter, version 2 (p. 256)
- **Who and when:** the body of **Dalvan Olensky**, killed by Strahd's dire wolves while fleeing Barovia, lies near the road in the Svalich Woods (Ch 2, area C, p. 34). The letter has a "B" in its wax seal and is dated a week earlier.
- **Reveals [PLAYER]:** the real burgomaster writes in despair. His adopted daughter Ireena has been bitten by a vampire that has preyed on Barovia for over 400 years and is too strong to beat. He begs outsiders to give the village up for dead and to ring the land with holy symbols to keep the evil inside. With bitter irony, he says any reward can be collected after they are all gone.
- **[DM]:** Dalvan was told to leave it at the gates so that visitors would turn back. It contrasts with version 1, which Strahd wrote.

### 6. Journal of Argynvost (p. 256)
- **Who and when:** the torn last page of the silver dragon's journal, in his study at Argynvostholt (Ch 7, Q40, "Journal Page", p. 140). The rest of the journal was destroyed.
- **Reveals [PLAYER]:** Argynvost's last words before his final battle. His knights are dead and the land is lost; he will throw off his human disguise and fight as a dragon. He regrets that his remains won't rest in Argynvostholt's mausoleum and expects his bones to be scattered as trophies. He hopes his spirit will live on as a **beacon of light** and hope. It is signed "A".
- **[DM]:** this points to the mausoleum (Q16) and the beacon (Q53), and to recovering his remains (see the Ch 7 and Ch 4 notes).
