# Krezk, Tsolenka Pass and Berez — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 8 The Village of Krezk (pp. 143–156), Ch 9 Tsolenka Pass (pp. 157–160), Ch 10 The Ruins of Berez (pp. 161–166); NPC lore from App D (pp. 225–234); Tarokka placements from Ch 1 (pp. 12–16). CR/XP from the 2014 stat blocks via 5e.tools (GitHub data mirror, https://github.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-src). Layer: [BOOK]. Draft.

## At a glance
- **Krezk (Ch 8, area S)** — walled, self-sufficient commune at the far west of the valley; the mist wall shows above the trees. Its only outside need is wine from the Wizard of Wines. Burgomaster Dmitri Krezkov. Book level 5th (Intro, Character Levels, p. 6).
- **Abbey of Saint Markovia (S6–S24)** — 400 ft above the village. It was once a convent and hospital; now it is a madhouse run by **the Abbot**, a fallen deva (CR 10). He built **Vasilka**, a flesh-golem bride for Strahd, and turned the Belview family into mongrelfolk.
- **Blessed pool / Shrine of the White Sun (S4)** — "Something Blue": Ireena can join Sergei's spirit here. That puts her beyond Strahd's reach and brings his lightning down on the pool.
- Ch 8 has **4 Tarokka treasure spots** (S4, S9, S13, S23) and **3 Tarokka ally options** (Ezmerelda, Clovin, Vasilka).
- **Tsolenka Pass (Ch 9, area T)** — a 7-mile mountain road to a black gatehouse (vrock statues, green flame), a white guard tower and a bridge over the Luna gorge. It leads to the Amber Temple. Book level 8th. Events: the roc, and Sangzor the goat.
- **Berez (Ch 10, area U)** — a flooded marsh ruin. Strahd once courted Marina here, an earlier look-alike of Tatyana. It is now the home of **Baba Lysaga** (CR 11), who believes she is Strahd's "true mother". Her **creeping hut** (CR 11) is animated by a gem stolen from the Wizard of Wines. Also here: 7 scarecrows and the ghost of the burgomaster. Book level 8th.

## Areas

### Krezk — Map 8.1 (S1–S5)
- **S1 Road Junction (p. 143)** — the Old Svalich Road runs on west for about a mile into the mists; the branch north climbs to the gate. [PLAYER] A 20-ft buttressed wall, snowy pines, chimney smoke, a bell tolling from the cliff-top abbey, and a switchback road up to it.
- **S2 Gatehouse (p. 145)** — two towers. The 12-ft ironbound doors are barred inside: the bar lifts with a DC 15 Str check, and breaking the doors needs a siege engine. **2 scouts** stand at the tower arrow slits, **4 guards** on the adjoining walls, and elsewhere one lone guard per 300 ft of wall.
  - [DM] If anyone is seen climbing or flying over, the alarm goes up. After 5 rounds every able adult arrives: the militia is **4 more guards + 40 commoners with handaxes**.
  - **Ways in:** ask, and a guard fetches Dmitri, who refuses strangers until they help Krezk. He asks for the overdue wagonload of wine from the Wizard of Wines, and Ch 12 (p. 180) confirms that wine buys entry. By force or magic: Dmitri orders his guards to stand down and hurries the party on their way.
- **S3 Village (p. 145)** — pine cottages in a planted forest. It is a commune: no money economy, no inn or tavern, no horses. The party can sleep in the burgomaster's or another cottage in exchange for chores.
  - *Burgomaster's cottage:* nearest the gate. The wine cellar is empty. The family graveyard behind holds all four Krezkov children; several older caskets have been robbed. Ilya's grave is fresh (4 days) and untouched.
  - *Commoner cottage:* 200 sq ft; 1d4 adults, 1d4−1 children, with pigs, hares and chickens kept indoors. Each has its own graveyard, and every casket from the last ten years is empty (taken by the mongrelfolk gravediggers).
  - *Krezk lore (p. 146)* — see Player-facing.
- **S4 Pool and Shrine (p. 146)** — a spring-fed pool blessed by Saint Markovia. The water resists corruption, and the **first drink grants *lesser restoration*** (its magic has faded over time). The gazebo is near collapse and holds a faded wooden Morninglord statue with arms reaching east; locals call it the Shrine of the White Sun without knowing why.
  - [DM] **Tarokka (Three of Glyphs — Healer):** the item is under the gazebo, which must be torn down. If it is damaged and not repaired, Charisma checks to shift villagers' attitudes have disadvantage.
- **S5 Winding Road (p. 147)** — a 10-ft switchback of loose gravel that climbs 400 ft.

### Abbey of Saint Markovia — Map 8.2 (ground floor, S5–S15) and Map 8.3 (upper floor and cellar, S16–S24)
**General (p. 147).** Every mongrelfolk is a Belview and every one is mad; for an unnamed one, roll or choose from the DMG Indefinite Madness table. Most are locked up. Only Otto and Zygfrek (gravediggers) and Clovin (manservant) move about freely. Clovin rings the bell when the Abbot calls dinner, and every mongrelfolk starts howling. The north wing has translucent leaded windows; the east wing's windows are broken outward.
- **S6 North Gate (p. 147)** — two stone huts, a 5-ft rubble wall and unlocked iron gates that squeal. **Otto and Zygfrek Belview** (LE mongrelfolk) sleep under furs.
  - [DM] A DC 12 Stealth check lets the party climb the wall quietly. Opening the gates, or failing the check, wakes them.
  - A friendly party is escorted to the courtyard (S12) to wait for the Abbot. A hostile party is let in but not escorted.
  - Each hut holds a twig-and-needle net and a shovel: their grave-robbing kit.
- **S7 Graveyard (p. 148)** — stunted pines, gravestones of priests and nuns, and a 400-ft view down to the village.
  - [DM] **Sun's Grave (marked X):** a rose-carved stone with a sun-shaped hollow and the name Petrovna. Setting Tasha Petrovna's holy symbol (Ch 4, K84 crypt 11) in the hollow makes both vanish; a sunbeam shines for 1 minute and the stone crumbles to reveal a ***ring of regeneration***. Smashing the stone without the symbol yields nothing.
- **S8 Garden Gatehouse (p. 148)** — empty.
- **S9 Gardens (p. 148)** — four walled plots, white rabbits and two scarecrows on crosses (harmless). The door to S15 is unlocked.
  - [DM] **Tarokka (Two of Glyphs — Missionary):** the item is in the southernmost scarecrow's stuffed gullet. Taking it out makes **7 wights** in Strahd's house livery burst from the soil.
- **S10 Abbey Entrance (p. 148)** — a 15-ft curtain wall; the "guards" on it are propped-up scarecrows (DC 10 Perception to tell). A copper plaque gives the abbey's name and a prayer that her light cure illness. The heavy doors are unlocked.
- **S11 Inner Gatehouses (p. 148)** — empty and unlocked.
- **S12 Courtyard (p. 150)** — fog, a well, troughs, padlocked sheds and tethering posts; screams come from the sheds.
  - S12a Well (80 ft deep): **Mishka Belview** (CE, Spider Climb) clings 20 ft down and attacks anyone who shines a light down. He enjoys killing.
  - S12b: the rotten troughs fall apart when touched.
  - S12c: 9 sheds, each with a shackled **mongrelfolk**. Clovin has the keys.
  - S12d: **Marzena Belview** (CN, bat wings, Flight), Mishka's older sister, is chained to a post. She is paranoid and lets only Clovin near; if freed she flies off for good.
- **S13 Main Hall (p. 150)** — a 50-ft hall with a cauldron of turnip-and-rabbit soup (the mongrelfolk's food), a **golden sun disk** over the fireplace, a long table with gold candelabras, and stairs up (S17) and down (S16).
  - **Vasilka** stands in a torn red gown. The **Abbot** is usually here teaching her etiquette; dancing comes next.
  - [DM] Within 5 ft, the seams in Vasilka's skin show.
  - **Treasure:** the disk (750 gp); behind it a niche holding a ***potion of healing (superior)*** in a crystal-and-electrum flask (250 gp); 4 gold candelabras (250 gp each).
  - **Tarokka (One of Glyphs — Monk):** the item is in the niche with the potion.
- **S14 Foyer (p. 151)** — a wrecked office with stairs up to S20. Noise or light here draws the S15 golem.
- **S15 Madhouse (p. 151)** — a dark corridor patrolled by the Abbot's first, cruder **flesh golem**, which attacks anyone not accompanied by the Abbot or Clovin. The east door opens onto S9.
  - **60 mongrelfolk** stay in unlocked rooms, too afraid of the golem or of being cast out to leave. Each has a dagger and a soup bowl.
  - S15a: 3 cowering; one cradles a brass candlestick like a doll and fights if it is taken.
  - S15b: 5, four of them brawling for dominance, behind a bite-marked wooden statue of Saint Markovia (just over 5 ft).
  - S15c: 7 chanting gibberish to "magically" ring the dinner bell.
  - S15d: 9 left unfed by Clovin; they try to kill and eat anyone who enters.
  - S15e: 16 in filth among gnawed bones, begging for food.
  - S15f: 8 dancing to a rhyme (the devil will drink her blood, then have to kill her). Their leader holds a **gold statuette of Saint Markovia** (250 gp; a good-aligned carrier gains +1 to saving throws). They weep if it is taken.
  - S15g: 10, three of them tending noncombatant mongrelfolk babies.
  - S15h: 2 in a fort of broken furniture (cover); they come out only for food.
- **S16 Wine Cellar (p. 152)** — Wizard of Wines barrels (the central ones are empty) and 33 Purple Grapemash No. 3 and 24 Red Dragon Crush bottles. [DM] An unstoppered "Champagne du le Stomp" bottle holds a ***spell scroll of heroes' feast***.
- **S17 Loft and Belfry (p. 152)** — the golem workshop (tools on a side table), a bronze bell 30 ft up, a shrouded table and a cot. **Clovin Belview** (NE, two-headed) plays the viol beautifully when drunk. Ringing the bell sets every mongrelfolk shouting for food until it is fed. Three bottles are hidden under his furs.
  - [DM] Teleport destination from Castle Ravenloft K78 (indigo), at point **T** on the map.
  - **Thing on the Table (p. 154):** Strahd's will makes the body under the shroud look stitched from the characters' own parts. Touching it shows the truth: leftover parts of dead women from Krezk's graves.
- **S18 Curtain Wall (p. 154)** — scarecrow sentries on stands. The drop into the courtyard is 15 ft; over the southwest wall it is a 400-ft fall.
- **S19 Barracks (p. 154)** — rotted bunks. **Ezmerelda d'Avenir** is here if not met elsewhere, as the Abbot's guest. She knows he is insane and is waiting for Strahd to visit the "bride" so she can destroy him away from his castle; she plans a magic circle. She joins a party committed to fighting Strahd.
- **S20 Upstairs Office (p. 154)** — a rotten counter and nothing else; the madhouse can be heard below.
- **S21 Haunted Hospital (p. 154)** — iron bed frames and doors to the Operating Room, Nursery and Morgue. [DM] **6 shadows** attack once characters are 10 ft inside; they can't leave the room.
- **S22 Operating Room (p. 155)** — a bloodstained table. The first touch sets off echoing screams (harmless).
- **S23 Nursery (p. 155)** — broken cribs. One random searcher sees a nun in white reflected in the window, once only.
  - [DM] **Tarokka (Two of Coins — Philanthropist):** under a crib.
- **S24 Morgue (p. 155)** — a raven on the sill flies to a garden scarecrow when approached.
  - [DM] Killing it curses the killer: disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until *greater restoration*, *remove curse* or similar.

### Tsolenka Pass — Map 9.1 (T1–T9)
**The road (p. 157):** 7 miles from the Raven River crossroads (Ch 2, area R), with wind and snow. Characters without cold-weather protection suffer extreme cold at night. The stonework can't be climbed without magic or a climber's kit.
- **T1 Gatehouse Portcullis (p. 157)** — a 30-ft black gatehouse with spiked 20-ft walls, topped by statues of horned vultures. The portcullis rises by itself when someone comes within 10 ft and stays open 1 minute. Flying or climbing over wakes T2.
- **T2 Demon Statues (p. 157)** [DM] — two petrified **vrocks**. If attacked or bypassed they turn to flesh, attack, pursue and fight to the death.
- **T3 Curtain of Green Flame (p. 157)** — fills the east arch. It deals **33 (6d10) fire** on first entering in a turn or starting a turn inside. *Dispel magic* (DC 16) suppresses it for 1 minute, as does an antimagic field.
- **T4 Guard Tower, ground floor (p. 157)** — the ironbound door is barred inside (DC 22 Athletics). Inside: a cold hearth and stairs. [DM] Teleport destination from K78 (violet), at point **X**.
- **T5 Upper floor (p. 159)** — freezing, with a dire wolf head over the hearth and a ladder to a trapdoor.
- **T6 Rooftop (p. 159)** — 10-ft gold-plated statues of female lancers. Under the snow lie 4 guard skeletons with ruined gear. The roof is 40 ft up and 540 ft above the valley.
  - [DM] **Tarokka (Three of Swords — Soldier):** **6 snow maidens** appear (specters, immune to cold, with a cold Life Drain). They warn the party off, then attack. The item appears when the last one falls.
- **T7 Western Arch (p. 159)** — a 30-ft arch with charging-knight statues and two empty guard posts (shelter from the wind).
- **T8 Stone Bridge (p. 159)** — 10 ft × 90 ft, 500 ft above the Luna River; safe despite slick patches. A **black-cloaked rider on a charcoal horse** blocks the middle: a manifestation of Strahd that scatters like ash at any interaction.
- **T9 Eastern Arch (p. 159)** — one statue has crumbled; two empty guard posts. **Beyond:** the pass runs 3 miles, then forks. North goes to the **Amber Temple** (Ch 13); south wraps round Mount Ghakis to the deadly mists.

### Berez — Map 10.1 (U1–U6) and Map 10.2 (the hut)
**Approach (p. 161).**
- The book assumes the party comes from the north along the river trail. Cottages are sunk in marsh, flies are everywhere, and a lantern flashes from the standing stones across the river (Muriel).
- The river is at most 10 ft deep. Fog limits sight to 120 ft in the village. Surviving road is normal terrain; the marsh is difficult terrain.
- **Any rest in the marsh**, even barricaded, draws 1d4 swarms of flies (swarm of insects (wasps)). They don't bother anyone in U3 or U5.

**Marsh scarecrows (p. 162):** **7 scarecrows** stuffed with raven feathers look ordinary until one is attacked, Lysaga commands them, or the skull fence howls.
- **U1 Abandoned Cottages (p. 162)** — rotted and empty, with 3-ft walls.
- **U2 Ulrich Mansion (p. 162)** — rubble on higher ground, with a goat pen to the east and a wild garden to the south.
  - **Ghost of Lazlo Ulrich** (NG) appears if the ruin is searched and begs the party to leave. Strahd denies him rest for killing Marina. He tells her story if asked.
  - He finds peace only if convinced that **Marina is reborn as Ireena**, and he must see her in person; he can't leave the ruin. He attacks if threatened or if the party hunts for treasure. At 0 HP he re-forms in 24 h. XP is awarded only for laying him to rest.
  - Tarokka: if a Berez treasure lies at U5, he sends them 200 paces west to "a monument to my folly" as he fades.
  - *Cellar:* under rubble (4 hours for one person, shared among helpers). A 30-ft room under 3 in of water holds 24 empty Champagne du le Stomp casks.
  - *Garden:* overgrown nude statues and benches. **4 giant poisonous snakes** attack anyone more than 10 ft in.
  - *Goat pen:* 9 goats for her longevity rites and **50 human skulls** on the posts. There is no gate; Lysaga flies in by skull. Damaging the fence makes the skulls **howl for 1 minute**. Lysaga then arrives in her skull on initiative 20, **2 rounds later**, and **all 7 scarecrows** come too (one initiative roll for them all).
- **U3 Baba Lysaga's Hut (p. 163)** — the hut stands on a giant stump whose roots look like spider legs. An upside-down hollow giant's skull floats beneath the door, and two iron cages of ravens hang from the eaves.
  - Lysaga is inside unless something has drawn her out. The ravens' racket hides the party's approach; only the howling skulls or nearby combat carry over it.
  - Cages: *arcane lock*. Open with *knock*, a DC 20 Str check or DC 20 thieves' tools. Freed, each **swarm of ravens** attacks Lysaga and her scarecrows.
  - **Giant skull:** a hill giant's skull made into a vehicle. Only Lysaga can fly it, from inside. Fly 40 ft; holds one Medium creature, who has cover. AC 15, 50 HP, immune to poison and psychic damage.
  - **Interior (15 ft square):** bolted-down cot, wicker cabinet (spell components), wardrobe (soiled robes), table, stool, brass-banded chest and a blood-stained iron tub. A crib holds an angelic child: a *programmed illusion* she calls "Strahd". Green light seeps up under the crib.
  - [DM] **The gem:** it sits in a 3-ft cavity under the floorboards. Breaking the floor takes DC 14 Str or 10 damage. While the hut lives, the cavity grows teeth: DC 20 Dex save to grab the gem; on a failure the grabber takes 10 (3d6) piercing and gets nothing (App D p. 226). Removing or destroying the gem **incapacitates the hut**.
  - The tub is for her **new-moon blood bath**; a party that arrives unseen at the right time can catch her in it.
  - [DM] **Chest:** a *glyph of warding* (DC 17 Investigation to find; 5d8 thunder), then **4 crawling claws** inside. Loot: see Treasure.
  - **Tarokka (Nine of Stars — Conjurer):** the item is in the chest.
- **U4 Churchyard (p. 164)** — a gutted church and a half-sunk cemetery with a fallen bell. Nothing of value.
- **U5 Marina's Monument (p. 164)** — raised by Strahd: a fenced plot with a life-size kneeling peasant girl holding a rose, who **looks like Ireena**. The epitaph names Marina, taken by the mists.
  - Hidden: found through Ulrich, or by searching the right map square for 10 minutes and making DC 15 Perception (repeatable).
  - [DM] **Tarokka (Three of Stars — Enchanter):** in a cavity under it (DC 15 Str to move).
  - Disturbing it raises **7 bloated corpses** 60 ft away (commoners with speed 20, immune to charmed and frightened). Each bursts at 0 HP into a **swarm of poisonous snakes**. The party can easily outrun them.
- **U6 Standing Stones (p. 165)** — 12 menhirs (15–18 ft) in a 100-ft ring. Four tall cardinal stones; the other eight bear animal glyphs (bear, elk, hawk, goat, owl, panther, raven, wolf).
  - The ring is older than the Amber Temple and made by the same people as the megaliths near Old Bonegrinder (DC 10 Int to notice).
  - The stones are nonmagical, but **nothing inside the ring can be targeted by divination or scrying**. Druids sense old power, and **Wild Shape inside gives maximum HP**.
  - The Yester Hill druids visit now and then. **Muriel Vinshaw** waits here.

## NPCs
- **Dmitri Krezkov** (LG human **noble**; S2/S3, p. 145) — burgomaster; his forebears founded Krezk after Strahd's conquest.
  - A proud lord who puts the village before strangers and treats adventurers as trouble either way. Once they help Krezk, his oath and honour require hospitality.
  - DC 12 Insight shows he is hiding grief for **Ilya**.
  - Possible fate: hangs himself after the mongrelfolk raid ("Something Borrowed"). His 5etools portrait is captioned "Baron Krezkov".
- **Anna Krezkova** (LG **noble**, "fearless") — supervises births when no priest is available. She would lead the gown expedition to Vallaki (2 guards, 4 commoners, a mule), which is lost unless the party escorts it.
- **Ilya Krezkov** — died of illness at 14, seven days before the party arrives; the last of four children. His death ends the bloodline, which dismays the whole village. If the Abbot raises him, he returns at 1 HP with an indefinite madness (Ch 2: anyone dead 24+ hours who returns gains one).
- **The Abbot** (App D p. 225; *deva*, LE, CR 10, 5,900 XP) — the oldest being in Barovia.
  - **Stats:** AC 17, 136 HP, fly 90. Two mace attacks (+8; 1d6+4 plus 4d8 radiant). *Healing Touch* 3/day (4d8+2, cures curse, disease, poison, blindness, deafness). *Change Shape*. Innate *detect evil and good* at will; *commune* and *raise dead* 1/day each. Magic resistance; resists radiant and nonmagical weapon damage.
  - **Looks:** a handsome priest in his late twenties, in a brown robe, wearing a painted wooden sun symbol of the Morninglord.
  - **Backstory:** sent from the Upper Planes over 100 years ago to honour Markovia. He cured the leprous Belviews' disease, then gave in to their wish for animal traits. Amber Temple lore supplied by "Vasili von Holtz" (Strahd in disguise) made that possible. He now believes a bride for Strahd will cure Strahd and lift the curse.
  - **Attitude:** means the party no harm and won't thwart Strahd's plans for them. If they threaten him or Vasilka, he takes angelic form to make them back down. He gives friendly visitors a tour.
  - **Offer:** for help finding a bridal gown, he will cast ***raise dead* up to three times** for them, or give each of them his **healing touch**. If they refuse or are rude, he orders them out, attacks if they stay, and protects Vasilka.
  - **Traits:** his Ideal is to cure the land by granting the devil his heart's desire; his Bond is love for his golems and mongrelfolk; his Flaw is believing himself incorruptible. He regrets the mongrelfolk and thinks locking them up necessary. Strahd doesn't want Vasilka; he enjoys corrupting an angel.
- **Vasilka** (CoS block, CR 5, 1,800 XP) — the flesh-golem bride.
  - **Stats:** AC 9, 93 HP. Immune to lightning, poison and nonmagical weapon damage; lightning heals her. May go berserk at 40 HP or less; the Abbot can calm her (DC 15 Persuasion).
  - She is mute, obeys the Abbot and screams when hurt. Tarokka ally option (Tempter B, Ch 1 p. 16).
- **Flesh golem (S15)** — the Abbot's first attempt (CR 5).
- **Clovin Belview** (NE two-headed mongrelfolk, CR 1/4; S17, pp. 152–153) — the Abbot's drunken, loyal manservant and a fine viol player.
  - He has the shed keys. The other mongrelfolk hate him for hoarding food; he would let them starve, but the Abbot forbids it.
  - Tarokka ally option (Marionette B, Ch 1 p. 15): he won't leave while the Abbot lives, but after the Abbot's death he will join for wine. He is no use without his viol.
- **Otto Belview** (LE; Standing Leap; thinks himself the finest person he knows) and **Zygfrek Belview** (LE; Darkvision; feels constantly judged) — the gate guards and grave robbers.
- **Mishka Belview** (CE; Spider Climb) — lives in the well. **Marzena Belview** (CN; Flight; believes she is hunted) — chained in the courtyard.
- **Mongrelfolk** (App D p. 234; CR 1/4) — they mimic sounds and use camouflage (advantage on Stealth to hide). *Greater restoration* restores those made by magic but not those born mongrelfolk.
- **Ezmerelda d'Avenir** (CR 8; S19) — see S19. Tarokka ally option (Mists, Ch 1 p. 15), with the abbey named as the place to find her.
- **Dimira Yolensky** (LG noncombatant) and **Kretyana Dolvof** (LG commoner, midwife) — "Something New".
- **Baroness Lydia Petrovna** (Vallaki, Ch 5 N3p, p. 108) — owns a white bridal gown and would give it up, but the Baron forbids it.
- **Sergei's spirit** — appears in the pool, for Ireena only.
- **Roc of Mount Ghakis** (CR 11, 7,200 XP) — thousands of years old. It nests on the summit of Mount Ghakis to the southeast and eats fish from the lake. AC 15, 248 HP; beak 4d8+9, talons 4d6+9 with a grapple (escape DC 19).
- **Sangzor, "Bloodhorn"** — a 9-ft giant goat known to the druids and berserkers: Int 6, CE, 33 HP, resists nonmagical weapon damage, CR 1 (200 XP).
- **Baba Lysaga** (App D pp. 228–229; CoS block CR 11, 7,200 XP) — the book's prose calls her a **hag**, but **her stat block is a human** (shapechanger).
  - **Stats:** AC 15, 120 HP. 16th-level wizard, save DC 17: *fireball*, *lightning bolt*, *Evard's black tentacles*, *polymorph*, *cloudkill*, *scrying*, *programmed illusion*, *true seeing*, *finger of death*, *mirage arcane*, *power word stun*. Three quarterstaff attacks. Can become a swarm of flies. Summons 1d4 swarms of insects once per rest. Nondetection (Mother Night's blessing); skin tough as stone.
  - **Backstory:** Queen Ravenovia's midwife. She believes she is Strahd's true mother and says she put the spark of magic in him. Banished for her unhealthy attachment to the infant, she prayed the queen to death. She moved near Strahd but has never faced him, fearing rejection.
  - **Wereraven war:** a coven of witches at Castle Ravenloft told her about the Keepers of the Feather, and she now wages war on the Wizard of Wines. She has allied with the Yester Hill druids by claiming she bore their god Strahd.
  - Must bathe in beast blood every new moon or crumble to dust.
  - Ideal: a mother's love. Bond: she is Strahd's mother. Flaw: she won't rest until her son's enemies are dead.
- **Lazlo Ulrich (ghost)** (NG ghost, CR 4) — see U2.
- **Marina** (dead) — a Berez peasant who resembled Tatyana. Strahd fed on her. Ulrich and Brother Grigor killed her to save her soul, so Strahd killed them both and flooded Berez (p. 161).
- **Muriel Vinshaw** (wereraven, CR 2; U6) — a Vallaki resident and friend of the Martikovs who spies on Lysaga.
  - She shares her information (see Player-facing), hides her nature and won't name other wereravens.
  - She won't join an attack on Lysaga and flees combat. She can point the party to Argynvostholt and Yester Hill, and she avoids the druids.

**Holy Symbol of Ravenkind** (App C p. 222): legendary; attunement by a good-aligned cleric or paladin. A platinum sun amulet with a crystal at its centre, said to have been brought to the paladin Lugdana by a giant raven (or an angel in that form).
- 10 charges; regains 1d6+4 at dawn.
- *Hold Vampires* (1 charge): vampires and spawn within 30 ft make a DC 15 Wis save or are paralysed for 1 minute.
- *Turn Undead* boost (3 charges).
- *Sunlight* (5 charges): 30-ft radius for 10 minutes.
- Finding it grants a level under the book's milestone option (Intro p. 6).

## Events and triggers
- **Something Old (p. 155)** — happens if the party doesn't raise Ilya themselves.
  - The Abbot, in human guise, knocks at the burgomaster's cottage and says the gods of light want the bloodline restored.
  - Dmitri digs Ilya up, and the Abbot casts *raise dead* without components. Ilya returns at 1 HP with an indefinite madness.
  - Anna praises the Abbot and Saint Markovia; Dmitri feels he has misjudged him and is now in his debt. The Abbot uses this as leverage for "Something Borrowed".
- **Something New (p. 155)** — Dimira's baby is born healthy but silent. A DC 10 Insight check shows the midwife is troubled; she confides that the child has no soul. She believes, rightly, that most Barovians lack souls.
- **Something Borrowed (pp. 155–156)** — the Abbot orders Dmitri to find a bridal gown **within a month**, as payment for Ilya or on pain of death.
  - Anna sets out for Vallaki, and Dmitri asks the party to escort her (normal random encounters on the road). She needs the Vallaki gate guards' permission to enter. Dressmakers charge 50 gp but would be too slow, and Anna can't pay; they point to **Lydia Petrovna's white gown** (Ch 5 N3p), which the Baron won't release.
  - **Without an escort:** the expedition is lost, and so is the search party. Dmitri pleads with the Abbot, who promises "divine retribution". The **next evening** the Abbot releases the whole madhouse on the village: they strip it of food but harm no one, and 2d6 mongrelfolk die. **A few days later Dmitri hangs himself.**
  - **To stop it:** deliver the gown, stop the mongrelfolk on the road down, or kill the Abbot beforehand.
  - **Outcome:** a real gown means the Abbot honours his deal. An illusory gown turns him hostile once found out.
- **Something Blue (p. 156)** — only if the party brings Ireena to Krezk, as Donavich suggested (Ch 3, E5f).
  - A gentle voice draws Ireena to the pool (S4). Sergei's image appears; she recognises her betrothed prince, and a hand of water rises to take hers.
  - **If the party pulls her away:** the image fades and she weeps his name.
  - **If they let her go:** she is drawn under, embraces Sergei, and both fade. She is **safe where Strahd can't reach her**.
  - **Either way, Strahd senses it:** thunder, a face in the clouds claiming her, and **blue lightning** strikes the pool.
    - Within 15 ft: DC 17 Dex check or fall prone.
    - In the water: DC 17 Con save, **44 (8d10) lightning**, half on a success.
    - The gazebo falls, and the pool's blessing is **destroyed for good**; Sergei can't appear again.
  - **Development:** if the two are united, Strahd blames the party and seeks to destroy them. He soon sends **Strahd's Invitation** (App F), and the road to the castle has no threatening random encounters.
- **Roc of Mount Ghakis (p. 159)** — triggered when the party crosses the bridge **east to west**, probably on the way back from the Amber Temple.
  - It snatches a horse or mule if there is one; otherwise it attacks a random party member.
  - It can't reach anyone in the arch guard posts. If it has nothing to attack on its turn, it shrieks and flies back to its nest.
- **Bloodhorn's Charge (p. 160)** — the party is surprised unless someone has passive Perception 16 or higher.
  - The goat charges; a hit and a failed save knocks the target 100 ft down onto a ledge.
  - It flees after taking 10 or more damage. Visibility is 60 ft, and it vanishes into a cleft.
- **Creeping Hut (p. 166)** — if the party overstays, Lysaga animates the hut. It fights until destroyed or until the gem is gone, and she fights to keep the gem.
- **Lost Battlefield (p. 166)** — travelling north from Berez, the party passes through a harmless ghost battle of mist (horsemen against devil-helmed pikemen, both armies human). If Argynvostholt is still unexplored, a silver mist dragon flies over and reveals the mansion.
- **Timing notes:**
  - Lysaga's new-moon bath.
  - Ch 12 (p. 180): three days after Wintersplinter attacks the winery, Lysaga sends all 7 Berez scarecrows to the vineyard.
  - Ch 12 (p. 180): the Martikov wine wagon can buy the party's way into Krezk.

  - Strahd doesn't want Vasilka.

## Player-facing [PLAYER]
- **Krezk lore (p. 146), known to villagers:**
  - They never leave for fear of wolves, dire wolves and werewolves.
  - A wine wagon comes from the Martikovs' Wizard of Wines about once a month.
  - Dmitri recently lost 14-year-old Ilya, the last of his four children.
  - The northern pool gives fresh water all year, beside the Shrine of the White Sun, a Morninglord gazebo.
  - The abbey is named for Saint Markovia, a Morninglord priest who led an uprising and stormed Castle Ravenloft, and died there.
  - The abbey was a hospital and convent that fell apart after the mists came: some clergy fell to Strahd, others went mad, starved or turned cannibal.
  - The ageless Abbot arrived over a century ago. He visits the shrine now and then, speaks little, demands wine as tribute, and is suspected of being Strahd or his servant.
  - Nobody visits the abbey. Its bell rings at odd hours, and screams and laughter carry down to the village.
- **Visible in Krezk:** the gate, the wall guards in fur hats, the pool that sparkles even under grey skies (the first drink visibly restores), the gazebo statue.
- **Visible at the abbey:** the unlocked squealing gate, the scarecrow sentries, the plaque, the bell and the din. The Abbot appears as a handsome young monk. Vasilka looks like a pale young woman in a torn red gown until someone is within 5 ft.
- **Visible at Tsolenka:** the black wall with vulture statues, green fire behind the portcullis, the white tower with gold knights, the bridge arches with charging-knight statues, and the rider who scatters like ash.
- **Visible at Berez:**
  - The marsh, flies and ruined cottages, the lantern signal, the skull-fenced goat pen, and the hut with its floating skull and raven cages.
  - Muriel tells the party:
    - The river flood emptied Berez.
    - An ancient, powerful hag, Baba Lysaga, lives in a hut in the middle and flies about in a giant skull.
    - The scarecrows are murderous, serve her and act as her alarm.
    - She raids the Wizard of Wines and is at war with the Martikovs.
    - Goats are penned by the old mansion.
  - Ulrich tells Marina's story. The monument's epitaph names Marina.
- **Handout:** *Strahd's Invitation* (App F), after "Something Blue" if Ireena and Sergei are united.
