# The Amber Temple — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 13, pp. 181–196. Layer: [BOOK]. Draft (reader 6b, 2026-09-24).
Neighbouring files: Tsolenka Pass (Ch 9) → `krezk-tsolenka-berez.md`; Castle K78/K84/K86 → `castle-ravenloft-part2.md`; Kasimir, Rahadin, Patrina → `npcs.md`.

## At a glance
- A vault cut into Mount Ghakis, reached by a road north from Tsolenka Pass (Ch 9). More than 2,000 years ago, good wizards built it to imprison **vestiges** (splinters of dead evil gods) and forbidden lore, under a faceless god of secrets. The vestiges corrupted them (p. 181).
- The archmage **Exethanter** learned lichdom here from a vestige, took over and made the dead guardians' skulls into **flameskulls** (p. 181).
- **Where Strahd made his pact.** He communed with the vestiges and sealed the pact by murdering Sergei. The Dark Powers were "born in the temple" and feed on him. He still returns for magic and for a way out, and they won't let him go (pp. 181–182).
- Core mechanic: 21 amber sarcophagi (20 occupied, 1 breached), each offering a **dark gift** with a permanent price (sidebar p. 191).
- Everywhere is −10 °F, so the DMG extreme-cold rules apply (sidebar p. 181).
- Book level: an average of 9th (Intro, Areas by Level, p. 6). Two map levels; optional events: Rahadin's Prayer and Kasimir's Dark Gift (p. 196).
- Tarokka treasure spots: X5a, X20, X40.

### Amber sarcophagi: the rules (sidebar p. 191)
- An 8-ft amber block (AC 16, 80 hp) holding a sliver of darkness: the vestige, which can't be harmed or controlled. Destroying the block banishes or destroys the vestige (DM's choice).
- **Touch** it and a telepathic offer follows, **in general terms only**. The exact effect is revealed after acceptance.
- **Willing acceptance only.** No gift if the creature is manipulated or coerced, and never the same gift twice. A gift works like a DMG charm.
- The gift brings a **permanent physical mark and/or flaw** (only *wish* or divine intervention removes it).
- **Non-evil creatures: DC 12 Charisma save** at every acceptance. On a failure they turn evil and become an NPC under the DM's control (the DM may let the player continue).

## NPCs
- **Neferon** (arcanaloth, MM; X5a, p. 184). Truesight. Poses as **"Heinrich Stolt"**, a confused old wizard, using *alter self* and Deception. Below half hp it teleports, turns invisible, flees and strikes again later. **Never leaves the temple.** It knows and spares **Rahadin**.
- **Exethanter** (lich; X27, pp. 188–189). 99 hp, nameless, cantrips only: **CR 10**.
  - First *greater restoration*: memory and spells return. He gives all the passwords except X28, the Strahd and temple lore, and, if asked, the library command words (the book misprints "K30"; it means X30).
  - Second *greater restoration*: 135 hp. He escorts the party, and nothing threatens them while he's with them.
  - Explains the sarcophagi if willing. **Neutral toward Strahd.**
- **Vilnius** (NE mage with a quasit; X9, pp. 185–186). The burnt apprentice of Jakarion: greedy, treacherous, cowardly. He wants his master's staff and spellbook. He knows three things: forbidden lore, flameskulls made from wizards, mountain folk. He carries the **shield guardian's control amulet** (1,000 gp) without knowing its purpose, and keeps it once he learns.
- **Helwa** (gladiator; X15). Hunting shelter; no lore. **Artimus** (gone; X20), the castle's architect.
- **Kasimir Velikov** (N dusk elf mage; App D pp. 232–233; Ch 5 N9a p. 121). Spied on the temple for years. His ring of warmth beats the cold. He seeks **Zhudun** (X42 E) to restore **Patrina**. See Events.
- **Rahadin** (App D, CR 10). Only in the Rahadin's Prayer event.

## Events and triggers
- **Chain reactions:**
  - Neferon casting → X17 flameskulls fire.
  - Noise at X6 → X33a's flameskulls come up.
  - Noise at X1a → an X15 scout. Noise at X14a → the ghasts get ready. Noise in X42 → the spawn wake.
- **Triggers:**
  - Lifting the ewer → specters.
  - Entering X24 → Wisdom save.
  - Opening the X26 chest → fall into X39.
  - Smashing a locked door → necrotic cube (at X40, the golem turns invisible instead).
- **Teleport:** K78's blue flame lands travellers at X42 T (Ch 4 p. 82).
- **Rahadin's Prayer** (p. 196). He believes Strahd's pact was with the god of secrets and prays for his master's release.
  - He rides a phantom steed up the pass, kneels at the X5 statue and swallows a live toad as an offering.
  - While he is there, the **arcanaloth and flameskulls attack no one else**.
  - If confronted, he defends himself without causing permanent harm, won't be taken alive and reveals nothing, not even who he is.
- **Kasimir's Dark Gift** (p. 196).
  - Having accepted Zhudun, Kasimir asks the party to escort him to **K84 crypt 21** (Castle Ravenloft).
  - Restored, **Patrina** is a **NE dusk elf archmage**. She feigns repentance while her strength and spells return, then seeks Strahd to become his vampire bride at last. She steers the party toward **killing Rahadin**, who opposed her marriage and mutilated Kasimir.
  - **Strahd has lost interest.** He would make her a spawn and re-entomb her, which she'll do anything to prevent; power matters more to her than he does.
  - K84 (Ch 4 p. 90): she returns with **no spells prepared** and asks for her spellbook, buried under her crypt coins, with the lie that she'll help destroy Strahd.
  - Epilogue (p. 208): if she lives, she mines the castle and the temple for lore to become **Barovia's new master**.
  - General rules that also apply: Kasimir (neutral) makes a DC 12 Cha save or turns evil. Anyone restored after 24+ hours dead gains an indefinite madness (Ch 2 p. 24).

## Secrets [DM]
- **Strahd's origin** (pp. 181–182; Ch 1, The Vampire's History, p. 9; *Tome of Strahd*, App F p. 252): a pact with the vestiges and Dark Powers for immortality, sealed by killing Sergei and drinking his blood. Shot by his guards, he rose as a vampire and the valley became a prison.
- **The Vampyr** (X42 W) mirrors that story: killing someone who loved him, drinking the blood, then a violent death at the hands of those who hated him. *Inference*: the book never says outright that Strahd took this gift. The *Tome* calls him "Vampyr".
- **Patrina led Strahd here** with the promise of immortality; Rahadin hunted for a rival woman meanwhile (App D p. 236). She told Kasimir the temple is where the pact was made (App D p. 233).
- Strahd, disguised as Vasili von Holtz, gave the Abbot forbidden lore from the temple for the mongrelfolk (App D p. 225). Argynvost built Argynvostholt nearby to keep the temple's evil in (Ch 7 p. 129).
- **Passwords:** X5c Etherna · X33a Shalx · X33b Maverus · X33e Harkotha · X33f Thangob · X40 Dhaviton · X28 Exethanter · X5b: knock three times.
- The book never says what the X33d vestige was or where it went.
- Ch 13 never mentions Madam Eva, Van Richten, the Mad Mage, the Hag Eye, Ireena or Strahd's brides.
