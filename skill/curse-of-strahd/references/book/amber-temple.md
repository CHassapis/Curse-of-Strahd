# The Amber Temple — published adventure
Source: Curse of Strahd (2016), Ch 13, pp. 181–196. Layer: [BOOK]. Draft (reader 6b, 2026-09-24).
Neighbouring files: Tsolenka Pass (Ch 9) → `krezk-tsolenka-berez.md`; Castle K78/K84/K86 → `castle-ravenloft-part2.md`; Kasimir, Rahadin, Patrina's backstory → `npcs.md`.

## At a glance
- A vault cut into the snowy face of Mount Ghakis, reached by a road running north from Tsolenka Pass (Ch 9). Over 2,000 years ago a secret order of good wizards built it to lock away **vestiges**, splinters of dead evil gods, along with forbidden lore. They dedicated it to a faceless, nameless god of secrets (p. 181).
- The vestiges corrupted the wizards and turned them on each other. Later the archmage **Exethanter** learned lichdom from a vestige, took over the temple and turned the dead guardians' skulls into **flameskulls** (p. 181).
- **This is where Strahd made his pact.** He communed with the vestiges, and the pact was sealed when he murdered Sergei. The Dark Powers "were born in the temple" and feed on Strahd's evil. Strahd still comes back for new magic and for a way out, but they will not let him go (pp. 181–182).
- Core mechanic: 21 amber sarcophagi (20 intact and occupied, 1 breached). Each offers a **dark gift**, which comes with a permanent physical mark or flaw and a risk to alignment (sidebar p. 191).
- The whole complex sits at −10 °F, and the DMG extreme-cold rules apply (sidebar p. 181).
- Book level: an average of 9th (Intro, Areas by Level, p. 6). Two map levels, 42 area keys, and two optional events: Rahadin's Prayer and Kasimir's Dark Gift (p. 196).
- Possible Tarokka treasure spots: X5a, X20 and X40.

### Amber sarcophagi: the rules (sidebar p. 191)
- A rough amber block, 8 × 5 × 5 ft, holding a few-inch sliver of darkness: the vestige. It can't be harmed or controlled and is immune to all conditions. The block has AC 16, 80 hp and immunity to poison and psychic damage. Destroying it makes the vestige vanish; the DM decides whether it is banished or destroyed.
- **Touching** the block creates a telepathic link, and the vestige makes its offer. The offer is described **only in general terms**; the exact game effect is revealed after acceptance (example: Fekre offers "the power to spread disease" and never mentions *contagion*).
- The gift must be **accepted willingly**. A creature that is manipulated, coerced or declines gets nothing. No creature can receive the same gift twice. A gift works like a DMG **charm** (Supernatural Gifts).
- On receipt the creature gains a **sinister physical trait and/or a flaw**, removable only by *wish* or divine intervention unless the entry says otherwise.
- **A non-evil creature makes a DC 12 Charisma save** each time it accepts a gift. On a failure its alignment becomes evil and the character becomes an NPC under the DM's control (the DM may let the player continue).
- Exethanter explains all of this if he feels like helping (p. 189).

## NPCs
- **Neferon** (arcanaloth, MM; X5a, p. 184). The temple's guardian. It has truesight, so it sees through its own darkness and through invisibility, and it wears the spectacles and robe listed under Treasure. It uses *alter self* and Deception to pass as **"Heinrich Stolt"**, a confused old wizard with a white beard who claims he was only guarding the temple. After losing more than half its hp it teleports to the floor, turns invisible, flees by the safest route and attacks again later. It **never leaves the temple**. It knows **Rahadin** and doesn't harm him (p. 196).
- **Exethanter** (lich, MM; X27, pp. 188–189). The body and mind are failing: 99 hp, his name forgotten, only cantrips left, **CR 10**. One *greater restoration* restores his memory and spells; a second restores his hp maximum to 135 (full lich).
  - Memory restored: he gives the passwords to every locked door except X28, all the Strahd and temple lore from the chapter opening, and, if asked, the library's command words. (The book says "area K30"; it means X30.)
  - Body restored: he escorts the party, and nothing in the temple threatens them while he is with them.
  - Attitude: he assumes visitors want knowledge and power, and explains the sarcophagi if he is inclined to help. He has **no alliance with Strahd and no grudge against him**, and no interest in ruling Barovia. He defends himself, turns to dust at 0 hp (phylactery in X28) and attacks anyone who takes his spellbook.
- **Vilnius** (NE human mage, with an invisible quasit; X9, pp. 185–186). Scorched and blistered, eating vermin. He was the apprentice of **Jakarion** (X17). He is greedy, treacherous, cowardly and suspicious, and he blames his dead master for bringing them here. He won't leave until the X10 golem is destroyed, and he wants only his master's staff and spellbook. He knows three things: the temple shelters forbidden knowledge, flameskulls made from dead wizards guard it, and mountain folk shelter here. He carries a **gold upside-down-V amulet (1,000 gp)**, the control amulet for the X35 shield guardian. He knows it is magic but not what it does. It thrums within 10 ft of the guardian, and once he understands it he won't part with it.
- **Helwa** (CE gladiator; X15, p. 187). Leader of the berserkers; hunts in the mountains; no lore.
- **Artimus** (gone; X20). The wizard-architect of Castle Ravenloft, who lived here before the castle was built.
- **Kasimir Velikov** (N dusk elf, mage stats; App D pp. 232–233; Ch 5 N9a p. 121). Leader of the dusk elves at the Vallaki Vistani camp. His **ring of warmth** protects him from the cold here (p. 181). He wants the gift that restores the ancient dead, and knows it when he touches **X42's east sarcophagus (Zhudun)** (p. 196). See Events.
- **Rahadin** (App D, CR 10). Appears only in the Rahadin's Prayer event (p. 196).
- **Patrina Velikovna** (not present). She is the reason Kasimir comes and the reason Strahd first came (App D p. 236). See Events and Secrets.

## Events and triggers
- **Chain reactions:** once Neferon starts casting, the X17 flameskulls open fire. Noise at X6 or on the shaft brings the X33a flameskulls up. Noise outside X1a draws an X15 scout. The X33c ghasts hear anyone reaching X14a with lights and noise. Noise in X42 wakes the spawn.
- **Touch and open triggers:** lifting the X22 ewer releases the specters. Entering X24 means a DC 16 Wisdom save. Opening the X26 chest drops the floor into X39. Smashing a locked door (X5c or an X33 vault) releases the necrotic cube; smashing X40's doors makes the golem invisible.
- **Arrival by teleport:** the K78 blue flame lands travellers at point T in X42 (Ch 4 p. 82; p. 196).
- **Rahadin's Prayer** (p. 196). Rahadin believes Strahd's pact was made with the nameless god and comes now and then to ask it to release his master.
  - He rides a phantom steed up Tsolenka Pass, kneels before the X5 statue, swallows a live toad as an offering and makes a gesture of supplication.
  - Neferon knows him. **The arcanaloth and the flameskulls attack no other visitor until Rahadin is dead or gone.**
  - If confronted, he defends himself but causes no permanent harm. He would die rather than be captured, and reveals nothing, not even who he is. Left alone, he rides home.
- **Kasimir's Dark Gift** (p. 196).
  - If Kasimir reaches X42 and accepts **Zhudun's** gift, he asks the party to escort him to the **Castle Ravenloft catacombs (K84, crypt 21)** to restore his long-dead sister **Patrina**.
  - On success Patrina returns as a **NE dusk elf archmage**. She feigns repentance until her strength and spells come back, then goes to Strahd to become his vampire bride at last.
  - She resents Rahadin for mutilating Kasimir and tries to steer the party into **killing Rahadin**, who has long opposed her marriage.

## Secrets [DM]
- **Strahd's origin** (pp. 181–182). Exethanter sensed a man of destiny in Strahd; the vestiges sensed a darkness greater than their own. Strahd made his pact with them, sealed it in Sergei's blood, became a vampire, and the Dark Powers turned his land into a prison. The Dark Powers were born here and feed on him, which is why they won't let him go. Compare Ch 1, The Vampire's History (p. 9: a pact with the Dark Powers for immortality, sealed by killing Sergei and drinking his blood) and the *Tome of Strahd* (App F p. 252: "a pact with death, a pact of blood"; he now sleeps far below the castle).
- **The Vampyr vestige (X42 W)** mirrors Strahd's story: killing a humanoid who loved him (Sergei) and drinking the blood, then a violent death at the hands of those who hated him (the guards' arrows). *Inference:* the book never says outright that Strahd took this gift, but it is the obvious reading. The *Tome* also has him call himself "Vampyr".
- **Patrina led Strahd here.** She tempted him with a vault holding the secret of immortality, and Rahadin hunted for a rival woman while Strahd explored it (App D, Rahadin, p. 236). She told Kasimir the temple is where Strahd made his pact (App D p. 233).
- Strahd, disguised as Vasili von Holtz, brought forbidden lore from the temple to the Abbot to create the mongrelfolk (App D p. 225).
- The dragon Argynvost built Argynvostholt near the temple to keep its evil locked in, and the Order of the Silver Dragon drove off people searching for it (Ch 7 p. 129).
