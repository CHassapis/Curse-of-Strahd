## NPCs

### Strahd von Zarovich (pp. 239–240)
- **What:** CR 15, lawful evil undead shapechanger. AC 16, 144 hp; resists necrotic damage and nonmagical weapon damage; darkvision 120 ft; passive Perception 22.
- **Traits:** polymorph into a bat, wolf or mist · Legendary Resistance 3/day · **Misty Escape**: at 0 hp he turns to mist and must reach his coffin within 2 hours · **Regeneration** 20 per turn (it stops for a turn after radiant damage or holy water) · spider climb · vampire weaknesses (can't enter uninvited, harmed by running water, a stake in the heart pins him, sunlight).
- **Actions:** two attacks, only one of them a bite · unarmed strike +9 (1d8 + 4 slashing plus 4d6 necrotic, or a grapple, escape DC 18) · bite (1d6 + 4 piercing plus 3d6 necrotic; lowers the target's hp maximum) · **Charm** (DC 17 Wisdom) · Children of the Night once a day (2d4 swarms of bats or rats, or 3d6 wolves outdoors).
- **Legendary actions:** move, unarmed strike, bite (costs 2).
- **Spells:** 9th-level wizard, DC 18. Includes *animate objects*, *scrying*, *greater invisibility*, *polymorph*, *blight*, *fireball*, *animate dead*, *nondetection*, *mirror image*, *detect thoughts*, *gust of wind*, *fog cloud* and *sleep*.
- **Tactics (p. 239):**
  - He strikes at the best moment and from the best position.
  - When damage outpaces his regeneration, he moves out of reach or flies off, with summoned beasts covering him.
  - He studies the party, then charms characters with low Wisdom into thralls or bodyguards.
- **Where:** anywhere in his land. He is always at the card-5 place unless he has been forced into his tomb (K86). Everywhere else, roll on the **Strahd's Minions** table (d20, p. 239):

  | d20 | He brings |
  |---|---|
  | 1–3 | 1d4 + 2 dire wolves |
  | 4–6 | 1d6 + 3 ghouls |
  | 7–9 | 1d4 + 2 Strahd zombies |
  | 10–12 | 2d4 swarms of bats |
  | 13–15 | 1d4 + 1 vampire spawn (they still need an invitation to enter a home) |
  | 16–18 | 3d6 wolves |
  | 19–20 | nobody |

  Indoors, his creatures break in through doors, windows or chimneys, or up through the ground.
- **Heart of Sorrow** (Ch 4, K20; p. 239):
  - A giant crystal heart with 50 hp that **absorbs all damage dealt to Strahd**. If damage takes it to 0, it is destroyed and Strahd takes the rest.
  - If it still has at least 1 hp, it resets to 50 at dawn.
  - As a bonus action Strahd can cut the link. He can restore it only while inside the castle.
  - It stops working if the heart or Strahd is fully inside an *antimagic field*.
- **Lair actions** (in the castle, initiative 20; p. 239):
  - Pass through walls until his next lair action.
  - Open, close or lock doors and windows (DC 20 Strength to force a locked one).
  - Call the spirit of someone who died in the castle to make one attack (as a specter).
  - Rip away a creature's shadow (DC 17 Charisma), which becomes an undead shadow that obeys him. *Greater restoration* or *remove curse* gives the shadow back, but only after that undead shadow has been destroyed.
- **Wants (Ch 1 p. 10):**
  - To turn Ireena (Tatyana reborn) into his consort.
  - To find **Van Richten**, lock him up and break him.
  - To judge whether any newcomer could be his successor or consort.
  - Strahd and his minions **never attack Ireena** (Ch 1 p. 10).
- **Fate (Epilogue p. 207):** he can only be destroyed in his coffin. If Rahadin is still alive, he attacks the party in grief.

### Madam Eva (pp. 233–234)
- **What:** CR 10, chaotic neutral human. AC 10, 88 hp, speed 20; Insight +13, passive Perception 19.
  - 16th-level cleric caster, DC 17. Spells include *spirit guardians*, *divination*, *greater restoration*, *raise dead*, *true seeing*, *harm*, *find the path*, *fire storm*, *regenerate* and *earthquake*.
  - **Curse** (once per long rest; DC 17 Wisdom): the target is blinded and deafened until the curse is lifted, and Eva takes 5d6 psychic damage when it ends.
  - **Evil Eye** (DC 17): *animal friendship*, *charm person* or *hold person*. If the target saves, Eva is blinded until the end of her next turn.
  - Dagger.
- **Secret [DM]:** her real name is **Katarina**. She is **Strahd's half-sister**, the daughter of King Barov and a Vistana. Strahd doesn't know; she has told no one.
  - Over 400 years ago she worked as a **maid at Castle Ravenloft** and was present at Sergei and Tatyana's wedding. After the murder she fled to the Vistani.
  - She made a pact with **Mother Night**, trading her youth for foresight, and became an ageless crone.
  - She uses that gift **to help Strahd**: she sends Vistani wagons to other worlds to bring adventurers, hoping they will destroy him or free him.
  - The Dark Powers would accept her as the new darklord. She doesn't want it and would rather find Strahd a successor, though she doubts she can.
- **Traits:** ideal, Strahd freed from his curse · bond, the Vistani are her people now · flaw, the people whose fates she reads are only tools.
- **Where:** her tent at the Tser Pool camp (Ch 2, area G, pp. 36–37). She greets each character by name, mentions something from their past, and offers the reading.
- **How she acts (Ch 2 p. 37):** cunning behind the madness. She **does Strahd's bidding when he calls on her**, never angers him or endangers the Vistani, and **never gives aid and never asks for any**.
- **Tarokka:** she is **not** among the 14 ally options (Ch 1 pp. 15–16). With the Mists as card 5, she re-reads for Strahd's location after at least 3 days (p. 17).

### Rictavio / Dr Rudolph van Richten (p. 238)
- **What:** CR 5, lawful good human. AC 12 (leather), 77 hp; Wisdom save +7, Arcana +9, Medicine +7, passive Perception 17.
  - **Undead Slayer:** his weapon hits deal +3d6 to undead.
  - **Sword cane:** a wooden cane (bludgeoning) with a silvered blade inside (piercing).
  - 9th-level cleric caster, DC 15. Spells include *death ward*, *freedom of movement*, *dispel evil and good*, *magic circle*, *remove curse*, *speak with dead*, *augury* and *protection from evil and good*.
  - **Special equipment:** *hat of disguise*, *ring of mind shielding*, *spell scroll of raise dead*.
- **Cover [PLAYER]:** a colourful half-elf bard who says he is a carnival ringmaster. He arrived with a monkey (Piccolo, now given to the toymaker Blinsky) and tells tall tales. Months ago he took over an old tower on Lake Baratok. He is at the Blue Water Inn in Vallaki (Ch 5, N2, p. 100). Twice a day he carries food out "for a friend"; it is really for his horse Drusilla (N2f) and his caged saber-toothed tiger in his wagon at the Arasek Stockyard (N5, p. 115).
- **True story [DM]:**
  - A doctor and scholar from Darkon. The Vistani stole his son **Erasmus** (aged 14) and sold him to the vampire **Baron Metus**. Erasmus became a vampire spawn, and Van Richten staked him when the boy begged him to. Metus then killed his wife **Ingrid**. Van Richten destroyed Metus, took revenge on the Vistani, and has hunted monsters ever since.
- **Wants:**
  - To kill Strahd, but not head-on. He waits for Strahd's long hibernations in his coffin.
  - To learn about the **Keepers of the Feather** without exposing them to Strahd; he thinks they could be useful.
  - To kill Strahd's spies, starting with **evil Vistani**. He plans to set the tiger on the Vallaki camp once he is sure they serve Strahd (Ch 5 p. 100).
- **Ezmerelda:** he doesn't know she is in Barovia. If he finds out, he protects her quietly without risking his plan, and may manipulate adventurers into watching her.
- **Works alone:** a Vistani seer's **curse brings doom to anyone he befriends**. If he thinks he is about to be unmasked, he withdraws to his tower (Ch 11) or somewhere quiet.
- **Traits:** evil must be challenged · keep loved ones distant and hidden · cursed, he will never have peace.
- **Tarokka:**
  - **Artifact (Joker 1)** makes him the ally. **Told about the reading, he drops the disguise** (Ch 1 p. 15).
  - The Illusionist card puts a treasure in his wagon.
  - With Horseman A, he gives the party his *raise dead* scroll.
- Strahd's spies are looking for him (Ch 1 p. 10).

### Ezmerelda d'Avenir (pp. 230–231)
- **What:** CR 8, chaotic good human (Vistana). AC 17 (+1 studded leather), 82 hp.
  - Three attacks: two with her +1 rapier, and one with her +1 handaxe or silvered shortsword.
  - **Curse** (once per long rest; DC 14): the target becomes vulnerable to one damage type she chooses, and she takes 3d6 psychic damage when it ends.
  - **Evil Eye** (DC 14).
  - 7th-level wizard, DC 14. Spells include *greater invisibility*, *lightning bolt*, *magic circle*, *clairvoyance*, *mirror image*, *knock* and *shield*.
  - Gear: two potions of greater healing, 6 holy water, 3 wooden stakes.
- **Story:**
  - As a child she saw her own family **kidnap Erasmus** and hand him to a vampire. Van Richten tracked the family down, questioned her parents and **spared them**; the mercy moved her.
  - She ran away at 15 and found him two years later. He almost killed her as a Vistani assassin before hearing her out. She stayed two years hunting at his side.
  - He never fully trusted a Vistana and kept secrets from her. They argued more and more, and parted as friends.
  - A werewolf bit off her **right leg below the knee**. She did not catch lycanthropy, and walks well on a **prosthetic**.
  - She heard he had gone to Barovia, came in her own wagon, found his tower and some of his belongings, and is studying his notes on Strahd. She wants his respect and means to **kill Strahd herself**.
- **Deck:** she keeps a **tarokka deck** in her wagon (Ch 11, V1). It is not magic, but she can do Eva's reading with it.
- **Traits:** evil that feeds on the innocent must be destroyed · Van Richten is like a father to her · she goes where angels fear to tread.
- **Where:**
  - Van Richten's Tower and her wagon (Ch 11, V1, p. 168). She knows the trick to getting past the tower-door trap (p. 172).
  - The abbey barracks (Ch 8, S19, p. 154).
  - A Castle Ravenloft random encounter (Ch 4, p. 50).
  - **Ezmerelda's Retreat** (Ch 11 p. 172): she comes back to the tower hurt (30 hp) after facing Strahd. From then on **Strahd wants her dead**.
- **Tarokka:** the **Mists** card makes her the ally.

### Izek Strazni (pp. 231–232)
- **What:** CR 5, neutral evil human. AC 14 (studded leather), 112 hp; Intimidation +8.
  - **Brute:** an extra weapon die on hits.
  - Two battleaxe attacks.
  - **Hurl Flame:** +5, 60 ft, 3d6 fire, and it sets unattended flammable objects alight.
- **Story:**
  - Born in Vallaki. On the way home from a fishing trip, a dire wolf bit off his right arm. His father carried him home while an uncle distracted the wolf. His **sister ran into the woods and was never seen again**.
  - He was "born without a soul", unlike her.
  - Orphaned, he killed the children who mocked him. When he was caught, **the Baron pardoned him and took him into his house**. He is loyal, drinks heavily, and does the Baron's dirty work.
  - One morning he woke up with a **fiend's arm**: barbed, long-fingered, able to snap fire.
- **Ireena:** he dreams of a woman and has forced **Gadof Blinsky** to make **dolls** of her for years. He doesn't know her name. He and Ireena **don't know they are brother and sister**. After the wolf attack Ireena wandered for days until **Kolyan Indirovich** found and adopted her.
  - If Izek sees her, he tries to drag her to the mansion and keep her in his room (Ch 5, N3j). He wants her in an unhealthy way and lets nothing come between them.
- **Traits:** fear is a weapon · loyal to the Baron, but "he isn't family" · would do anything to find his sister.
- **Fate:** if townsfolk burn the mansion, he flees Vallaki, perhaps to Old Bonegrinder, Argynvostholt or Berez (Ch 5 p. 124).

### Kasimir Velikov (pp. 232–233)
- **What:** uses the mage stat block, neutral; darkvision 60 ft; Fey Ancestry. He wears a *ring of warmth* and has a spellbook (its spells are listed at Ch 5, N9a).
- **Story:**
  - A **dusk elf**. His people surrendered to Strahd's armies, and the Vistani carried the survivors into the valley.
  - He took the name of **Velikov**, the Vistana who adopted him into his clan (dead for over a century). He lives among Velikov's descendants and thinks less of today's Vistani.
- **Patrina [DM]:**
  - He and his fellow dusk elves **stoned his sister Patrina to death**, believing her the devil's concubine. Strahd had the tribe's women killed, and **Kasimir's ears were cut off** (by Rahadin); he hides the wounds under a cowl.
  - Patrina now speaks to him **in dreams**, claiming she has repented. The text says he knows Strahd corrupted her and wants Strahd destroyed so she can be saved; his flaw is that he believes she can be redeemed.
  - **She is using him. Her real goal is to become a vampire as powerful as Strahd.**
- **Amber Temple:** Patrina told him that Strahd made his pact there. He has spied on it for years and needs adventurers to survive it. He hopes it holds the secret of bringing the long-dead back to life.
- **Traits:** atone or be damned · bring Patrina back even at the cost of his life · believes she can be redeemed.
- **Where:** his hovel at the Vistani camp outside Vallaki (Ch 5, N9a, p. 121). At the Amber Temple he can accept a dark gift that lets him **restore Patrina** (Ch 13, "Kasimir's Dark Gift", p. 196). She pretends to repent, then goes back to Strahd. Strahd no longer wants her and would make her a spawn. She tries to turn the party against **Rahadin** (Ch 13 p. 196).
- **Tarokka:** the **Seer** card makes him the ally, but only after the Amber Temple. With the **Six of Coins (Beggar)**, a treasure is in his hovel.

### Rahadin (pp. 236–237)
- **What:** CR 10, lawful evil dusk elf. AC 18 (studded leather, Dex 22), 135 hp, speed 35; Stealth +14, Perception +11 (passive 21), Intimidation +12.
  - **Deathly Choir:** anyone within 10 ft hears in their mind the screams of everyone he has killed (*mind blank* protects). As a bonus action he forces a DC 16 Wisdom save: 3d10 psychic damage, half on a success.
  - Fey Ancestry; Mask of the Wild.
  - Three scimitar attacks, or two poisoned darts (+2d4 poison).
  - Innate spells: *magic weapon* and *nondetection* once a day each; *misty step* and *phantom steed* three times a day each.
- **Story:**
  - Exiled by a dusk elf prince he thought weak and corrupt, he **helped King Barov conquer the dusk elves**. Barov made him an **honorary member of the family**. He then served as Strahd's general.
  - He brought the wizards and artisans who built Ravenloft, then became its **chamberlain**. He flogs any staff who fall short. He has served for nearly 500 years.
  - He distrusted **Patrina** and looked for a woman to pull Strahd away from her; Tatyana did it. He enjoyed telling Patrina she was no longer welcome.
  - After Strahd became a vampire, he made sure each new bride was **lavished with jewels and fine clothes**.
  - After Patrina was stoned, Strahd sent him to punish her people: he **killed the dusk elf women** and **cut off Kasimir's ears**.
- **Traits:** loyalty is everything · a son of Barov, serving his "brother" forever · will kill thousands more for the von Zarovich legacy. He cares only for Strahd.
- **Where:**
  - Castle Ravenloft: chamberlain's office (Ch 4, K72, p. 79), the great entry (K8, p. 55), random encounters (p. 49).
  - The Amber Temple (Ch 13, "Rahadin's Prayer", p. 196): he rides a phantom steed there to pray at the great statue (X5) for his master's release. The arcanaloth leaves him alone, and he won't do the party lasting harm.
- **Fate:** "Rahadin's Revenge" (Epilogue p. 207): he appears moments after Strahd dies and attacks, beyond reason.

### Baba Lysaga (pp. 228–229)
- **What:** CR 11, chaotic evil human shapechanger. AC 15, 120 hp, Int 20.
  - Three quarterstaff attacks.
  - Can turn into a **swarm of flies**. **Blessing of Mother Night:** protected from divination as if by *nondetection*.
  - Summons 1d4 swarms of insects (recharges on a rest).
  - 16th-level wizard, DC 17. Spells include *power word stun*, *finger of death*, *mirage arcane*, *true seeing*, *programmed illusion*, *cloudkill*, *geas*, *scrying*, *Evard's black tentacles*, *polymorph*, *blight*, *fireball* and *lightning bolt*.
- **Story:**
  - She was Queen Ravenovia's **midwife** and believes she is Strahd's **true mother**. She cast protections over baby Strahd, sang to him on stormy nights, and gave him his "spark of magic".
  - Ravenovia banished her. She prayed to Mother Night for the queen's death, then moved as close to Strahd as she dared.
  - She has **never faced him**, because she couldn't bear his rejection.
- **Gifts of Mother Night:** skin as hard as stone, resistance to harmful magic, shielding from divination. To stay alive she must **bathe in beasts' blood on new-moon nights**; if she fails, she ages to dust within seconds.
- **Allies:**
  - A **coven of witches in Castle Ravenloft** (Barovian witches), who helped her discover the **Keepers of the Feather**. She wages war on their winery refuge.
  - An alliance with the **Yester Hill druids**, who see Strahd as a god; she tells them she gave birth to him.
- **Traits:** no love beats a mother's · anyone who doubts she is his mother can rot · won't rest until her son's enemies are gone.
- **Where:** Berez, in her hut (Ch 10, U3, p. 163), and flying in a giant skull (Ch 10).

### Vladimir Horngaard (pp. 241–242)
- **What:** a revenant variant, CR 7, lawful evil. AC 17 (half plate), 192 hp.
  - **Regeneration** 10 per turn (stops for a turn after fire or radiant damage).
  - **Rejuvenation:** 24 hours after his body is destroyed, his soul takes another corpse. Only *wish* sends it on for good.
  - Immune to turning.
  - **Vengeful Tracker:** always knows the direction and distance to Strahd.
  - **+2 greatsword** with a hilt shaped like silver dragon wings and a dragon-head pommel holding a black opal. Two attacks at +9 for 4d6 + 6, plus 4d6 against Strahd. Fists can grapple.
  - **Vengeful Glare:** Strahd within 30 ft must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom save or be paralysed.
- **Story:**
  - He was a knight of the **Order of the Silver Dragon**, a friend of Argynvost, and field commander. He died defending the refugees after watching Strahd kill **Sir Godfrey Gwilym, his beloved**.
  - He came back as a revenant, and his hatred raised Godfrey and other knights with him.
- **Wants:** Strahd to **suffer forever, never to be destroyed**. He knows that even a temporary defeat of Strahd would lift the mists and free the knights' spirits, but prefers Strahd's torment. If he let go of his hate, he would find peace and remember love.
- **Where:** Argynvostholt, the dragon's audience hall (Ch 7, Q36, p. 138), and his bedroom (Q39). With the **One of Swords**, he holds a treasure and gives it up only once the beacon is lit (p. 139).
- **Ally (Ghost A) is Godfrey, not Vladimir** (Q37, p. 139).

### The Abbot (p. 225)
- **What:** uses the **deva** stat block, but lawful evil. Usually appears as a strikingly handsome human priest of about 30.
- **Story:**
  - Sent from the Upper Planes over a century ago to honour Saint Markovia, he reopened the abbey and cared for the sick until the Dark Powers corrupted him.
  - He cured the **Belviews**, an inbred family of lepers, of their disease, then gave in to their wish for animal traits. **"Vasili von Holtz"** (**Strahd in disguise**) brought him lore from the Amber Temple, and together they turned the Belviews into **mongrelfolk**.
  - Strahd played the tragic prisoner of his curse. The deva decided Strahd can't truly die in Barovia and now believes **a bride will cure him**. He has built the flesh golem **Vasilka** and is teaching her manners.
  - **Strahd has no interest in her**; he enjoys corrupting the angel.
- **Traits:** giving the devil what he wants will save the land · loves his golems and mongrelfolk · believes he can't be corrupted.
- **Where:** the abbey main hall, Krezk (Ch 8, S13, pp. 150–151). He wants a **bridal gown** for Vasilka and offers *raise dead* up to three times, or his healing touch, in return.

### Pidlwick II (pp. 235–236)
- **What:** CR 1/4, neutral evil small construct. AC 14, 10 hp; club and darts; **Ambusher**.
- **Story:**
  - A clockwork copy of the jester Pidlwick, made by the toymaker **Fritz von Weerg** for **Duchess Dorfniya Dilisnya**, who hoped to marry Strahd.
  - It **pushed the real Pidlwick down the stairs**, and later smothered guests in their beds. Strahd saw through it and kept it to get rid of guests who annoyed him.
  - It is afraid of Strahd, **follows anyone who pays it attention**, and can't speak.
- **Where:** Castle Ravenloft (K59 per the Marionette card; Ch 4 p. 73). The **Marionette A** card makes it the ally.

## Monsters

### Baba Lysaga's creeping hut (p. 226)
- CR 11, gargantuan construct. AC 16, 263 hp; blindsight 120 ft; **siege monster**.
- Three root attacks (reach 60 ft, 4d10 + 8), or two roots and a thrown rock (range 120 ft, 3d8 + 8).
- *Dispel magic* can knock it out for 1 minute (Constitution save). An *antimagic field* on the gem stops it.
- **Its heart:** one of the **three Wizard of Wines gems**, stolen and twisted by Lysaga, lies in a hollow under the floorboards.
  - The floorboards come up with a DC 14 Strength check or 10 damage.
  - While the hut is alive, the hollow grows teeth: anyone reaching for the gem makes a **DC 20 Dexterity** save or takes 3d6 piercing and misses.
  - **Removing the gem stops the hut.**
- Inside: a 15-ft room with the furniture bolted down.

### Broom of animated attack (p. 226)
- CR 1/4. AC 15, 17 hp, flies 50 ft.
- Looks like a *broom of flying* and **attacks anyone who grabs or rides it**. Some can be ridden, but carry only half a broom of flying's load.
- Found in Death House (App B, area 14, p. 215), Castle Ravenloft random encounters, and the Amber Temple (X32).

### Guardian portrait (p. 227)
- CR 1. AC 5, 22 hp. The eyes follow you.
- Innate spells, three times a day each: *counterspell*, *crown of madness*, *hypnotic pattern*, *telekinesis*.
- Where: the portrait of Strahd (Ch 4, K47, p. 68).

### Strahd's animated armor (p. 227)
- CR 6. AC 21, 112 hp. Headless burgundy plate with gold angels.
- Greatsword (+1d6 lightning); **Shocking Bolt** (3d6, advantage against targets in metal armour).
- Obeys only Strahd. Where: the parapets (Ch 4, K46).

### Barovian witch (p. 229)
- CR 1/2, chaotic evil. AC 10, 16 hp; darkvision.
- 3rd-level wizard: *sleep*, *ray of sickness*, *Tasha's hideous laughter*, *alter self* (which gives claws) and *invisibility*.
- They keep familiars, mostly cats, and are **obsessive hoarders of junk**. They fear only Strahd and privately call themselves "brothers and sisters of Strahd".
- Where: **Baba Lysaga's allies** in Castle Ravenloft (Ch 4, K56 and random encounters); also the Amber Temple (X32).

### Tree blight (p. 230)
- CR 7, Huge. AC 15, 92 hp.
- Branch attack (reach 15 ft, 3d6 + 6) and grasping root (grapple DC 15, 1d6 + 6 each turn). Roots can be cut: AC 15, 6 slashing damage, with no harm to the blight. Bite against a grappled target (3d8 + 6).
- Soaked in blood, so it is hard to burn. It hates other tree blights and treants.
- **Wintersplinter** (Ch 12 p. 180; Ch 14 p. 198).

### Mongrelfolk (p. 234)
- CR 1/4. AC 11, 26 hp.
- Each has one random extraordinary feature (amphibious, darkvision, wings, keen senses and so on). They **mimic sounds** (DC 12 Insight to tell) and are experts at camouflage.
- *Greater restoration* restores those **made by magic**; those **born** mongrelfolk cannot be restored.
- The Belviews: abbey (Ch 8, S12c, S15), Clovin (S17), and **Cyrus Belview** at the castle.

### Phantom warrior (p. 235)
- CR 3. AC 16, 45 hp; incorporeal; can go ethereal; two spectral longsword attacks (force).
- It stays by choice, out of duty, and remembers only the last 1d10 + 10 days of its life.
- Argynvostholt (Ch 7, several areas); **Sir Klutz** (Ch 4, K84, crypt 33), the Ghost B ally option.

### Strahd zombie (p. 241)
- CR 1. AC 8, 30 hp.
- **Loathsome Limbs:** 5 or more bludgeoning or slashing damage cuts off a leg, an arm or the head, and the severed parts keep fighting.
- Made from the castle's long-dead guards.

### Wereraven (p. 242)
- CR 2. Secretive, law-abiding and good. Groups of 7–12 are called "kindnesses".
- They collect shiny things, give to charity, and **hide magic items to keep them from evil**. They are reluctant to bite.
- Rules for player wereravens: Dexterity 15, and a raven-form bite that deals 1 piercing damage and passes on the curse.
- The **Keepers of the Feather**: the Martikovs (Ch 5, N2; Ch 12). The **Raven** card ally is Davian.

## Secrets [DM]

- Izek and Ireena are siblings. Patrina is manipulating Kasimir.
- Lysaga has never met Strahd since his infancy.
- Pidlwick II murdered Pidlwick.
- The Heart of Sorrow soaks up Strahd's damage.
