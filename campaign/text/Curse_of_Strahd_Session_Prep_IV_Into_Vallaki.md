### Ground floor — the bakery

| READ ALOUD A baker's front room: a counter, a slate with prices chalked on it — "Dream Pastries, 1 copper, or trade" — and shelves of empty baskets. Behind the counter, a brick oven big enough to walk into, still warm. Trays of small round pastries are cooling on a rack. The room smells of butter and cinnamon, and underneath that, of something else. |
|---|

Morgantha's body. READ ALOUD: In death she no longer looks like a sweet old woman. Blue-black skin, a mouth of broken yellow teeth, hands like roots. Around her neck, on a greasy cord, a smooth dark stone. At her belt, a small bag of stitched grey leather that moves, very slightly, on its own.
- Heartstone — the black gem on the cord. What it does for them: its touch cures any disease (2014 Monster Manual lore). Only a hag can use it to go Ethereal. It sells for 300 gp to the right buyer.
- Soul bag — the grey leather bag. Anyone who holds it to their ear hears faint children's voices. What it does for them: nothing they can use. It has AC 15, 20 HP and Resistance to all damage; destroy it and the souls inside go free (2024 Monster Manual). Fedra can lead them on with a short rite, a Grave cleric's job. Once it's gone, Offalia has one less thing to come for.
- Iron key — on the same cord. Opens the chest upstairs.
- Three rings — copper and bone, 5 gp for the lot.
Ask the table: who takes the heartstone, and who takes the soul bag? Write the names down. Offalia comes for them in Scene 5.
The oven. READ ALOUD: At the back of the oven, in the ash, are small pale shapes. Children's bones.
The pastries. Two dozen Dream Pastries. Anyone who eats one sleeps for 8 hours and can't be woken except by damage or an ally using an action to shake them.
The pantry. Flour, lard, honey, a jar of dried poppy heads. Investigation DC 12: the pastry recipe, pinned inside the door.

| READ ALOUD (the pantry) A narrow cupboard of a room, shelves to the ceiling. Sacks of flour, crocks of lard, honey gone to crystal in its jar. Everything is labelled in the same spidery hand. One jar holds dried poppy heads. The one next to it holds something grey and powdery, and the label just says: "Finest." |
