Printing
Print in colour at actual size (100%), not "fit to page". Thicker paper (120–160 gsm) makes the letter and the
invitation card feel right.
Cut each paper out along its own ragged edge. The white around it isn't part of the prop.
Write the blanks on the contract with a brown or black pen, in your own hand.
Making Strahd's letter
Fold the letter in three along the faint creases, writing inside.
Wrap the band from page 3 around the folded letter and glue the plain end under the end with the seal. Or skip the
band: cut out one of the loose seals and glue it across the letter's open edge.
The players break the seal to open it: they tear the band, or peel the seal. Keep the spare seal for a second letter.
Optional ageing
Crumple the contract, flatten it, and rub the edges with a cold, used tea bag. Let it dry flat under a book.
Leave the invitation card crisp and clean. Lady Wachter's paper is expensive.
Fonts: Pinyon Script, Mrs Saint Delafield, Meddon, Cedarville Cursive, UnifrakturMaguntia, IM Fell (SIL Open Font License). All artwork drawn for this handout set.
