W HO  SHE  IS
The first of the three, a bride for two hundred years. A foundling from
Amn, far beyond the mists, who stowed away in a Vistani wagon as a

in Vallaki took her in. She never felt she belonged. At eighteen she went
looking for the Amber Temple and found Rahadin instead. He gave her
to Strahd as a distraction. Strahd fed on her and taught her magic and
manners. When she proved too clever to waste, he made her a bride.
Rahadin told her the truth: Strahd tires of every wife. So she made
herself useful: his books, his errands, his eyes. She even found him the
next one: disguised by magic as "the Count's agent", she went to
Anastrasya's parties.

all three of them. She won't cross Strahd while he's winning. If the party
starts to look like winners, she'll quietly change sides. His death sets her
free.

A PPEARANCE
Tall and still, with dark skin, long braids and a gold crown worked
like a church window. An old white wedding gown, gold at the throat
and wrists. She never hurries, and she never stops smiling.
W HO  SHE  IS
A Vallaki noblewoman, famous a century ago for her parties: lavish
nights in honour of a Count she had never met, whose reputation she
knew perfectly well. She was beautiful, and sure it would make her his
bride. At one party a veiled guest, Ludmilla, invited her to Castle
Ravenloft. Ludmilla expected her to be horrified. Anastrasya fell in love
with the place, and Strahd took her that night.
She's the strongest of the three in the old vampire gifts: the charm, the
bats, the rats. She believes she's his favourite. She isn't; nobody is but
Tatyana. Old families in Vallaki still remember her parties. Lady
Wachter's great-grandmother went to them, and the Wachters have kept
the faith ever since.
What she wants: to be first. She resents Ludmilla, who recruited her
and still gives the orders. Most of all she hates Ireena, who is everything
Anastrasya was supposed to be. She doesn't want Ireena dead. She wants
her ruined, face first.

Running the Brides
Three wives he keeps but doesn't love — and how strong to make them as the campaign goes on
W HAT  THEY  ARE
In the book they're three plain vampire spawn (CR 5) who sleep beside
Strahd in his tomb (K86), in old wedding gowns and very good
jewellery; if he's destroyed, they're free. These sheets keep that, and
add each bride's history from the r/CurseofStrahd community
(u/JonathanWriting's version), her own trick, and enough strength for
level 12.
Strahd treats them as possessions. He never gave them his full power
and never will. Each one knows it. That resentment is your lever.

E SCAPING  — AND  DYING  EARLY
When a bride decides to leave, she Dashes or Disengages (Deathless Agility) and goes up a wall, out a window or over a roof. First, check whether
she can:
She's trapped, no roll — if she's Grappled, Restrained or Paralyzed; if there's no wall, window or door she can reach this turn; or if she's inside
real sunlight (a Daylight spell). She fights on.
Otherwise roll a d20 — the escape roll. 1–5: cut off this round (a door slams, a guard's lantern, a slip on a wet roof). She fights on and rolls again
next turn. 6–20: she's gone.
So the brides can die early. At half HP, a cornered bride can be finished by a level 5 party, and a party that plans (a net, a grapple, Daylight on the
only exit) can make her first appearance her last. Then see page 5.
