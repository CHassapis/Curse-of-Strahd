BEFORE IT STARTS
Ask: where is everyone sleeping, who's on watch, who has the heartstone and the
soul bag?
Perception DC 14 (DC 10 on watch) for anyone awake who didn't eat a pastry: the inn
has gone silent; the candle leans away from the darkest corner. Fail = Surprised:
Disadvantage on Initiative. Anyone using the Hag Eye acts first.
Ethradir can't be put to sleep (Trance) and stays aware: he rolls as on watch. Fedra's
raven counts as on watch and can peck one sleeper awake as its action.
Sleepers are Unconscious. Nobody attacks one on purpose; a hit wakes them and isn't a
crit.
Revivify: 450 gp of diamonds = one casting (300 gp), within 1 minute of a death.
Half the party ate pastries, or no long rest since Bonegrinder? Remove one swarm.

HOW IT ENDS
To stop Offalia escaping: knock the heartstone out of her hand, or keep her
Incapacitated. Grappling her works too (table rule).
If she dies: she turns back into her true form on the floor. Heartstone, soul bag,
broom, a pouch of Dream Pastries, 15 gp. Bella is the last sister.
Bargain: "The heart and the bag, and the girl comes back up out of my pet. Tonight."
She keeps her word, for tonight.
Most likely: Offalia escapes, Ireena is saved, and the inn burns. Ethradir hears where
Bella went: "Miranda, isn't it? Bella's going to tuck her in."
