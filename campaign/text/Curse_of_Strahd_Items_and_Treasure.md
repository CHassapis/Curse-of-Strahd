## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT
The deck has 67 item cards. The party should end the campaign attuned to about 15 items and carrying perhaps 20 more — most of those consumables, story items or things to sell. This document says, for every card:
- Where it is.
- How they get it: found, given, taken from an enemy, bought, or held back until you release it.
- Who uses it: the character it suits.
- What it sells for: what Oksana pays for a spare.
Every card is one of four kinds:

| MARK | MEANING | CARDS |
|---|---|---|
| GIVE | Found or handed over in play — if they go there and do the thing. | 41 |
| ENEMY | Carried or used by a villain. They only get it by winning, and looting. | 14 |
| SHOP | In Oksana's shop in Vallaki. They see it, and have to earn the gold. | 8 |
| HOLD | Kept back — give at most one or two, late. | 4 |

Why 41 GIVE cards is less than it looks:
- 6 are consumables, junk or curse cards: the Potion of Dreaming, the Scroll, the Basilisk Oil, the Glamerweave, and the Cloak of the Other Self's two cards.
- 4 are Tarokka items: story objects the party needs, not loot.
- 4 are either-or: the Baron or Lady Wachter rewards them, never both.
- 9 suit Fedra, and she can only attune to three. The rest get sold to pay for the others' gear.
The real limit is attunement: three per character. With this many cards, most of what they find is something to sell, trade or leave behind. That is the point.
Selling: Oksana buys any magic item at half its shop price — the SELLS FOR column. She won't touch anything very rare or rarer ("too dangerous to keep in the shop"), and nobody sells a Tarokka item.

### Held back — release two at level 11–12
These are the power spikes. With the party reaching level 12, you can release two of them for the final assault — at the Amber Temple or in the castle. The very rare items are on curve at level 11+; the two legendary ones (Efreeti Chain, Blood of Lathander) are still above it, so give one at most.

| CARD | ITEM | WHY IT'S HELD |
|---|---|---|
| 70 | Efreeti Chain | AC 19 and Immunity to Fire make every other armour pointless. |
| 63 | Belt of Hill Giant Strength | Strength 21 fixes Ireena's one weakness at a stroke. |
| 71 | The Blood of Lathander | Its sunlight can end Strahd in a minute. Without Dawnbreak it's safe. |
| 64 | Staff of Healing | Takes all pressure off Fedra's spell slots. |
